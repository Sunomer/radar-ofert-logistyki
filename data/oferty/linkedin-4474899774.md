About the role
We are looking for a Junior Connected Planning Analyst to join our Supply Chain team and support planning activities across a dynamic product portfolio.
In this role, you will work closely with Supply Planning, Demand Planning, Manufacturing, Procurement, and Logistics teams to help ensure product availability and support day-to-day planning operations. You will analyse data, monitor inventory and supply performance, and contribute to planning activities related to product launches and portfolio changes.
This is an excellent opportunity for someone at the beginning of their Supply Chain career who wants to develop their analytical skills, gain exposure to end-to-end planning processes, and learn how supply chain decisions impact business performance in a global organisation.
Your responsibilities
Support supply planning processes for assigned products and markets
Monitor inventory levels and product availability
Assist in identifying and resolving supply issues and exceptions
Support planning activities related to product launches and portfolio changes
Analyze planning data and prepare reports for stakeholders
Help identify risks and potential supply gaps
Work closely with Demand Planning, Manufacturing, Procurement, and Logistics teams
Maintain planning data and support planning system updates
Participate in continuous improvement initiatives within Supply Chain
Contribute to scenario analysis and planning recommendations under guidance from senior team members
The experience we're looking for
Must have:
1-2 years of experience in Supply Chain, Supply Planning, Production Planning, Demand Planning, or a related field
Strong analytical skills and confidence working with data
Advanced Excel skills
Experience working with ERP or planning systems
Good organizational skills and attention to detail
Ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities
Strong communication and stakeholder management skills
Fluent English
Nice to Have:
Experience in FMCG, manufacturing, or a fast-moving environment
Exposure to planning systems such as Kinaxis, SAP APO, SAP IBP, OMP, or Blue Yonder
Exposure to inventory planning or supply planning processes
Experience supporting product launches or portfolio changes
Experience gained through internships, graduate programs, or junior planning roles
