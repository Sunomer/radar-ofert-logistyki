Job Overview
WHO WE ARE
Relativity is a leading legal data intelligence company building technology that helps users organize data, discover the truth, and act on it with confidence. Our AI-powered, cloud platform, RelativityOne, transforms massive volumes of complex information into actionable insights for litigation, investigations, regulatory inquiries, data breach responses, and other high-stakes legal work where accuracy and trust are crucial.
The world’s largest law firms, corporations, and government agencies rely on Relativity’s legal AI software to securely surface and manage the most relevant and impactful information in their matters. Beyond our commercial impact, we are committed to expanding access to technology and supporting pro bono legal work.
What We Do
The Product Management team is looking for a Product Operations Analyst with strong communication, analytical, and collaboration skills, and a strong desire to help enable a high performing product management team. You will be responsible for supporting and optimizing the enabling tools and processes that help our product team collect and organize product feedback, prioritize and plan what to build next, and ensure successful feature releases. You will also help support and improve the department-level operating rhythms including recurring product ceremonies, reporting, and collaboration with other departments.
About The Role
This role also carries a growing emphasis on AI fluency, systems thinking, and automation. As our product organization evolves how it works, you will help identify high-friction, manual work and apply AI tools and workflow automation to make our core processes more consistent, scalable, and efficient. Over time, there is significant opportunity for this role to take on deeper ownership of AI and automation initiatives across the product organization.
A successful candidate must be energized by building great products, collaborating with a diverse range of partners, optimizing team processes, deeply exploring data, and experimenting with new tools and technology to improve how teams work.
Job Description And Requirements
WHAT YOU’LL DO
Support and optimize our feature launch process and collaborate with internal partners to help ensure successful releases
Administer and provide expert guidance for our tooling for product feedback and roadmap management
Support product team operating cadence including request routing, coordinating high value content opportunities for department meetings, and maintaining high-quality internal process guidance and product roadmap presentation assets
Identify opportunities to enable and empower product managers, including simplifying workflows and reducing operational overhead
Identify high-friction, manual, or repetitive work across Product Ops processes such as planning, reporting, roadmap coordination, and internal communications, and prioritize opportunities to improve or automate it
Design and implement practical automations using AI tools, workflow platforms, and system integrations, and improve process consistency and scalability through templates, SOPs, and reusable patterns
Support the product organization’s adoption of AI-enabled ways of working across discovery, requirements, and delivery
Measure the impact of process improvements and automations, and iterate based on adoption and outcomes
Required
