Join Global Graduate Programme in BAT!
It's one of a kind
18-month managerial programme
that's geared specifically for high-potential individuals.
Goal of it?
To lay the foundation for your successful and internationally oriented leadership career in BAT.
As a Global Management Trainee, you’ll have the opportunity to rotate through various Supply Chain functions to fully understand how an end-to-end supply chain operates. All of it in a diverse and multinational environment with accountability from day one through dedicated projects.
What’s waiting for you within 18 months?
Onboarding - tailored induction trainings and field visits with our sales teams to understand how the business really works, meeting your dedicated mentor and buddy who’ll support you along the way in BAT.
Rotations in possible areas
Supply Chain Excellence - lead cross-functional project in logistics focused on process improvement, technical solutions, digitalization and operational excellence while collaborating with stakeholders across functions.
Manufacturing - gain end-to-end exposure to manufacturing, logistics and distribution operations within BAT. Develop first-hand experience in production processes, continuous improvement initiatives, quality standards, warehousing and transportation activities.
Supply Chain Planning - learn how demand and supply planning processes ensure product availability, optimize inventory levels and support business performance.
Grad Academy - a two‑week international experience in the UK, where graduates from around the world come together to train leadership skills and networking.
What’s next?
After successful completion of the programme you’ll become
Junior Manager in the Supply Chain area
. Based on your performance, you may also receive an opportunity to go for an international rotation.
