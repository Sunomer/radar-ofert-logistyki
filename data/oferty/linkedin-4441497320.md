Job Description
About the role
At Bluecrux, we support global clients in transforming their end to end supply chain planning through advanced technology and deep expertise. As an OMP Configuration consultant, you will work hands-on on OMP implementations, with a strong focus on technical configuration and customization.
You contribute to real planning transformations by translating complex supply chain challenges into robust OMP solutions. You join a team of global specialists, working closely with experienced colleagues across projects and locations.
In This Role, You Will
Translate real world supply chain planning problems into structured models, logic, and system customization and configuration.
Work on solver validation and tuning in OMP for optimal planning results.
Support testing of new developed features and ensure clear documentation of what has been built.
Work closely with functional consultants and international client teams to understand planning requirements and provide adequate solutions.
Collaborate with internal OMP specialists and the OMP vendor for knowledge sharing and technical support.
Support and co-lead workshops, handover sessions and working sessions with client teams.
Work within a project development team, using different OMP environments and planning layers to build basic and advanced functionalities in customer/project repositories, using Git, Azure DevOps and Visual Studio Code.
Job Requirements
About you
You bring minimum 2 years of hands-on experience in OMP with a focus on implementation and configuration work.
You have a Master’s degree in Supply Chain, Engineering, Business, or a related field.
You have hands-on experience with OMP configuration, including OPR.
You have hands-on OPAL coding experience.
A solid background in supply chain planning, with strong knowledge of supply planning processes.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills, with the ability to support workshops, coach client users, and manage stakeholder expectations.
Other programming experience outside OMP is a plus
Fluent in English.
You feel connected with and are ready to represent our company values: dig deep, own it, come together, move fast and be kind.
You show a willingness to travel globally and to work in a hybrid working model.

Grow your career in a flexible way, deepening your expertise or expanding your scope based on your interests.
About us

We collaborate closely with industry leaders like Johnson & Johnson, GSK, Bridgestone, AkzoNobel, Beiersdorf, Oatly, and many more – creating partnerships that drive meaningful impact. We do not work for them but rather with them, and we stand by their side the entire time. That is the only way to make changes stick and flourish.
