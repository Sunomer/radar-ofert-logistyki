The Impact You'll Make
As a Production Engineer at Danfoss Saginomiya in Natolin, you will play a key role in ensuring stable, efficient, and high-quality production processes.
Your impact? Thanks to your work, production processes will become more reliable, optimized, and aligned with high quality standards. You will support product development and process improvements that directly impact daily operations and customer satisfaction.
You will collaborate closely with Production, Quality, and Engineering teams, supporting process optimization, technical problem-solving, and implementation of changes in the manufacturing environment.
What You’ll Be Doing
Ensure quality and optimization of pressure switch production processes
Create and update work instructions and process documentation
Analyze production KPIs and identify improvement opportunities
Lead implementation, quality, and continuous improvement projects
Cooperate with Danfoss and Saginomiya teams on product and process changes
What We're Looking For
Bachelor’s degree in Production Engineering, Mechatronics, Mechanical Engineering, or a related field
Minimum 2 years of experience in a manufacturing environment within production, quality, or process engineering roles
Knowledge of quality tools and continuous improvement / Lean Manufacturing principles
Professional proficiency in English and very good Polish language skills
Analytical mindset, problem-solving skills, and ability to work collaboratively across teams
What You'll Get From Us
Opportunity to grow within a global engineering and technology company
Participation in improvement and implementation projects with real business impact
Supportive team environment and opportunities to develop technical and quality competencies
Collaborative and knowledge-sharing work culture with hybrid work possibilities
Benefits package including private medical care, life insurance, pension program (PPE) and sports card co-financing
