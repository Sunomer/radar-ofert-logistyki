We are looking for an experienced Business Analyst to join a long-term project delivered by Sii in cooperation with a global organization operating in the Life Sciences industry. In this role, you will support manufacturing system integration initiatives, helping bridge business and technology teams across complex, regulated environments. You will work remotely with international stakeholders and contribute to process design, requirements management, and enterprise integration efforts. Apply now and let’s discuss how your experience can contribute to this exciting project!
Your tasks
Gathering, analyzing, and documenting functional and non-functional requirements for manufacturing and integration solutions
Collaborating with business stakeholders, architects, and technical teams to design and optimize end-to-end business processes
Supporting integration initiatives across manufacturing and enterprise platforms, ensuring alignment with business objectives
Facilitating workshops, discussions, and decision-making sessions while maintaining a productive and outcome-focused approach
Contributing to process mapping, data interoperability analysis, reporting requirements, and solution validation activities
Requirements
5–10 years of experience as a Business Analyst, Functional Architect, or Consultant in Life Sciences, Pharmaceuticals, or a similar regulated environment
Hands-on experience with manufacturing-related systems such as MES, LIMS, ERP, PLM, ELN, or comparable platforms, including system integration projects
Strong understanding of manufacturing processes for drug substances and/or drug products, business process mapping, and enterprise integration concepts
Solid knowledge of SQL, relational databases, data structures, and reporting systems; familiarity with ISA-95/ISA-88 and SAFe Agile is an advantage
Fluency in English (C1 level, both spoken and written) and availability to collaborate with global teams during overlapping EU and US working hours
