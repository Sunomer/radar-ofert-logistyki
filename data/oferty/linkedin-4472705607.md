The Impact You'll Make
As an
MT Engineer
, you’ll be one of the people behind a production environment that is safer, smarter and more efficient. You’ll work directly with machines, processes and production teams to solve technical challenges, improve performance and turn ideas into practical solutions.
Your impact?
You’ll help make sure our equipment performs at its best, processes run efficiently and production can continuously improve. From optimizing machines and improving OEE to leading technical projects and introducing new equipment, your work will have a direct impact on safety, quality, productivity and cost.
You’ll combine engineering expertise with a hands-on approach — seeing a problem on the production floor, finding out what is really causing it and working with others to make it better.
What You’ll Be Doing
In this role, you will:
Lead technical and production improvement projects — from process optimization and change control to productivity and OEE initiatives
Optimize machines and production processes, identifying opportunities to improve reliability, quality, efficiency and performance
Plan and deliver CAPEX projects, taking new equipment and technical solutions from initial concept through implementation and handover to production
Use Lean, Kaizen and other continuous improvement methods to improve safety, quality, productivity and cost performance
Provide hands-on technical support to production, troubleshoot challenges and work closely with suppliers and internal teams to implement effective solutions
What We're Looking For
Degree in Engineering — Mechanical, Manufacturing, Industrial or a related field
Experience working in a production or manufacturing environment, ideally in a technical or engineering role
Knowledge of production processes, machine optimization and structured problem-solving
Familiarity with quality and process tools such as PFMEA, Control Plan, MSA and CPK
A continuous improvement mindset, strong communication skills and good command of Polish and English; experience with Lean, Kaizen or Six Sigma is an advantage

Exposure to modern technologies, production processes, and cross-functional collaboration
