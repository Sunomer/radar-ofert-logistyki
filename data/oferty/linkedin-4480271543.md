British American Tobacco POLAND has an exciting opportunity for a SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGER
in WARSAW
The Supply Chain Manager is responsible for developing and managing the end-to-end secondary supply chain strategy for Poland, ensuring the efficient flow of finished goods from warehouses to customers across all product categories.
The role delivers the required customer service levels while optimising inventory, logistics costs and working capital. It leads local warehousing, transportation and third-party logistics operations, drives continuous improvement, and ensures that all secondary supply chain activities comply with BAT governance, quality, security and EHS requirements.
Your Key Responsibilities Will Include
Develop and implement the Secondary Supply Chain strategy for Poland, aligned with commercial and operational priorities.
Ensure efficient warehousing, transportation and distribution across all product categories.
Optimise inventory, product availability, service levels, logistics costs and working capital.
Manage third-party logistics providers, contracts, SLAs, KPIs and improvement plans.
Own the Secondary Supply Chain budget, forecasting and delivery of productivity savings.
Lead continuous improvement, network optimisation and transformation projects.
Ensure compliance with BAT governance, internal controls, EHS, security, quality and local regulations.
Lead the Secondary Supply Chain team and collaborate cross-functionally to ensure operational continuity and strong business performance.
What are we looking for?
Degree in Supply Chain, Logistics, Business or a related field.
Relevant experience in supply chain or logistics, preferably in an international FMCG environment.
Knowledge of warehousing, transportation, inventory and 3PL management.
Strong analytical, commercial and stakeholder-management skills.
Experience in budget management, continuous improvement and project delivery.
Strong people leadership and ability to work in a matrix organisation.
Fluent Polish and English; good knowledge of SAP and Microsoft Excel.
