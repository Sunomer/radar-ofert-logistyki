Join our Automotive team and shape what’s next for leading OEMs and mobility players across Europe. You’ll be part of a multilingual, European network that collaborates across disciplines to deliver end‑to‑end change for our clients.
ABOUT TEAM AND WORK:
We work at the intersection of automotive, technology, and business.
We support leading automotive companies in digital transformation, customer experience, and process optimization, focusing on how technology changes the way businesses operate and interact with customers.
What makes this role unique in Poland is the opportunity to actively co-create automotive strategies, projects, and products implemented by top global OEMs.
THE WORK:
Collaborate with cross-functional, international teams across different business areas.
Contribute to international projects by supporting the design, implementation, and improvement of business and digital initiatives (with guidance from experienced team members).
Assist in analyzing and improving business processes across key areas (e.g. sales, customer experience (CX), and aftersales), identifying opportunities to enhance performance and efficiency.
Research automotive industry trends, emerging technologies, and best practices across sales, marketing, and customer service to support client projects.
Support transformation initiatives related to digital platforms, customer journeys, and operational processes in the automotive sector.
Participate in project work using both agile and classic methodologies, building your consulting and project skills.
Continuously develop your knowledge in areas such as automotive trends, digital solutions, AI, and automation.
What's in it for You?
Early career role in an international, professional environment.
Opportunity to gain hands-on experience on real automotive projects.
Exposure to a variety of projects and topics, giving you the opportunity to quickly build a broad understanding of the business and develop diverse skills.
Access to structured learning, certifications, and training programs.
A supportive team environment where you are not only guided in project work, but also in shaping your individual career path with the possibility to actively define and develop it based on your interests.
We work mostly remotely, but some projects require travel and on-site work across Europe.
Your winning qualities
Very good German (min. B2/C1) and good English.
Interest in automotive and/or new technologies.
Analytical thinking and willingness to learn.
Openness to work in an international environment.
Team player: you collaborate across cultures and disciplines.
