The Opportunity
To facilitate and support the development and the delivery of the site’s vision and strategy.
To lead the agenda for Lean deployment and managing priorities aligned to the Vision and Strategy whilst also have strong governance.
To create leadership capability through effective training and coaching in all aspects of Lean deployment working towards increasing sites Lean maturity level.
Responsibilities
A key member of the site management team coaching the leadership team & site basic coaches to achieve Lean maturity progression.
This will involve:
Deeply understanding the Lean Framework and developing others capability
Identifying, recruiting, training and coaching basic coaches
Using PBAS to evaluating and reporting on the behaviours
Coaching in the weekly and period meetings to improve the standards
Coaching Site Leadership Team in their high impact moments
To support all Lean specialists (PSS, SWS and BCs) to build their capability towards certification
Facilitate the site leadership team to review and refresh the site strategy on an annual, quarterly basis
Facilitate and support the site to lead a Deployment, Planning and Evaluation (DPE) event annually
Support and coach the development of each leader in the use of Leadership standard work for daily, weekly, monthly and yearly
Facilitate the development and cascade of the site annual activities and communication plan
Develop and implement site communications strategy
Support and coach the leadership team in the use of prioritization tools including Benefit and Effort and the problem solving tools (incl 5 Whys and various A3’s) in the appropriate QCDM forums
Working closely with the site’s lean six sigma resource to support the site in productivity ideation through the site standard meeting forums
Deliver improvement projects through QCDM process utilizing LSS/DMAIC approach achieving Green belt savings per year on year

Excellent communication skills across all levels of the organization, from frontline employees to senior leadership
