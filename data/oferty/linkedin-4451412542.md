Senior Solution Architect acts as a member of on-site/remote project teams. Uses in-depth knowledge of customer business and processes to design and implement Blue Yonder solutions on medium to large supply chain transformation programs. Understands how the Blue Yonder solutions can support customer business.
Job Description
As a Solution Architect you will be:
Leading implementation of Blue Yonder products, including ownership of end-to-end solution design
Working with customer business and IT teams during all project phases
Providing leadership to functional team during implementation cycle
Serving as a functional and industry expert and participating in key meetings between Project Manager, key customer contacts, and customer users
Working closely with Product Management Department regarding specific trends for future product functionality/enhancements
Providing mentorship to junior associates within Blue Yonder organization
Working with the pre-sales team to assess workload on a given project and recommend roles
