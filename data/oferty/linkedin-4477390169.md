Title And Summary
Senior Product Specialist, MDES for Issuers and Wallets (M4D)
The European Digital Payments team mission is to bring digital-first, differentiated, inclusive solutions to make consumers' everyday life easier. With our tokenization platform (MDES), digital product suite and together with our partners across the payment’s ecosystem, we power the future of payments, beyond the plastic card. We are now seeking a product enablement senior specialist responsible for supporting the rollout of tokenized digital wallets and wearables.
Key Responsibilities Will Include
Monitor and track progress against our Issuer KPIs on digital wallets/wearables, with the main objective to reach 100% issuer enablement to all participating digital wallets via MDES Express
Support roll-out of new digital wallet/wearable wallets or key issuer launches of wallets
Drive product marketing efforts, such as ensuring marketing toolkits and sales materials always being up to date
Raise product enhancements / issues towards global product owners
Leverage working knowledge of Mastercard business, systems, products, and customer requirements in making fact-based business and product decisions
Lead by example with hands-on approaches to demonstrate product management excellence and share best practices
All About You

Very good knowledge of MS Office suite (Excel and PowerPoint primarily)
