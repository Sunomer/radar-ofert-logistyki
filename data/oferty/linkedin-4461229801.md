Ashland has an exciting opportunity for Logistics Specialist – Export Processor to join our Ashland Specialty Ingredients Poland Sp. z o.o. business at our Warsaw (Poland) office. This is a very visible, significant role within the Company and the Logistics function. This position will report to Logistics Supervisor.
The responsibilities of the position include, but are not limited to, the following:
Monitor and create deliveries, perform Goods Issue and Receipt, block damaged goods, unblock when needed
Prepare documents required for shipping
Monitor stock to make allocations on daily basis, inform CS about incomplete or blocked orders
Follow up on expected inbound shipments, especially urgently needed for outbound orders
Arrange ‘express shipments’
Manage return orders.
Update deliveries / shipments to acknowledge confirmation of shipping
Enter freight accruals
Help drive improvement projects within the Supply Chain
Support month end closures
Cooperate with internal and external Teams
Cooperate with TMS processors to execute bookings
Actively contribute to the organizational goals by taking initiative, executing on goals and focusing on performance and safety
Improve the organization capacity and capabilities through building collaborative relationships, optimizing diverse talent and utilizing positive communications and influence with others
