Are you looking for a career move that will put you at the heart of a global financial institution? Then bring your skills in analysis, problem solving, and communication to the Senior Business Analyst role within the Trustee Change Team.
By joining Citi, you will become part of a global organisation whose mission is to serve as a trusted partner to our clients by responsibly providing financial services that enable growth and economic progress.
Responsibilities:
Preparation and analysis of proposed system changes, gathering documentation and consensus between business requirements and technology deliverables to assure smooth projects implementation
Lead and coordinate gathering business change requests, system developments, and new business on boarding across Trustee Operations
Identify and resolve project issues across multiple business areas and proactively mitigate implementation risks
Formulate analysis of effort, cost and impact on various Citi businesses to ensure timely execution and mitigate potential risks
Cooperate with internal and external stakeholders to effectively deliver projects
Challenge the status quo, display initiative and innovation
Ensure that all necessary project documentation is maintained and all necessary client and internal approvals for the project are obtained
Ensure implemented process is compliant with local and global regulations.
Key skills and experience:
Extensive relevant experience in business analysis, writing business requirements and process mapping
Experience in UAT (User Acceptance Testing) and writing test scenarios
Excellent knowledge of English (spoken and written) and strong communication skills
Ability to work in a fast-paced environment under tight deadlines
Ability to work independently and as part of a team
Strong multitasking and organizational skills
Ability to build relationships with various project stakeholders
Experience in supporting operations teams with production issues/system queries
Familiarity with AI-enabled productivity tools and the ability to leverage them
