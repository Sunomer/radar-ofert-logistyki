FREIGHT FORWARDER - LAND TRANSPORT SPECIALIST
JOB LOCATION: TYCHY
Role Overview
We are looking for a dedicated Land Freight Forwarding Specialist who will be responsible for the endto-end organization and coordination of domestic and international road transport operations,
ensuring the timely execution of shipments, efficient information flow, and the highest level of customer service.
Key Responsibilities:
Organize and coordinate domestic and international road transportation operations.
Plan transport routes and ensure the timely execution of transport orders.
Monitor transport activities and maintain ongoing communication with drivers, carriers, and customers.
Coordinate cargo loading and delivery schedules.
Prepare, verify, and manage transport documentation.
Ensure a high standard of customer service and build long-term business relationships.
Resolve day-to-day operational issues related to transport execution.
Handle invoicing and settlement of transport orders.
What You Will Bring To The Role:
Experience in organizing international transportation, particularly Less-Than-Truckload (LTL) shipments.
Very good command of English, with the ability to effectively support and communicate with international customers.
Good knowledge of the Microsoft Office suite, particularly Excel.
Strong ability to organize own work and make decisions under time pressure.
Excellent communication skills and the ability to build and maintain strong relationships with customers and carriers.
High level of commitment, motivation, and willingness to develop within the transport, forwarding, and logistics (TSL) industry.
What We Offer:
Opportunity to join one of the world's leading global logistics organizations with a strong international presence.
Work in a dynamic environment that supports professional growth and the development of new skills and competencies.
Exposure to international customers and global-scale logistics processes.
Opportunity to collaborate with experienced professionals in logistics, transportation, and customs services.
Access to training programs and international best practices within a global organization.
Friendly and supportive organizational culture based on collaboration, diversity, and mutual respect.
Attractive employee benefits package and competitive employment conditions.
Genuine career development opportunities within an international organization.
