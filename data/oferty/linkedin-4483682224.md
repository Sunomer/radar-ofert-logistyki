Overview
As the Rail Operations Manager, you will lead and manage the daily freight operations in Poland, ensuring shipments are handled reliably, compliantly, and cost-effectively. You will also develop the operations team, improve service quality and operational performance, and implement best practices aligned with AIT standards and business objectives.
Responsibilities
Lead daily rail freight operations, ensuring shipments are executed safely, accurately, on time, and in line with customer requirements.
Lead, develop, and motivate the rail operations team, including training, performance management, resource planning, and recruitment support.
Oversee shipments from booking through delivery, proactively managing disruptions, escalations, exceptions, documentation, and billing accuracy.
Coordinate with customers, rail carriers, terminals, customs brokers, international partners, and internal teams to ensure seamless end-to-end execution.
Ensure compliance with customs and trade requirements, safety standards, company policies, and customer SOPs, while maintaining strong operational controls.
Drive operational performance and continuous improvement by monitoring KPIs, improving processes, controlling costs, supporting quotations and forecasts, and developing rail freight services in line with business objectives.
Qualifications
Fluency in Polish and very good written and spoken English skills
Proven experience in rail/ air/ ocean freight logistics
Strong knowledge of rail/ air/ ocean freight operations, international trade, and customs procedures
Good understanding of safety and compliance requirements relevant to freight operations
Strong organizational and multitasking abilities in a fast-paced operational environment
Excellent written and verbal communication and interpersonal skills
Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to make sound decisions under pressure
A bachelor’s degree in logistics, supply chain management, or a related field is preferred; equivalent relevant experience will also be considered.
