Supplier Performance Engineer manages and oversees relationships with suppliers to ensure the delivery of high-quality metal work components and contribute to solutions to the complex aspects of a project or process. You will work with suppliers to establish and maintain relationships, monitor performance, ensure delivery certainty and identify areas for improvement. You will be part of AP1000 program in Europe with a specific focus on Poland.
Responsibilities
Supplier Evaluation and Development involves conducting overall supplier assessments. These assessments evaluate suppliers' capabilities for complex technical products and processes. Additionally, they ensure that a supplier's manufacturing facility has all the necessary resources, including machines, spare parts, material, components, and supplies, to operate. These goods must comply with Westinghouse's quality and standards requirements.
Lead supplier development and qualification activities for new products and cost reduction efforts with a subject matter expert approach. This includes identification of project risks and mitigating actions, managing project schedules, approval of supplier process documents, process capability analyses, measurement system analyses, input to design for manufacturability
Supplier Oversight Evaluating procurement risk, soliciting partner input, and using resources to develop and manage Oversight Plans and estimates
Define Witness and Hold points during the manufacturing
Complete or delegate oversight of main supplier operations and activities, documenting results and taking appropriate action
Support the project management team to work with Supplier Quality Organization for performing Quality surveillance
Supplier Management Managing flow down of Quality and Technical requirements to suppliers, e.g., NQA-1, ISO, Design Specifications, Approved Processes
Guide the supplier performance, including supplier performance scorecard/dashboard. Ensure maximum value is received for the Purchase Order that WEC places to its suppliers, including full compliance to the quality and technical specifications.
Responsibility to develop Performance Improvement Plans for poor or negative trends in performance and managing those plans to completion. This includes overseeing the Supplier Corrective Action Request (SCAR) process: performing cause analyses and verifying completion of preventative and corrective actions
Lead and escalate “quality events”, coordinating activities, issuing communications, identifying partners to ensure prompt identification of impacted products, disposition, and corrective action. This requires engaging your technical experience and capabilities for providing Manufacturing technical expertise to the supplier when needed
Coordinate supplier documentation reviews by appropriate Westinghouse functions, including the Supplier Non-Conformance Reports
Provide regular progress report to the department (Project Manager, Program Director)
Support Commercial Operations and Legal for Quality/technical bases of supplier claims
Qualifications
Bachelor's Degree or higher in Mechanical/Material Engineering
Fluent in Polish and English
Working knowledge of Manufacturing and Quality Plans, PFMEAs
3-5 years of experience on similar role
Experience leading multiple projects
Experience with nuclear products and standards (ASME)
Experience with NDE/NDT processes
Personal, industrial and nuclear safety as an important area of concern
Knowledge about Design-To-Manufacturing and Manufacturing Readiness Level process
Experience performing process capability analysis and qualification
Willing to travel to the supplier Workshop based in Europe. The expectation is up to 100% of your time on business trip to allow to be on the workshops in Kielce or Siedlce.
Experience in large projects execution
Experience with US standards and norms (ASME, AWS)
