The Company
A leading international logistics provider is looking to strengthen its IT organisation within its Eastern European operations. With a strong focus on transport, warehousing, freight forwarding and supply chain excellence, the company continuously invests in digitalisation and operational improvement across its European network.
To support these ambitions, they are seeking a
TMS Application Specialist
based in Poland.
The Role
As a TMS Application Specialist, you will act as the bridge between
transport operations and IT
, ensuring that Transport Management Systems (TMS) effectively support business processes and operational requirements.
You will be responsible for configuring, maintaining and optimising TMS applications, supporting users, and contributing to both local and regional initiatives. Your activities will range from application support and troubleshooting to system enhancements, testing, integrations and implementation projects.
Working within an international environment, you will collaborate closely with operational teams, IT colleagues, external vendors and project stakeholders across Europe.
Key Responsibilities
Configure and support Transport Management Systems based on operational requirements.
Translate business needs into effective system solutions.
Troubleshoot incidents and provide application support to end users.
Test new functionalities, configuration changes and software upgrades.
Support User Acceptance Testing (UAT) activities.
Collaborate with software vendors and development teams on enhancements and improvements.
Participate in TMS implementation, migration and optimisation projects.
Create reports and analyse operational data using tools such as SQL, Crystal Reports or Power BI.
Support integrations through EDI, APIs and related technologies.
Maintain functional and technical documentation.
Identify opportunities to improve and digitise transport operations.
Your Profile
Approximately
2-5 years of experience
in TMS, logistics applications, application support or a related role.
Experience working with Transport Management Systems such as
Blue Yonder, Oracle Transportation Management (OTM), Transporeon, Manhattan TMS, CargoWise, Soloplan CarLo, e2Open
or similar platforms.
Understanding of transport, logistics, freight forwarding or supply chain operations.
Experience with system configuration and application troubleshooting.
Knowledge of SQL and databases is considered an advantage.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Customer-focused mindset with excellent stakeholder management skills.
Ability to work independently while collaborating within international teams.
Good command of English.
Practical experience and technical expertise are considered more important than formal educational background.
