Responsibilities
Are you looking for a role that combines regulation, risk management, governance, strategic transformation, and international exposure?
Join the Global Banking & Advisory (GLBA) Business Unit of Societe Generale and become a key contributor to the regulatory and governance framework supporting one of the world's leading wholesale banking franchises.
As part of the Business Transformation Office, you will work across a broad range of strategic and regulatory topics, partnering with business, compliance, legal, tax, and risk teams to support key initiatives and governance frameworks.
The role provides exceptional visibility across the organization, regular interaction with senior stakeholders, and exposure to some of the most significant regulatory and transformation challenges facing a global wholesale bank. It is particularly suited to intellectually curious professionals who enjoy tackling complex topics and turning them into practical solutions.
Your future scope of responsibility will cover:
Partnering with Front Office teams on regulatory, governance, risk, and control-related matters
Advising on complex transactions and new business activities
Translating regulatory requirements into practical and scalable operating solutions
Leading transformation and process improvement initiatives
Strengthening governance, risk, and control frameworks through ongoing review and enhancement
Leveraging AI and digital tools to improve efficiency and productivity
Monitoring key risks and coordinating mitigation and escalation actions
Building regulatory and risk awareness through training and knowledge-sharing activities
Supporting governance committees and management forums
Managing relationships with auditors, regulators, and inspectors and coordinating follow-up actions

You build strong relationships and work effectively with stakeholders at all levels
You are proactive, solution-oriented, and comfortable working independently
