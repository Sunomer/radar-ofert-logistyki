Job Description
Are You Ready to Make It Happen at Mondelēz International?
Join our Mission to Lead the Future of Snacking. Make It Matter.
As an organization we are committed to developing the next generation of Makers and Bakers. Joining us at an early career stage in our fast paced and ever changing environment will enable you to do more, learn more and grow more. We will ensure you are given the support you need to be at your best and enable you to be yourself and bring passion and personality. Here you can lean in and speak up and bring your own flavor.
More About This Role
Are you passionate about IT, artificial intelligence, sourcing, negotiations and global business? Do you enjoy working with data, solving problems, and collaborating with different teams? Join Mondelez International as an IT Procurement Intern and gain hands-on experience in a dynamic FMCG environment while supporting our global digital sourcing and strategy team. After successful internship completion, a full-time position might be offered.
Your responsibilities
:
Help in global IT contract management
Prepare data driven insights about supplier performance
Support the team with negotiations preparation
Assist in designing sourcing strategy for BPO and software
Participate in reporting and KPI tracking activities
Collaborate with Mondelez Digital Services (MDS), Finance and Legal departments
Assist with process improvement initiatives leveraging AI tools
Use data and analytical tools to identify opportunities and support decision-making
What we’re looking for:
Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Management, Information Technology, Economics, Supply Chain Management, or a related field
Good knowledge of Microsoft Excel and Microsoft Office
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Good numerical and logical reasoning abilities
Strong communication and interpersonal skills
Eagerness to learn and develop in a fast-paced environment
Strong level of English
Basic knowledge of Procurement, IT, LLMs and Artificial Intelligence
Awareness of source-to-pay, order-to-cash and record to report processes would be a plus but not mandatory
What we offer:
6 months paid internship (full-time and part-time options available)
Hands-on experience in a global FMCG company
Exposure to global IT sourcing landscape and strategy
Opportunity to work with cross-functional teams
Development of analytical, business, and communication skills
A supportive environment focused on learning and growth
Please apply with your CV or Resume in English
The responsibilities of this position are performed within the framework of a regional business model that is defined and managed by Mondelēz Europe GmbH, Switzerland .
No Relocation support available
