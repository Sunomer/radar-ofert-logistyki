About The Role
With this role you have opportunity to lead regional business planning capability and drive adoption of planning systems, processes, and ways of working across the region. The role connects business planning, digital capability, change management, and operational execution to improve forecast quality, planning effectiveness, and user adoption of enterprise planning platforms.
Your responsibilities
Lead regional deployment and continuous improvement of business planning processes across markets and functions.
Drive adoption, utilization, and governance of planning systems and digital tools.
Partner with Supply, Commercial, Finance, Demand Planning, and Market teams to align planning capabilities with business priorities.
Monitor planning performance KPIs and system adoption metrics; identify gaps and improvement opportunities.
Coordinate system enhancements, testing, training, and user support activities with global capability and technology teams.
Act as the regional change lead for planning transformation initiatives and new capability rollouts.
Ensure planning standards, master data governance, and process compliance are maintained across the region.
Facilitate business readiness activities including stakeholder engagement, communication, training, and hypercare support.
Translate business requirements into scalable process and system solutions.
Support scenario planning, business reviews, and decision-making through data-driven insights.
The experience we're looking for
Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain, Business, Engineering, Analytics, or related field.
Significant experience in business planning, IBP, supply chain planning, or capability deployment.
Experience with enterprise planning platforms such as SAP IBP, APO, Kinaxis, o9, or similar systems.
Proven experience leading regional or cross-functional transformation initiatives.
Strong analytical, communication, and influencing skills.
Experience working in global or matrix organizations preferred.
The skills for success
Supply Chain Management, Business Partnership, Collaboration and partnership building, Relationship Management, Business accumen, Productivity management, Improve business processes, Advanced Analytics, Data Analytics, Supply Chain Planning, Logistics Management.
