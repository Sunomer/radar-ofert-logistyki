## Treść ogłoszenia

Job Description
Are You Ready to Make It Happen at Mondelēz International?
 Join our Mission to Lead the Future of Snacking. Make It Possible.
How you will contribute  
You will:
- Own and drive the demand management cycle of the integrated business planning (IBP) process – manage end-to-end forecast compilation process, including full ownership of the short-term weekly plan and the long-term forecast, ensuring accuracy and alignment across stakeholders 
- Meet your KPIs including (but not limited to) meeting or exceeding customer fill rate and delivering finished goods inventory at target levels; proactively identify risks and propose corrective actions 
- Develop strong cross-functional partnerships with Supply Hub Planners, Order to Cash and Commercial Teams, acting as a key point of contact for demand & fulfillment topics 
- Highlight production planning issues / bottlenecks / propose-initiate immediate action – focus on root cause analysis and support for end-to-end process 
- Proactively manage Ageing Stock and waste risk through initiating and coordinating appropriate stock balancing activities across the network 
- Act as the main Point of Contact for other team members in terms of problems or escalations as well coordinate their daily work and assign tasks 
- Take an active role in product development projects 
- Conduct ad hoc analyses and deep-dives to support business decisions and address non-standard situations as they arise 
- Create, update and continuously improve internal tools and processes for simplification and optimization of daily operations 
 What You Will Bring
A desire to drive your future and accelerate your career and the following experience and knowledge:
- Proven experience in and solid understanding of Supply Chain (Demand Planning, Replenishment, Co-packing, Order to Cash, Logistics) or Supply Planning – ability to independently manage end-to-end processes 
- Experience in a fast-moving consumer goods or consumer packaged goods environment, dynamic industry where shelf life is a constraint 
- Experience within an IBP/S&OP environment 
- Fluency in English 
- Strong analytical skills with the ability to conduct ad hoc analyses, interpret data, and translate findings into actionable recommendations 
- Problem solving skills, drive for results, and ability to handle non-standard situations with confidence 
- Ability to delegate tasks and distribute work among others 
- Out-of-the-box thinking and proactive attitude 
- Ability to work independently and take ownership of assigned processes with minimal supervision 
- Excellent communication style and ability to work under time pressure 
- Experience in working with multinational teams would be an asset
