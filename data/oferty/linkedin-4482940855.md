Clinical Supply IRT Specialist
Location
Remote / Hybrid / Europe
Job Overview
IQVIA is seeking a
Clinical Supply IRT Specialist
to support the successful delivery of clinical trials through expertise in
Interactive Response Technology (IRT)
and clinical supply management processes.
In this role, you will partner with cross-functional study teams to support IRT setup, change management activities, system validation, and process optimization. You will act as a subject matter expert, helping ensure that clinical supply strategies are accurately translated into system requirements while maintaining quality, compliance, and operational efficiency.
This position offers an excellent opportunity for professionals with experience in clinical supplies, clinical operations, or IRT systems who enjoy working at the intersection of technology and clinical trial delivery.
Key Responsibilities
Support the design, setup, and maintenance of IRT solutions for clinical studies.
Collaborate with study teams and stakeholders to translate operational and supply requirements into IRT specifications.
Contribute to risk assessments and support user acceptance testing (UAT) activities for new study setups and system changes.
Review, develop, and execute testing documentation and associated validation activities as required.
Provide guidance and expertise on IRT-related processes, systems, and best practices.
Partner with internal and external stakeholders to support study startup, amendment implementation, and ongoing study management.
Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives aimed at enhancing IRT processes, tools, and ways of working.
Support training activities and knowledge sharing across study teams and functional groups.
Ensure activities are conducted in compliance with applicable quality standards, Good Clinical Practice (GCP), and regulatory requirements.
Required
Qualifications & Experience
Experience within clinical research, clinical supplies, clinical operations, or a related pharmaceutical/biotechnology environment.
Knowledge of clinical trial processes and the drug development lifecycle.
Familiarity with Interactive Response Technology (IRT), RTSM, IVRS/IWRS, or similar clinical trial systems.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities.
Experience collaborating with cross-functional and global stakeholders.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities.
Strong attention to detail and a quality-focused mindset.
Proficiency with clinical systems and standard business applications.
Preferred
Experience supporting clinical supply chain activities.
Exposure to study startup and system validation activities.
Understanding of GCP, GMP, and applicable regulatory requirements.
Experience contributing to process improvement initiatives and training activities.
Previous experience working within global clinical development organizations.
What You'll Bring
A collaborative and proactive approach to problem-solving.
The ability to navigate complex processes and technical systems.
Strong organizational skills and attention to detail.
A passion for improving clinical trial operations and supporting the delivery of medicines to patients.
