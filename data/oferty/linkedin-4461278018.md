At Flix, we offer a dynamic work environment with competitive pay, strong growth opportunities, and a tech-driven approach to making travel more accessible, sustainable, and affordable.
We are looking for a curious and driven Junior Technical Operations Manager with focus on AI & Automation who wants to build the operational backbone that lets us scale our platform globally with minimal resource investment. You will keep third-party partner inventory live on our platform while challenging how processes can be improved.
You will be part of a self-organized, cross-functional Platform Business Team where we test and launch new business models. This role starts from the same foundation as the rest of our Technical Operations team – integrating and maintaining partner inventory – and grows it in a new direction: using automation and AI to remove manual, repetitive work so the inventory can scale while remaining a stable team. Your work directly contributes to shaping the future of mobility.
About The Role
Integrate and maintain partner inventory. You onboard third-party carriers onto our platform and keep their inventory accurate and healthy across our tools and integration modes (self-service, manual, hybrid, and API integration), the operational core our revenue and inventory growth depend on.
Automate the repetitive. You identify manual, recurring steps in our onboarding and inventory processes and replace them with scripts, workflows, and automation so the team scales without scaling effort.
Put AI to work. You prototype and apply AI tooling – from data checks and inventory QA to document handling and internal assistants – to speed up operations and improve quality of information.
Be an expert user of our internal tools. You work closely with our engineer to resolve technical issues, improve the partner and customer experience, and turn recurring pain points into tooling improvements.
Make it data-based. You use data to find where automation pays off, measure the impact of what you build, and prioritize the next opportunity.
About You
Holds a degree in a technical, analytical, or business field – e.g. Business Information Systems, Computer Science, Engineering, Economics, Business Administration, or similar with 3+ years of relevant experience.
Comfortable with data and automation tooling – for example spreadsheets, SQL, scripting (e.g. Python), no-code/low-code automation, or hands-on experimentation with AI tools.
You are a thorough, detail-oriented person who cares about clean, reliable data.
You can set your own goals, structure your work to meet them, and find creative workarounds to meet stakeholder needs.
You love an international environment and are a team player.
You are fluent in English; any other language is a plus.
We recognize that everyone carries a unique set of valuable skills and experiences. If you think you could have an impact even though you don't meet 100% of the requirements, we still encourage you to apply. We want to hear from you!
