WHAT YOU WILL NEED:
3+ years of experience in Manufacturing Industry with a understanding of the overall functional and technical landscape (systems, tools, and technologies enabling manufacturing processes at the shop floor level)
Knowledge of IT-OT architecture (ISA-95/IEC 62264, Purdue Model, Frameworks, etc.) and its functional components, automation and robotics, plant network, security protocols and industry standards
Experience in the E2E deployment of industrial OT/IT solutions for manufacturing, including gathering functional requirements, designing the blueprint, creating test scenarios, performing regression testing, preparing technical documentation
Experience in digital transformation of manufacturing and industrial operations through the integration of advanced technologies, data, and automation with the use of solutions powered by IoT and Cloud Platforms (i.e., MS Azure, AWS) will be an advantage
Active interest and up-to-date knowledge of emerging trends and best practices in digital solutions for manufacturing ecosystems and Industry 4.0 technologies
Bachelor in automation engineering or production engineering or related field
English communication skills (written & spoken). Knowledge of German, Spanish or French will be an advantage
Key skills:
