Do you want your work to keep real robots running in the physical world?
Are you ready to learn the new exciting world of smart robots?
Do you feel tired of working in a big company?

If your answers are mostly
yes
, then you should keep reading.
At
Nomagic
, we’re on a mission to teach robots the real world. We’re now looking for a
Warehouse Operations Specialist
, who will
take full ownership of our Warsaw warehouse,
keep the inventory of robot components accurate and support the rollout of our new ERP system.
Offer essentials:
Play with robots every day
Salary: PLN 10,000-14,000 gross UoP per month
Onboarding training and support from the team
Private medical care
Multisport card
English-speaking environment
Here is why we love this job ourselves, and hope you will enjoy it too:
We build robots powered by AI
We have them in production at scale and see their real impact on critical operations.
You will work hands-on with the parts and components that go into our robots!
We’re still pretty small, so everyone has a direct impact on the final result
Our team has built Google Warsaw, unicorn startups (Climate Corp, Deepmind), tested rocket engines and worked at top companies such as Nvidia, ByteDance, Snowflake or Deepmind. Now we shape the future of smart robotics together!
We combine world-class research with top-notch engineering and apply it to solve real problems
Some of the problems you may try to solve with us:
Receiving, unloading and checking deliveries for quantity and quality
Preparing and packing goods for shipment in line with orders
Organizing stock in the warehouse so that space is used well
Operating the warehouse system (WMS) and keeping inventory data accurate
Operating warehouse equipment safely and efficiently
Keeping the warehouse clean and in order, in line with Health and Safety rules
Finding and implementing improvements to warehouse processes on your own initiative
Supporting the buyer with material requests, including spare parts
Tracking shipment statuses and reporting on incoming and ongoing deliveries
Supporting the transition of warehouse processes to our new ERP system
What skills we’d like you to have:
Experience working in a warehouse
Technical knowledge of machine parts and components
Hands-on experience with ERP and WMS systems
Accuracy, conscientiousness and good organization of your own work
Teamwork and clear communication
English at B1 level or higher, spoken and written
Nice to have:
Experience with an ERP or WMS implementation
Good Excel skills
Experience running stocktakes and inventory counts
What should you expect once you apply?
30-minute call with our internal recruiter
45-minute call with the Hiring Manager, including short practical tasks
On-site interview in Warsaw: a 45-minute meeting with our Hardware team, a 45-minute problem solving session, a 30-minute culture fit conversation and a 30-minute warehouse tour
We make the final decision after max. 2-3 days after on-site interview
