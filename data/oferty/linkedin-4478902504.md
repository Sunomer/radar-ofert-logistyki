Title And Summary
Customer Engagement & Relationship Specialist
Overview
Mastercard’s Bezcenne Chwile is a leading banking loyalty program in Poland, connecting cardholders with rewards, offers and experiences that make everyday spending more rewarding.
Operating in a B2B2C model, we partner with banks and fintechs to create value for program users and strengthen long-term customer engagement. Through an attractive rewards catalog, relevant offers and customer-focused initiatives, we help our partners deliver a compelling experience for their customers.
We are looking for a Customer Engagement & Relationship Specialist to support three connected areas: customer engagement, partner relationships and the continued development of the rewards proposition. This is an opportunity to contribute ideas, work closely with internal and external partners, and help shape the experience of Bezcenne Chwile program users.
All About The Role
In this role, you will:
Build and maintain strong relationships with participating banks and fintechs, supporting their customer engagement objectives and helping them maximize the value of the program.
Contribute to the development and optimization of the rewards catalog, offers and other customer engagement initiatives.
Identify opportunities to strengthen customer engagement and program participation, drawing on customer needs, performance insights and market trends.
Collaborate with Marketing and agency partners to develop clear and engaging program-user and partner communications.
Review program, campaign and reward-redemption performance and translate insights into practical recommendations.
Research market trends and benchmark loyalty programs, rewards and potential partners to bring new ideas to the program.
Coordinate selected customer engagement and rewards initiatives from idea through implementation.
Partner across internal teams, including Product, Marketing, Account Management and Operations, to support a consistent program experience.
Contribute to agreed program and partner objectives and KPIs.
All About You
2+ years of professional experience in a customer, commercial, relationship, marketing or other relevant business environment.
Fluent Polish, with strong written communication skills, and very good English.
Strong communication and relationship-building skills, with the ability to work effectively with different stakeholders.
Curious and proactive, with the confidence to contribute ideas and look for opportunities to improve the customer experience.
Comfortable combining creative thinking with data and performance insights to support recommendations.
Well organized, with the ability to manage multiple priorities and follow through on agreed actions.
Collaborative and comfortable working across teams in an international environment.
Nice to Have
Experience in loyalty, rewards, CRM or customer engagement.
Experience in marketing communications, campaigns or working with creative agencies.
Experience working with banks, fintechs or other customer-focused organizations.
Exposure to account management, partnerships or external stakeholder management.
Experience working with customer engagement, campaign or behavioral data.
