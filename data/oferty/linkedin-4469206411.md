About the role: What's in the Box
As HelloFresh scales globally, our Supply Chain Technology team is expanding to solve unique, high-stakes logistics challenges. In this role, you will lead our Delivery Network Squad, taking ownership of scalable products that directly impact how millions of meal kits move through our logistics network and reach customer doorsteps. You will collaborate closely with engineering tribes and operational leaders across all HelloFresh markets to optimize existing shipping capabilities and build innovative 0-to-1 solutions.
At HelloTech, flexibility and cross-functional collaboration are core to how we work. While this role is aligned to a specific Alliance, strong candidates may also be considered for opportunities across different teams or projects.
