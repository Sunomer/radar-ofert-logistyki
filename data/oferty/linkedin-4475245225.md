About the job
Orbico Group is No. 1 distributor and omnichannel sales and marketing services provider
in Europe, founded in 1987. We have a powerful international distribution and logistics network in 20 European countries and we employ more than 10 000 people. We are organized into three divisions: FMCG, Beauty and Sport & Style and we distribute many globally known brands including P&G, Lavazza, Kellogg’s, Mars, Mattel, SC Johnson, Coty, Converse and others. Winning in our business demands high flexibility and investing in people's development. Orbico way is to do it with the whole heart and with full dedication.
Currently Orbico is looking for:
Group Supply Process Expert
The role will be part of Group Supply Transformation Programme within Orbico Group structure. This role is ideal if you enjoy combining business understanding, technology and want to actively contribute to the improvement of supply operations. You will be working directly with stakeholders from majority of Orbico markets (most of all Bulgaria, Romania, Bosnia and Hercegovina, Croatia, Slovenia and Poland) as well as with Group functions.
Main objectives:
The role objective of the Group Supply Process Expert is to support the design, standardization, implementation and continuous improvement of end-to-end supply processes across the Group, covering warehouse and transport operations. The role translates business and operational requirements into scalable process and system solutions and supports their successful implementation and adoption across companies and countries. Through process analysis, performance monitoring, stakeholder coordination and functional expertise in applicable supply solutions, the role contributes to improved service, productivity, quality, cost efficiency, safety and process compliance. Depending on business priorities, the role may cover the full supply-process scope or specialize in warehouse and/or transport processes.
