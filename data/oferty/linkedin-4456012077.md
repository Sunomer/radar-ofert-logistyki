Who We Are
With 40,000 people on the five continents, SUEZ is a world leader in smart and sustainable resource management. We provide water and waste management solutions that enable cities and industries optimize their resource management and strengthen their environmental and economic performances.
The teams contribute to waste recovery services from collection, sorting, dismantling, recycling, recovering and marketing of new resources and specialized services on industrial sites. Industrial Waste Specialties (IWS), the Business Unit in charge of hazardous waste management within the SUEZ Division Recycling & Recovery Europe, was launched in 2016 in order to boost the business activity with industrial customers across Europe.

SUEZ
is present in Poland since 1972 with current activities in DBFO, O&M, Smart Solutions and entering the industry of Energy from Waste. Suez Poland is looking for a Project Manager to lead a PPP, DBFO project in Czernica:
