Job Description
We are looking for an Intern who will join the team of Regional Supply Planners in our Global Business Services (GBS) in Warsaw.
In this role you will have an opportunity to gain hands-on experience in the area of Supply Planning, by supporting planning process via learning to plan in APO, short and long term planning process audit, harmonization proposal and preparing operating manuals with the help of our experts.
We will also assign to you one additional project to help you develop your project management skills and to enhance your knowledge about supply chain processes.
Qualifications
Working at JDE Peet’s is hands-on, giving you the chance to leave your mark while working together as one team. We’re a fast-paced company on a mission, focused on what matters most. Skills
For this role as an
Intern in Regional Supply Planning team,
we’re looking for someone who has the following skills and experience:
You are in your final year of Bachelor's studies or currently pursuing a Master's degree in the field of Supply Chain, Economics, Management, Data Analysis, or a related area;
You are interested in career development in Supply Chain;
You have strong communication skills, you’re not afraid to tackle challenges and are eager to learn and take accountability for your results;
You are fluent in English both is speaking and writing;
You have good knowledge of MS Office, especially Excel;
Nice to have: you already have some experience with SAP and/or PowerBI;
You are available to spend with us at least 30 hours per week.
