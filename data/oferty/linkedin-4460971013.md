Department : Customer Care
Reporting to : Customer Care and Quality Director
Location : Warsaw, Al. Jerozolimskie 180, Poland
Job Purpose
The Customer Care Manager is responsible for strengthening the commercial relationship with clients, to adapt process to needs of market, to control the services of the repair centers and to ensure that the costs involved match to the service produced.
Key Responsibilities
Define, negotiate, ensure the implement of services proposed to clients for the country devoted
Coordinate and ensure the follow up services delivered by the repair centers
Monitor the KPI and take measure necessaries
Meet service goals and achieve service level agreements (SLAs)
Make the follow-up of SLA with operators and distributors
Choose the repair Center suitable and negotiate the services
Follow the warranty budget and check the invoices
Work with customers and/or partners to receive accurate information used to analyse and diagnose product and service issues
To be interface and main representative of the product and services quality for clients
Make a report and a follow up of quality issues
Analyse statistics and data to determinate the level of customer service
Develop process, policies, and standards of customer service.
Profile and Experience
Previous experience in a similar role within the telecom industry or a fast-paced goods international company, ideally with both a B2B and B2C background.
Strong knowledge of Consumer goods products, White goods, TV, Mobile categories
Prior successful experience in managing teams within a multicultural environment.
English – both written and oral communication skills.
Key Competencies
Demonstrated excellence in communication skills.
Customer focus
Analytical skills
Strong autonomy, sense of priorities, quickly adaptable
Proactive and persuasive
Resilient
