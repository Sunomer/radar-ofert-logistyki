We are looking for a Change Management candidate for finance integration and transformation initiatives. The ideal candidate is a pragmatic professional with a firm understanding of finance processes, systems, and operating models.
The change manager role requires good interpersonal and communication skills as well as the ability to build and maintain business relationships. You can stand your ground and know how to manoeuvre across the organisation as you adapt the way to pitch the change management activities to the context and stakeholder needs.
“You must be resilient and able to manage a variety of stakeholders, create transparency, listen, and find ways to guide and accommodate project stakeholders in the change process. This means you should be able to sell and facilitate the change management approach and be prepared to adjust the pitch and ambition level accordingly,” explains Kira.

Your Responsibilities
Execute change management activities for finance transformation, integration, and ERP‑driven projects.
Translate complex finance and system changes into clear, structured, and actionable communication for impacted stakeholders.
Develop and deliver communication, training, and stakeholder‑engagement plans tailored to finance audiences.
Support testing, cutover, Hypercare, and stabilization phases from a people‑and‑adoption perspective.
Provide fact‑based insights on change readiness and adoption risks, escalating gaps where needed.
Partner with Finance Transformation, IT, and external vendors to minimise business disruption and enable value realisation.
Build strong relationships with global, regional, and local finance stakeholders.
Facilitate alignment across Finance leaders, process owners, SMEs, and country teams.
Adapt the change approach and messaging depending on project context and stakeholder maturity.
Promote transparency, collaboration, and continuous improvement throughout the change journey.
Our Requirements
Core experience & capabilities
3–5 years of hands‑on experience applying a structured change management framework (e.g., Prosci ADKAR®).
Experience supporting ERP‑driven finance transformations, mergers, or integrations.
Ability to convert complex system and finance‑process changes into clear and actionable stakeholder messages.
Strong stakeholder‑management skills and ability to operate across global, regional, and local teams.
Proven ability to identify adoption risks and propose practical mitigation measures.
Professional fluency in English (both spoken and written).
Ways of working and mindset
People‑focused and delivery‑oriented mindset.
Ability to navigate a matrix organisation and build strong working relationships.
Resilient, adaptable, and comfortable working with diverse stakeholders.
Proactive approach to problem‑solving and readiness assessments.
Ability to facilitate change activities and adjust approach based on stakeholder needs.
