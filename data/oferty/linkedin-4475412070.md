Position Summary:
We are looking for a stand-out Project Manager to lead end-to-end finished goods (FG) transfers to EMO (third-party manufacturing) within the Europe Disentanglement programme. This is a significant and complex Business Transformation to create a standalone operation by separating Vestacy business from Reckitt core, touching all functions, with Supply Chain and Manufacturing at its heart.
You will run multiple sub-projects in parallel (typ. 2-4), each lasting 6-24 months, depending on complexity. You will own delivery: developing an integrated plan, identifying risks/issues, execution and escalation, ensuring no disruption to supply.
Key Responsibilities:
Lead a portfolio of EMO transfer projects end-to-end (2-4 in parallel).
Build and drive integrated project plans covering all workstreams.
Coordinate cross-functional teams: R&D, Quality, Procurement, Regulatory, Logistics, Finance.
Drive EMO manufacturing readiness (validation plans, documentation, process readiness - through functions).
Own risk and issue management: quantify impact, drive mitigation, escalate early with solutions.
Ensure supply continuity.
Track and control project costs (no direct budget ownership) and escalate deviations.
Provide clear, fact-based reporting to PMO and senior stakeholders.
Drive execution discipline - no passive coordination.
The Experience we are looking for:
3-5 years project management experience in FMCG / pharma / regulated environment.
Proven delivery of complex supply chain or product transfer projects.
Strong communication skills at all levels of the organisation including stakeholder management and influencing.
High degree of autonomy with ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
Results oriented, with ability to manage multiple projects in parallel under pressure.
Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making abilities.
Structured, pragmatic and delivery-focused mindset.
Fluent English; Polish is a plus.
Willingness for occasional travel within Europe (limited, mostly remote collaboration).
Previous experience in Manufacturing, Engineering or Production environments is a nice-to-have.
Previous experience in Change Management, significant Business Transformation (operational or digital) is a plus.
