About Customs Support Group
Customs Support Group (CSG) is Europe’s leading independent customs services provider. Founded in 1964, we operate in 14 European countries with 100+ offices and a team of 1,700+ customs professionals.
We help companies move goods across borders smoothly and compliantly, taking the pressure out of customs.
What we do: We support more than 60,000 clients and process over 20 million declaration lines each year.
From day-to-day customs declarations to compliance, duty optimization and trade advice, we make cross-border trade easier and more predictable.
How we work: We combine local customs expertise with modern digital tools to handle large volumes accurately and at speed.
Our technology helps teams work smarter, reduces manual effort, and improves turnaround times.
Why it matters: Customs rules are becoming more complex and mistakes are costly. Delays, compliance risks and unclear regulations can slow businesses down.
CSG helps clients stay compliant, reduce risk and keep their supply chains moving.

Purpose of the Position
We are looking for an experienced and motivated Customs Declarant for Norway to join our international customs operations team.
In this role, you will be responsible for handling import and export declarations for shipments to and from Norway, as well as supporting transit procedures and bonded warehouse activities.
You will ensure that customs declarations and supporting trade documentation are processed accurately and in accordance with applicable regulations. You will also cooperate closely with suppliers, carriers, recipients and internal customs teams to ensure smooth and efficient cross-border operations.
This is a permanent, full-time position. The team operates Monday to Friday between 07:00 and 18:00, with individual working hours arranged according to the operational schedule.
Main Responsibilities
Prepare and process customs declarations for import and export shipments to and from Norway
Handle transit procedures and maintain accurate bonded warehouse records
Register, review and perform quality assurance checks on customs and trade documentation
Ensure customs declarations comply with applicable import and export laws, regulations and procedures
Perform goods classification and ensure accurate customs data is used in declarations
Provide guidance regarding documentation requirements for border crossings
Cooperate with suppliers, carriers, recipients and internal customs teams to ensure efficient customs clearance
Identify and resolve issues related to customs documentation and clearance procedures
Maintain high standards of accuracy, compliance and service quality
