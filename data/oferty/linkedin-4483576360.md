Lead Manufacturing Excellence Across Four Production Plants
At Coca-Cola HBC, we are looking for an experienced and inspirational Manufacturing Director to lead and transform manufacturing operations across our four production facilities in Poland and the Baltics. This is a strategic leadership role for a manufacturing professional who combines operational excellence, strategic investments for growth, people leadership, and continuous improvement expertise to drive performance, efficiency, quality, and sustainable growth. As Manufacturing Director, you will shape the future of our manufacturing network by leading modernization initiatives, driving digital transformation, building organizational capability, and ensuring consistent manufacturing standards across all plants.
WHAT YOU'LL DO
Strategic Leadership
Develop and execute short-, medium-, and long-term manufacturing strategies aligned with business objectives.
Drive operational excellence across four production facilities, ensuring performance consistency and best-practice sharing.
Lead manufacturing transformation initiatives, including digitalization, automation, and capability-building programs.
Support long-term investment planning and manufacturing network development.
Operational Excellence
Oversee manufacturing operations, production planning, asset utilization, and operational performance.
Deliver productivity, efficiency, quality, cost, and service targets.
Implement Lean Manufacturing, Six Sigma, and continuous improvement methodologies.
Identify opportunities to optimize production processes, reduce waste, and improve plant performance.
Quality, Safety & Compliance
Ensure the highest standards of product quality, food safety, environmental management, and workplace safety.
Maintain compliance with internal standards, industry regulations, and ISO certifications.
Foster a strong culture of quality, safety, and continuous improvement across all manufacturing teams.
People Leadership
Lead, coach, and develop high-performing manufacturing leadership teams.
Build organizational capabilities and succession pipelines across manufacturing functions.
Drive employee engagement and foster a culture aligned with Coca-Cola HBC values:
Cross-Functional Collaboration
Partner closely with Supply Planning, Engineering, QSE, Procurement, Logistics, and other business functions.
Ensure seamless coordination across the end-to-end supply chain to support business growth and customer satisfaction.
WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR
Experience
12+ years of progressive manufacturing leadership experience, including significant experience in senior manufacturing management roles
Proven track record of leading large-scale manufacturing operations within a complex, regulated environment
Experience in FMCG, beverage, food, consumer goods, or similar manufacturing industries is highly preferred
Demonstrated success in driving operational excellence, transformation, and cost optimization initiatives
Master's degree in Engineering, Manufacturing, Business Administration, or a related field
MBA is considered an advantage
Strong knowledge of:
Manufacturing operations and production management
Bottling technologies and processes
Quality Management Systems and ISO standards
Engineering and asset management
Health, Safety & Environmental standards
Lean Manufacturing and Six Sigma methodologies
ERP, MES, and manufacturing analytics platforms
Leadership Competencies
Exceptional people leadership and coaching skills
Strategic thinker with strong business acumen
Strong problem-solving and decision-making capabilities
Ability to lead through change and influence across multiple stakeholders
Strong project and program management experience
Languages & Certifications
Fluent English (C2 level)
Certifications such as CMP, CSCP, Lean Six Sigma, or similar are considered an advantage
