We are looking for:
Transition Manager
Your Future Role:
Lead and coordinate the end-to-end transition lifecycle for new AES Managed Services engagements,
Define and manage transition scope, plans, milestones, dependencies, risks and readiness criteria,
Lead client and service onboarding into the AES and MSO operating model,
Mobilize the required delivery organization by triggering and coordinating MSO processes, including recruitment, staffing, team onboarding, training, and access provisioning,
Plan and coordinate knowledge transfer between implementation teams, clients, incumbent providers and Managed Services teams,
Coordinate Shadow, Reverse Shadow, service rehearsal and Hypercare activities, as applicable to the agreed transition approach,
Establish transition governance, including roles and responsibilities, reporting routines, decision forums, escalation paths, and stakeholder communication,
Ensure operational readiness before go-live, including required people, processes, tools, accesses, documentation, runbooks and support procedures,
Facilitate readiness reviews, quality gates and transition sign-off based on agreed acceptance criteria,
Monitor and manage transition risks, issues, assumptions, dependencies, and open decisions, ensuring timely escalation and resolution,
Coordinate cross-functional and cross-territory stakeholders, including client teams, AES, MSO, Service Delivery Managers, delivery centers and external providers,
Support service stabilisation after go-live and manage the structured handover to the Service Delivery Manager and business-as-usual operations,
Capture lessons learned and provide structured feedback to support the improvements of AES and MSO transition templates, checklists and practices.
Apply if you have:
Experience leading service transitions, outsourcing transitions or Managed Services implementations,
Experience managing client onboarding and operational service activation,
Experience coordinating knowledge transfer, service setup and operational readiness activities,
Strong project and stakeholder management skills,
Experience coordinating cross-functional and geographically distributed teams,
Experience managing risks, issues, dependencies, and escalations,
Understanding of Managed Services operating models,
Strong planning, organization, communication skills and problem-solving skills.
