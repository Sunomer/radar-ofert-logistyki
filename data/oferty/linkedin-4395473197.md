Global E-Commerce Operations Specialist
We are looking for a Global E-Commerce Operations Specialist to join our growing DTC team. In this role, you will be at the heart of our e-commerce operations — driving commercial performance, owning analytics, and collaborating across functions to deliver outstanding results on a global scale.
Your Contribution Is Appreciated, and You Will:
Commercial & Operations (50%)
Deliver commercial and operational initiatives to support business growth across each region
Continuously optimize and improve the end-to-end performance of the E-Commerce platform
Serve as the primary operational point of contact within the DTC team, liaising with Marketing, Engineering, Planning, I.T., Supply Chain, DTC Operations and Customer Service
Business Analytics & Demand Generation (50%)
Own all E-Commerce analytics and metrics, ensuring data accuracy and actionable insights
Manage global E-Commerce campaigns — ensuring agreed activities are executed, the calendar of events is kept up to date, and all relevant stakeholders are briefed
To Perform Well in This Role, You Will Have:
Essential
Bachelor's degree in Economics, Business Administration, Statistics, Finance, or a related field, with relevant work experience
Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines across various ongoing projects
Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office programs
Excellent communication skills to effectively interact with a diverse group of business stakeholders
Ability to work both independently and collaboratively as part of a cross-functional team
Desirable
Operational and technical knowledge in a DTC business environment
Familiarity with business intelligence software such as Power BI, Qlik, Tableau, or similar
Experience working within an agile framework, including managing backlogs and product ownership
MA or MBA in a related field
Experience with Salesforce or similar E-Commerce platforms
Understanding of the DTC vertical and its relationship with other sales channels within the business
The Offer
A full pay package, together with an annual bonus, and an attractive benefits package including medical care, lunch/cafeteria card, life insurance and pension plan
An environment which provides opportunities of working with multiple stakeholders, professional support and the possibility to share knowledge and best practices
On-going development opportunities in a multinational environment that will inspire you to grow professionally and personally
Wide variety of projects and tasks, innovative goals, and freedom to plan and execute them professionally
Flexible working conditions – hybrid or remote work model within Poland
Possibility to work in a modern office in the centre of Warsaw
