The Impact You'll Make
What does it take to ensure that products manufactured by external partners meet the quality standards our customers expect? It takes technical expertise, strong collaboration and people who are not afraid to challenge the status quo.
At
Danfoss Climate Solutions
, we're looking for a
Senior Engineer, EMP Customer/Supplier Quality
to help us ensure the quality and reliability of our Externally Manufactured Products (EMP).
As our new Senior Engineer, EMP Customer/Supplier Quality, you will play a vital role in improving customer experience and ensuring product and process quality by supporting both Danfoss customers and finished product suppliers. Your proactive approach and technical expertise will help sustain and enhance the high standards expected from Danfoss Climate Solutions – a segment focused on energy-efficient, sustainable heating and cooling.
Every day, you’ll collaborate with both internal and external stakeholders to ensure product quality, handle claims, drive improvements at suppliers, and support Danfoss’ commitment to customer satisfaction. Your time will be spent leading problem-solving initiatives (8D), auditing suppliers, and supporting product approval and development processes assuring strong quality voice during its execution from design phase up to the industrialization and serial production launch.
From investigating customer complaints to ensuring quality requirements are met before production launch, your work will have a direct impact on customer satisfaction and the performance of our heating and cooling solutions.
What You’ll Be Doing
Ensure the quality and reliability of finished products manufactured by external suppliers, driving compliance with Danfoss quality requirements and customer expectations.
Lead customer and supplier complaint investigations, using structured problem-solving methods such as 8D to identify root causes and implement effective corrective and preventive actions.
Work closely with external manufacturing partners to improve product and process quality, conduct supplier audits and follow up on improvement initiatives.
Support new product development and industrialization projects, ensuring quality requirements are met from the design phase through supplier PPAP approval and serial production launch.
Collaborate with international teams, including Engineering, Sales and Application Engineers, to resolve technical quality challenges, strengthen quality processes and continuously improve customer satisfaction.
What We're Looking For
A degree in Engineering, preferably in Manufacturing, Electronics, Mechanical Engineering, Quality or a related technical field.
Experience in manufacturing, supplier or customer quality engineering, ideally around 5 years, with knowledge of ISO 9001 and/or IATF 16949 standards.
Familiarity with structured problem-solving and quality methodologies, such as 8D, APQP, PPAP and Quality Core Tools.
Experience with supplier or internal audits, new product development or quality improvement projects. Knowledge of electronic or HVAC products would be an advantage.
Good communication and collaboration skills, fluency in English and confidence working with international stakeholders, along with proficiency in MS Office.
Nice to have:
ISO 9001 or VDA 6.3 auditor certification and project management experience.
