Responsibilities
Leading and coordinating day-to-day operations of the customs administration team.
Leading and optimizing work processes, including implementation of best practices.
Coordinating daily tasks with team members.
Managing delivery of Service Level Agreements within the managed service area.
Creating and maintaining effective cooperation with other teams within the Customs department, other departments, and branches.
Acting as a Point of Contact for issues, escalations, and operational support.
Managing team performance and efficiency based on relevant analyses, KPIs, and reports.
Creating and maintaining process documentation.
Participating in budget planning and actively managing the P&L to ensure financial performance and target achievement.
Our Requirements
Minimum 2 years of hands-on experience in a similar position.
Strong communication skills and the ability to effectively interact with stakeholders at all organizational levels.
Higher education, preferably in logistics, customs, management, or a related field.
Knowledge of customs regulations and the transportation, shipping, and logistics (TSL) industry.
Knowledge of English at a minimum B2/C1 level.
Knowledge of MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint). Proactive use of AI-powered tools and digital solutions to improve productivity, streamline processes, and enhance operational efficiency is encouraged.
Ability to effectively engage, motivate, and develop direct reports.
Supporting the professional development of team members.
Ability to deliver company goals and drive operational excellence.
Strong understanding of the transportation and logistics industry, customs operations, and financial management of an operational team.
Good understanding of change management principles and practices.
