Job Purpose
We are seeking an experienced
Problem Solving Expert (Continuous Improvement & Operational Excellence)
to drive strategic improvement initiatives across our manufacturing operations. Lead complex problem-solving activities addressing customer complaints, warranty concerns, chronic manufacturing issues, and product quality challenges.
