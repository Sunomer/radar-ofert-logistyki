THE WORK:
Although no two days at Accenture are the same, as an Aftersales, Spares & Warranty Analyst or a Consultant in Industry X / Intelligent Asset Management Team, a typical day might include:
Working closely with client business stakeholders to understand their requirements and provide optimal solutions in the areas of aftersales, warranty and spare parts management business processes
Development, optimization, reshaping of different business process from the area of aftermarket service operations with focus on warranty optimization, spare parts optimization (incl. parts pricing & inventory) and supply chain management and bridging business processes with technology (eg. SAP, Business Intelligence tool, etc.)
Leading technology platform solutioning, implementation and enhancement projects, including planning, design, fit/gap analysis, build and configuration, testing, cutover and any other deployment activities;
Collaborating with other cross-functional teams to ensure seamless integration with other ERP modules
Master Data Management and Governance focused on assets maintenance operations, MRO supply chain, quality assurance and warehouse management

HERE IS WHAT YOU’LL NEED:
Experience of working in aftermarket service operations space across areas like warranty optimization, after market spare parts optimization including parts pricing, parts inventory and supply chain management, most preferably in industrial or automotive
Strong experience with SAP SD/CS or related modules (at least Key User) and perfect knowledge of related business processes with main focus aftersales, especially warranty management, spare parts management
Proven ability to work creatively and analytically in a problem-solving environment
Project Management skills and proficiency in analyzing and translating Business Requirements to Technical Requirements and IT Architecture;
Experience in Project Management activities including planning, deliverables development, project execution monitoring, managing stakeholders’ relationships, mitigating risks and managing change requests
Change Management in review of existing processes and procedures, definition of future state processes, development of training material and delivery of training sessions to business users.
Excellent Business English communication skills (written & spoken). Knowledge of German or French would be appreciated
Analytical and quantitative skills and the ability to use hard data and metrics to back up assumptions and develop business cases, including ability to clearly communicate these data insights to others.
Experience in developing analytics model to drive cost to serve optimization in the service operations space and understanding of manufacturing processes is well received
Ability to work in a rapidly changing environment where continuous innovation is desired.
