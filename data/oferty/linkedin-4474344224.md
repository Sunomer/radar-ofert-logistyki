Responsibilities
The COO Office, Transformation, Process & Risk Manager plays a key to support the continued growth and transformation of Societe Generale Poland by strengthening governance, operational efficiency, and organizational resilience
The role is responsible for leading business transformation initiatives, supporting process optimization and governance activities, and contributing to the identification and mitigation of operational risks. Acting as a trusted partner to the COO, the incumbent ensures effective coordination across business lines and support functions while helping maintain a strong control and risk culture within the organization.
The role acts as the COO's trusted delegate, providing direct support on strategic priorities, governance matters, and executive decision-making
This role offers broad exposure to Corporate and Investment Banking activities and supporting functions, including Capital Markets, Global Banking, Transaction Banking, Securities Services, Operations, IT, Finance, Compliance, Legal, Human Resources, and Real Estate.
The role is based in Poland and covers CIB activities. Given the strong integration of activities within Societe Generale Group, the role requires multiple interactions with international stakeholders.
The role is currently an individual contributor position with significant transversal leadership responsibilities across the organization
.
Your future scope of responsibility will cover:
Business Transformation & Change Management
Lead and coordinate strategic and operational transformation initiatives across SG Poland
Support the design and execution of business improvement roadmaps
Drive change management activities to facilitate the successful implementation of new operating models, processes, systems, and regulatory requirements
Identify risks associated with transformation initiatives and propose appropriate mitigation actions
Coordinate stakeholders across business, operations, IT, risk, compliance, legal, and support functions
Business Process Management & Governance
Review, document, standardize, and optimize business processes
Support business units in aligning procedures with internal policies, regulatory requirements, and industry best practices
Ensure effective implementation of process changes across teams
Conduct regular process reviews to identify efficiency opportunities and strengthen the control environment
Contribute to governance initiatives supporting operational excellence and sustainable growth
COO Office Support & Governance
Act as designated backup to the COO and ensure continuity of key activities during periods of absence
Prepare executive presentations, dashboards, and management reports for senior leadership
Develop a strong understanding of all activities coordinated by the COO Office
Work closely with Operational Risk, Business Continuity, Data Governance, and Third-Party Risk specialists to identify risks and recommend mitigation actions
Become the back up of experts to ensure a continuity of operations
Coordinate and chair the Regulatory Reporting Committee and monitor the execution of agreed actions
Support continuous improvement of the COO Office operating model and governance framework
