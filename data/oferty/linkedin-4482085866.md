At ICON Strategic Solutions, you will work in a sponsor-dedicated model, supported by ICON’s global expertise.
As the world’s largest FSP organisation, with over 90 sponsor partnerships, we offer stability, career development and a collaborative environment where your work contributes to the development of treatments that benefit patients worldwide.
As a Supply Chain Planning Analyst, you would be responsible for supporting the Supply Chain Planners in coordinating and executing Clinical supply plans across the Drug Development and Supply network through system updates, data inputs, and operational management
What You Will Do
Contribute to delivering achievable supply plans that meet project timelines and enable efficient utilisation of internal and external manufacturing network
Key Responsibilities Include
BSC/BA. or M.S. degree in Pharmacy, Chemistry, Engineering, Supply Chain, Logistics, orequivalent experience in a relevant field.
Support maintenance of master data, supply plans, and inventory projections forDrug Product, Placebo Bulk, Commercial Product (comparators), and Primary Packs.
Liaise with manufacturing network partners (GSC, CMSC, External Supply, QA, Logistics) to align forecast updates, delivery dates, and release updates in support of the delivery of our Clinical supply plans.
Help facilitate and document DP/PB/CP/PP supply planning meetings, includingpreparation of materials and recording decisions and actions in support of theD&OP cycle.
Capture and update information for long-range planning (CMC development plans) to support rough cut capacity planning.
Provide inputs to secondary pack readiness and supply chain network planning bymaintaining accurate information in relevant supply chain nodes and tools.
Execute and follow through on shipping requests to enable the movement of bulk materials within the supply chain.
Support projects that address supply chain planning issues
Your Profile
You will bring relevant clinical supplies and logistics experience, along with the following qualifications and skills.
Required Qualifications And Experience
Strong Excel skills and ability to understand complex workbooks for planning and inventory analysis.
Understanding of planning activities between supply chain nodes and key master data elements (yields, lot sizes, lead times, etc.).
Ability to work effectively in a matrix environment and influence stakeholders inmanufacturing, supply chain, quality, and logistics on inventory and supply topics.
Experience (or strong interest) in project management and inventory management within a global or complex supply chain context.
Strong organisational and planning skills, with flexibility to work to aggressive andchanging timelines and with limited information.
Strong oral and written communication skills, interpersonal skills, and problem solving abilities suitable for complex global supply issues.
Experience working with supply chain logistics processes, including material flow, transportation, inventory control, and planning methodologies used in supply chain organizations.
