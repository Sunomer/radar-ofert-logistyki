Role Overview
Join a large-scale SAP S/4HANA rollout project for a leading manufacturing client as a SAP EWM Consultant. We are looking for two experienced professionals who want to play a key role in shaping and delivering a global transformation program.
Willingness to travel (Min 50%. 10-12 weeks of travel over 6-9 months of rollout duration)
Requirements
At least 5 years of SAP project experience in SAP EWM / WM / Stock Room Management solutions.
Experience with both S/4 HANA embedded and decentralized warehouse scenarios on SAP EWM.
Strong experience in Inbound, Outbound, and Internal warehouse processes. Hands-on experience with integration to mobile devices
Experience with MFS (Material Flow systems) & warehouse automation scenarios are a value add.
Deep experience with integration to other SAP modules like MM, SD, PP and TM. Experience with integration with 3PL warehouse scenarios
Experience at least 1 SAP S/4HANA rollout with experience in conducting Fit to template / explore phases.
Experience with data migration concepts using standard SAP toolsets for Controlling masterdata and transaction objects.
Good communication and assertiveness as the consultant will play the role of ‘Template Defender’.
