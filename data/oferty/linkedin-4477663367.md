We are looking for a
Material Handler
to join a growing manufacturing facility producing strut and 2-D steel frames for major data centre projects across Europe.
You will be responsible for the
movement, storage and preparation of materials
across the production facility, making sure the production team has everything they need to keep the operation running efficiently.
Key Responsibilities
Receive, check and move materials around the facility
Prepare and supply materials to the production lines
Load and unload deliveries
Organise and maintain stock within the warehouse
Support the strut and frame fabrication teams
Check quantities and materials against production requirements
Assist with loading finished products for shipment to European sites
Maintain a clean, safe and organised working environment
Follow H&S and warehouse procedures
Work closely with production, warehouse and logistics teams
About You
Previous experience in a
warehouse, production or material handling
role
Comfortable working in a manufacturing environment
Good organisational skills and attention to detail
Physically fit and comfortable with manual handling
Forklift/MEWP experience is advantageous
Reliable, hardworking and safety conscious
Hands-on attitude with no ego
Able to work well as part of a team
Manufacturing, construction, MEP or data centre experience is advantageous
