Responsibilities:
Shipment management
Oversee end‑to‑end domestic and international (within EU) transport by road and courier via freight forwarders
Use the TMS to analyze and optimize costs for freight, customs clearance, warehousing, and related logistics activities
Prepare and run tenders for domestic and cross‑border shipments; evaluate carrier bids to secure the best rates, transit times, and delivery terms
Maintain strategic relationships with key carriers and forwarders; monitor service levels and performance
Generate all shipping documents and courier labels per system and operational standards; coordinate pickups and deliveries with partners (e.g., DPD, DHL)
Issue transport orders in the ERP system and track execution
Schedule delivery appointments with customers; liaise with Sales and Warehouse teams to align on dispatch slots
Manage claims and discrepancies, collaborating with Operations, Logistics, and customers to resolve issues and provide high‑quality support via phone and email
Warehouse coordination
Align with warehouse teams on order volumes and pick‑pack schedules
Generate ERP packing lists and prepare regional shipment documentation by customer order
Monitor loading operations to safeguard on‑time departures
Arrange warehouse appointment notices and oversee inbound receipt and loading activities
Investigate and resolve inventory discrepancies throughout the supply chain
Support periodic physical inventory counts
Invoice processing (transport & customs)
Record, verify, and reconcile invoices for logistics and customs services
Enter invoices into ERP, allocate costs to individual shipments, and manage quarterly/annual settlements
Coordinate with subcontractors to clear payment delays and balance variances
Office shipment handling
Pack and label office parcels; arrange courier pickups and drop‑offs
Maintain stock of packaging materials according to internal demand
Serve as primary contact for couriers to ensure seamless internal mail and parcel flows
Requirements:
Professional Experience & Technical Skills
Bachelor’s degree in Logistics, Supply Chain Management or a related field
Indepth knowledge of road cargo transport document flow and regulatory frameworks (e.g. CMR Convention, ADR)
Minimum of 4 years’ handson experience in national and international logistics and freight forwarding
Proven ability to apply and interpret Incoterms 2020 in import/export transactions
Proficiency with ERP platforms and transportplanning systems (e.g., TMS)
Full professional fluency in Polish; English proficiency at B1/B2 level; Ukrainian is a strong advantage
Core Competencies & Personal Attributes
Strong quantitative skills with the capacity to analyze costs, KPIs, and performance metrics
Proactive in issue identification and rapid resolution of operational challenges
Exceptional at prioritizing tasks, managing deadlines, and coordinating crossfunctional activities
Collaborative mindset with excellent interpersonal and stakeholdermanagement abilities
Comfortable multitasking under high workload; responds effectively to changing priorities and crisis situations
Eager to embrace new tools (TMS, ERP) and best practices to optimize logistics processes
