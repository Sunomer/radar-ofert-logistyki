About The Department
BYD's planning & commerce department is a key link in BYD's supply chain system. We are committed to tasks such as demand forecasting, production factory docking, European market docking, and distribution plan. Our mission is to ensure a smooth process from manufacturing to delivery of BYD products, provide customers with efficient and reliable services, and provide strategic support for the company's global business development.
