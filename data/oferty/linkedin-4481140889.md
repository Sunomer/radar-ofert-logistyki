Employee Experience Process Specialist (m/f/d)
Temporary (approx. 2 years)
The Employee Experience Process Specialist ensures the efficient delivery of services to employees, entities and regions in line with agreed service levels. The role provides timely, high-quality, end-to-end support across People and Culture processes while following established procedures and addressing stakeholder needs.
Position
As the Employee Experience Process Specialist, you will
Deliver Talent Management and Learning and Development services to employees, entities, regions and headquarters in line with agreed service levels
Manage Growth Compass sessions and Talent Reviews in People Central, ensuring timely completion and review quality
Support training programmes by managing employee nominations, enrolments and completion follow-up
Coordinate mentoring programme activities, including nominations, mentor and mentee matching and communication
Administer assessments in accordance with the Global Assessment Portfolio
Manage access to and analyse the use of global digital learning platforms, including LinkedIn Learning, when required
Maintain local learning catalogues in People Central and manage employee enrolment and session follow-up
Address Talent Management and Learning and Development queries and act as an internal adviser within the assigned area of responsibility
Update Standard Operating Procedures and related working documents following system or process changes
Advise the JTI community on the effective use of MyJTI and direct user feedback to MyJTI power users
Support additional tasks and projects as requested by the manager
Requirements
University degree at bachelor’s or master’s level
One to two years of experience in a multinational organisation in a similar role
Experience in an administrative role is an advantage
Fluent written and spoken English
Additional language skills are an advantage
Knowledge of JTI Talent Management processes and Learning and Development tools is a plus
Strong customer service mindset
Cross-cultural awareness and sensitivity
Collaborative, open-minded and flexible approach
Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and Microsoft PowerPoint
Knowledge of automation is an advantage
