About the Role:
Do you enjoy working with data, collaborating across teams, and turning insights into action? Are you passionate about creating accurate forecasts that help businesses deliver the right products at the right time? If so, we'd love to hear from you!
As a
Demand Planner
, you'll play a key role in shaping our demand planning strategy, optimizing inventory levels, and ensuring excellent product availability for our customers. You'll work closely with Sales, Marketing, Finance, Supply Chain, and Logistics teams to build reliable forecasts and drive business performance.
You'll become part of a dynamic and collaborative environment where your ideas matter, your expertise makes an impact, and continuous improvement is part of our everyday way of working. This is a great opportunity to develop your planning expertise while contributing directly to business success and customer satisfaction.
What you'll do:
Create and maintain short- and long-term demand forecasts across multiple products, customers, and sales channels.
Partner with Sales, Product LIne, Marketing, and Finance teams to develop a collaborative and consensus-driven demand plan.
Lead and facilitate cross-collaboration with supply chain, logistic and demand planning to ensure forecast plans feasibility
Work closely with Supply Chain, Logistics, and Planning teams to ensure forecast feasibility and smooth execution.
Actively participate in the monthly S&OP process and contribute to business planning decisions.
Identify opportunities to improve forecast accuracy and implement effective planning solutions.
Proactively communicate risks, trends, and demand-related issues to key stakeholders.
Monitor supply, inventory, and product availability, taking corrective actions to achieve stock and service level targets.
Support product lifecycle planning, including new product launches, phase-ins, and phase-outs, in collaboration with internal and external partners.
Prepare, analyze, and maintain demand planning reports and performance metrics.
