The chemical and natural resources industries are undergoing a fundamental transformation as sustainability, circular economy principles, and digitalization reshape their future – creating both significant challenges related to regulatory pressure, cost competitiveness, and operational complexity, while also creating substantial opportunities for innovation, efficiency improvements, and sustainable growth. Across both industries, we partner with clients to strengthen operational resilience, enable responsible production, drive operational excellence, and realize value through digitalization, advanced analytics, and end‑to‑end value chain optimization.
We seek experienced professionals with strong communication skills—fluent in English and preferably at least one additional language—and the ability to translate complex topics into clear, actionable insights. Our success is built on trusted client relationships and close collaboration with our global delivery centers to deliver measurable, transformative outcomes.
THE WORK:
No two days at Accenture are alike, but your typical day may include:
Work on innovative initiatives that help Chemicals and Natural Resources clients transform, grow, and adapt in an increasingly digital world.
Translate complex business requirements into intelligent, innovative, and harmonized solutions that drive transformation across Chemicals and Natural Resources clients.
Collaborate within multidisciplinary, globally distributed teams, seamlessly combining business insight with technology expertise.
Manage and grow client relationships by delivering deep industry insights across Chemicals and Natural Resources, including Metals, Mining, Forest Products, and Building Materials.
Build new consulting capabilities by contributing to digital transformation and analytics initiatives, supporting client proposals, and maintaining strong, up‑to‑date industry knowledge.
Working flexibly — primarily remotely within the Intelligent Consulting Hub Europe (ICH) model, but also ready to travel and collaborate onsite with clients across Europe and beyond when project needs require.
HERE’S WHAT YOU’LL NEED:
Background and/or experience in the Chemicals and Natural Resources industries. Solid understanding of end-to-end value chains—from commercial functions through supply chain operations.
Experience working in core business functions and/or ERP platforms (such as SAP S/4HANA, SAP ECC, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, Oracle ERP Cloud), CRM platforms such as Salesforce.
Experience working in a multinational environment, collaborating with diverse, cross-functional and cross-cultural teams.
Hands‑on experience using digital technologies such as IoT, cloud platforms, analytics, and data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI).
Highly driven and resilient, with the ability to perform under pressure, embrace challenges, and deliver results; excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
BONUS POINTS IF YOU HAVE:
Experience with Jira, Confluence, LeanKit, Planbox, Python
Knowledge of German, Spanish or French.
Hands‑on experience delivering large‑scale ERP programs within Chemicals or Natural Resources Industries, covering key implementation phases including process blueprinting, system configuration, system and user acceptance testing (SIT/UAT), data migration, cutover planning, and go‑live support.
