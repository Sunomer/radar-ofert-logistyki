About Mettler Toledo
METTLER TOLEDO is a leading global supplier of precision instruments and services. We are recognized as an innovation leader and our solutions are critical in key R&D, quality control, and manufacturing processes for customers in a wide range of industries including life sciences, food, and chemicals. We operate throughout the world with a culture shaped by innovation and international flair.
Our Opening and Your Responsibilities
SCM Logistics Business Analyst is responsible for the process areas of SCM Logistics. 🚚📦
This is a key contact person for key users and local Management during the course of the project and a change agent for the implementation of our template processes and ensures the successful outcome of the projects.
Your tasks:
* Maintaining and protecting the SAP-SCM template through the global roll-ins, parallel and subsequent projects.
* Interfaces Improving of the Process Development worldwide and harmonization of the template.
* Translate Business Processes into the System and providing process and template guidance.
* Evaluate new identified business processes in co-ordination with the business responsible.
* Work closely with the central SAP Solution Architects and Product Owners within these areas.
* Specifying, designing, and testing new or changed functionalities.
* Creating and maintaining high quality process documentation, requirement definitions, training materials and test cases to help assure global implementation continuity.
What You Need to Succeed
* Proven experience in business or business transformation programs in Logistics, Warehousing and Transportation areas.
* Solid background in Logistics, Warehousing, Transportation processes in SAP (preferably).
* Very good English knowledge (written and spoken).
* Self-driven team player with a result-oriented and positive attitude.
* Analytical skills with excellent attention to detail.
* Advanced communication and presentation skills.
* Ability to work in a fast-paced and global environment (cultural awareness).
* Experience with SAFe methodology and / or SCRUM is a plus.
