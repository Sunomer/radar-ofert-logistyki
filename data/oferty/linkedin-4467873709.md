The Senior Service Delivery Manager is responsible for driving delivery excellence, service management, governance, and operational execution across the Supply Chain Technologies portfolio. Acting as a trusted partner to the Director, the role ensures successful delivery of projects, services, customer implementations, vendor engagements, and strategic initiatives across IMS, Lead Logistics, SCO, and related technology platforms.
What you'll do:
Lead delivery governance across projects, services, implementations, and transformation initiatives
Drive portfolio planning, reporting, risk management, resource planning, capacity management, and financial oversight
Coordinate global cross-functional teams across business, IT, infrastructure, operations, customers, and vendors
Ensure service performance, operational stability, continuous improvement, and adherence to SLA/KPI targets
Manage strategic vendors, contracts, licensing, and service provider performance
Support customer onboarding, migrations, operational readiness, and business transformation initiatives
Act as a senior escalation point for delivery, service, and operational issues
Prepare executive reporting and provide leadership support to the Director
Ensure compliance with IT governance, security, infrastructure, audit, and regulatory requirements
What we're looking for:
8+ years of experience in IT service delivery, program management, technology operations, or supply chain technologies
Proven experience leading global, cross-functional teams and managing complex multi-vendor environments
Strong experience in service management, portfolio governance, resource planning, capacity management, and financial management
Experience managing customer implementations and technology-enabled business transformations
Strong understanding of enterprise applications, infrastructure services, cloud technologies, and IT operational processes
Experience working within regulated and compliance-driven environments
Healthcare, pharmaceutical, life sciences, or other regulated industry experience preferred
ITIL, PMP, PRINCE2, Agile, or equivalent certifications preferred
Strong leadership, communication, and stakeholder management skills
Structured, analytical, and results-oriented
Strong problem-solving and decision-making capabilities
Able to operate effectively in a global, matrix organization
