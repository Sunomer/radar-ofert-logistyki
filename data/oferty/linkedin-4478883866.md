Your IMPACT
The Commercial Logistic Experience Specialist enhances the customer delivery experience through proactive and reactive logistics management. You will analyze open order books, address delivery issues, coordinate with customers and internal stakeholders, and improve logistics processes. Working closely with Sales, Customer Satisfaction and Quality, Master Data, Supply Chain and Customer Care teams, you will help ensure accurate information, smooth operations and high customer satisfaction.
Your Responsibilities
manage strategic customer relationships through regular delivery reviews, proactive communication and structured follow-up on delivery issues, critical order lines and escalations,
analyze weekly open order books, identify risks and issues, perform sanity checks, and share accurate customer files and reports,
act as the Single Point of Contact for logistics escalations in the Nordics and coordinate taskforce and CMI activities together with Customer Satisfaction and Quality teams,
support Sales and Customer Care teams with urgent escalations, availability questions, order status updates, priority requests and other logistics-related inquiries,
ensure data quality and accuracy in the E2E Control Tower, report issues, support improvements and integrate relevant updates into the OPM tool,
prepare and deliver training on logistics, SAP functionalities, tools and best practices, and support the onboarding and knowledge development of new agents,
participate in user acceptance testing and implementation activities across C2, BFO/CRM, E2E Control Tower and SAP,
drive continuous improvement and lean initiatives, including collaboration with Master Data on product roadmaps, phase-outs and potential RDC-related issues,
participate in internal projects and operational forums, approve Fast Lane and SOS requests where applicable, and provide clear updates on logistics initiatives.
Your Profile:
Bachelor's degree in Logistics, Supply Chain Management, Business Administration or a related field,
Experience in logistics, supply chain management, order management or a comparable customer-facing role,
proficiency in SAP and experience with logistics management, CRM or analytics tools,
strong analytical skills, attention to detail and confidence working with open-order data and operational reports,
excellent communication and stakeholder-management skills, with a strong customer focus,
ability to work independently, manage several priorities and collaborate effectively across teams and countries,
experience in training, facilitation, continuous improvement or project work is an advantage.
Key competencies:
proactive problem solving,
strong customer focus and relationship management,
ability to prioritize and coordinate multiple topics,
continuous improvement and growth mindset,
effective project coordination and cross-functional collaboration.
Languages:
Fluency in English, both written and spoken. A Nordic language is an advantage.
