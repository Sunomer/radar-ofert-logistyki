Senior Logistics Planning Consultant / Senior Logistics & Automation Consultant (d, f, m) (Warehouse Automation)
Our client is an international supply chain and logistics consultancy, advising companies worldwide from strategy through to hands-on implementation. After a strong run of growth, they are expanding their team and looking for an experienced professional to join them.
The role
Take a lead role in complex logistics planning and implementation projects for national and international clients
Create and evaluate layout alternatives, design material flows and define efficient logistics and production processes, factoring in automation and digitalisation potential
Develop detailed functional and system specifications for warehouse automation technology
Support tendering processes for logistics equipment and systems, working closely with customers and suppliers
Implement concepts on site at client locations
Own the delivery of sub-projects and workstreams
Contribute to internal optimisation projects and help build the team's knowledge base
About you
Master's degree in logistics, supply chain management, industrial engineering or a comparable field
Several years of experience in logistics planning, logistics engineering or process automation, with independent management of complex projects such as distribution centre builds or production environments
Strong analytical and conceptual skills, with the ability to structure and clearly communicate complex topics
Very good MS Office skills and a solid technical understanding of logistics systems and automation (CAD experience is a plus)
Confident English; German is an advantage
Professional, persuasive presence with clients
Willingness to travel; international project experience is ideal
What's on offer
Stable, permanent position with a well-established international employer
International environment with fast career progression
Individual training budget plus internal training programmes
Competitive base salary, attractive bonus scheme and strong benefits package
Flexible working, including remote options
