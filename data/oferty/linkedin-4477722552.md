Position Overview
UZ Cargo Poland Sp. z o.o. is seeking an experienced professional for the position of
Intermodal Logistics Manager (Sales & Operations)
to drive business development and oversee the operational management of international intermodal transportation services.
The position involves responsibility for expanding the company's client portfolio, developing and implementing transport and logistics solutions, coordinating transportation operations, ensuring operational profitability, and fulfilling contractual obligations.
The primary geographical focus of the role covers the transport and logistics markets of Poland, Ukraine, and other European Union countries.
Key Responsibilities
Sales & Business Development
Develop and expand sales of international rail freight and intermodal transportation services.
Identify and acquire new clients while maintaining and strengthening relationships with existing customers.
Conduct commercial negotiations and agree on terms and conditions of cooperation.
Prepare commercial quotations, calculate transportation costs, and assess the profitability of transport operations.
Develop tailored transport and logistics solutions based on customer requirements.
Analyse market conditions, identify business opportunities, and explore potential areas for business development.
Operations Management
Organise and coordinate international intermodal freight transportation.
Plan transportation routes and ensure effective coordination among all parties involved in the transport process.
Establish and manage cooperation with railway undertakings, terminal operators, and intermodal transport operators.
Monitor the execution of transport orders, ensuring compliance with delivery schedules and contractual obligations.
Ensure operational efficiency and cost-effectiveness of transportation services.
Resolve operational issues arising during the execution of transport orders.
Candidate Requirements
Proven professional experience in international rail freight or intermodal transportation.
Experience in sales of transport and logistics services, including commercial negotiations with corporate clients.
Practical knowledge of international freight transportation, pricing structures, and transport logistics.
Strong understanding of the transport and logistics markets in Poland and other European Union countries.
Experience working with railway undertakings, intermodal operators, and terminal operators.
Skills in commercial planning, operational management, and financial performance monitoring.
Professional working proficiency in Polish, English, and Ukrainian.
Preferred Qualifications
Experience in developing new transport corridors, organising container transportation, and implementing international logistics projects will be considered an advantage.
Employment Conditions
Full-time employment.
Opportunity to contribute to the development of the intermodal logistics business of UZ Cargo Poland Sp. z o.o.
Cooperation with international clients and business partners across Poland and the European Union.
Employment terms and remuneration will be discussed individually during the recruitment process.
