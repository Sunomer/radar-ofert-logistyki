## Treść ogłoszenia

Are you interested in being part of an innovative team that supports Westinghouse's mission to provide clean energy solutions? At Westinghouse, we recognize that our employees are our most valuable asset and we seek to identify, attract and recruit the most qualified talent while recognizing and encouraging the value of diversity in the global workplace.

As a Lead Procurement Engineer you will manage project, product and service deliveries for Westinghouse, for new AP1000 plants in Poland and other European locations. This requires successful procurement, delivery and turnover of equipment that supports the APX portfolio. This includes managing activities during the pre-PO phase, PO placement phase, PO execution phase, and final delivery and turnover of all equipment to the customer. The Lead Procurement Engineer is responsible for all phases of the procurement including project management, and achievement of financial and schedule milestones. You will report to Manager of Auxiliary Equipment area.

The team works in a hybrid model, with two days per week based at the office.

Responsibilities

 Drive localization/regionalization efforts to identify and develop suppliers locally in Poland to support new AP1000 projects in Poland and in other European countries. 
 Develop and implement the appropriate program management model, complementary to global execution model(s) to profitably promote Westinghouse's participation and leadership in the APX Supply Chain Portfolio. 
 Forecast program performance, including identification of risk areas and development of mitigation strategies to ensure overall program meets safety, quality, and financial goals. 
 Be a trusted partner to the Delivery Stream project procurement leader for each major customer project. 
 Provide strategic direction and execution plans focused on delivery and implementation of APX Supply Chain products that meets/exceeds incremental profitable growth for Westinghouse. 
 Lead and provide oversight to Delivery Stream teams across all Westinghouse, supplier, and customer sites. 
 Guide and lead decision-making, strategy development, and implementation for procurement of components and equipment. 
 Work with GSCS partners to develop and provide strategic sourcing strategies and execution plans focused on successful delivery and implementation of products that meet or exceed incremental profitable growth for Westinghouse. 
 Oversee training and mentor new hires and existing employees working on customer projects for the first time. Serve as primary project interface for project management, status, and responsibility for delivery of components and equipment within Westinghouse scope of supply. 
 Support development of Delivery Stream project management staffing forecast. 
 Participate in bid opportunities/meetings. Once projects are secured, serves as primary project interface, taking responsibility for status and delivering components and equipment within Westinghouse scope of supply. 
 Collaborate with design engineering and quality to develop appropriate document submittal and witness and hold point (W&HP) requirements. 
 Provide supplier strategy and procurement planning by driving supplier Identification and down selection, technical evaluations, quality audits and request for quotation package creations 

Qualifications

 Bachelor's degree required, Engineering Discipline Required 
 10+ years of relevant experience; at least 5 years in a leadership role of project delivery to major technical design projects 
 In-depth manufacturing, financial, and economic experience, understanding of Energy Systems Product Lines 
 Ability to build relationships at executive level internally with WEC and with customers/utilities. 
 Proven major program leadership experience 
 Experience in written and verbal communication to executives 
 Strong organizational skills, with the ability to multi-task and prioritize work.
