Dover Imaging & Identification (DII) is a segment of Dover Corporation that includes the following brands: Markem-Imaje, Blue Bite, Solaris Laser, Systech, MS Printing Solutions and JK Group. We design, manufacture and deliver industrial equipment, consumables, software and services solutions for the marking & coding, consumer engagement, product traceability, authentication, and digital textile printing markets.
With more than 40 offices and over 3,000 employees worldwide, DII has a presence in +28 countries and offers numerous job opportunities globally. If you're looking for an international, multidisciplinary and collaborative environment that offers a world of opportunities, come join us at DII.
The Role
In this role you’ll be working as part of our
Supply Chain
organization, ensuring the efficient, safe and timely operation of warehouse, logistics and customer service activities, including receiving, storage, inventory control and material flow, supply of production lines and Repair Centers, and execution of customer shipments.
What You Will Do
Lead and develop the Warehouse and Logistics team, ensuring effective organization, staffing, performance and operational continuity
Manage end-to-end warehouse operations, including receiving, storage, picking, issuing and control of materials
Optimize warehouse capacity, infrastructure and material flows, including the Kardex automated storage system and high-bay racking
Ensure inventory accuracy and data integrity, including stock control, cycle counting, annual inventory and accurate SAP transactions
Ensure timely and continuous material supply to Production and Repair Centers, managing internal material flows and the Milk Run replenishment system
Manage cooperation with the external warehouse, ensuring material availability, accurate inventory records and efficient transfers between locations
Oversee shipping operations for products, spare parts and other shipments, ensuring timely execution and compliance with customer, customs and transport requirements
Supervise Logistics and Customer Service activities, ensuring effective coordination with Sales, Production, Planning, Procurement and Supply Chain
Own and improve logistics and warehouse KPIs, identify root causes of issues and implement corrective and preventive actions to improve performance
Drive continuous improvement and Lean initiatives, including Kaizen, 5S and waste elimination, to improve efficiency, quality, reliability and cost-effectiveness of end-to-end material flow
What You Need To Have
Minimum 10 years of experience working in logistics, with a minimum of 5 years as a manager for mid-size team (20+)
Experience in transportation and import/export process
Experience with Lean methodologies, such as Six Sigma, Kaizen and Value Stream Mapping,...
Strong analytical skills and advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel
Experience of working with SAP
Good English skills (at least B2 level)
