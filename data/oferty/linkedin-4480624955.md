Assistant Manager — AUG Medical
Location: Warsaw, Poland
AUG Medical is looking for an organized and proactive Assistant Manager to support daily sales, administration and warehouse operations. This role suits someone with previous sales experience who enjoys working with customers, coordinating orders and keeping business operations running smoothly.
Key Responsibilities:
- Handle customer enquiries, prepare quotations and follow up on sales opportunities.
- Process orders and maintain accurate customer, sales and inventory records in ERP and CRM systems.
- Coordinate stock receiving, order preparation and dispatch.
- Prepare invoices, maintain documentation and support daily administrative tasks.
- Communicate with customers, suppliers and colleagues.
- Assist management with day-to-day operations and follow up on pending tasks.
Requirements:
- Previous sales experience is essential.
- Experience with administration and practical use of ERP/CRM systems.
- Basic experience in warehouse or inventory operations.
- Fluent Polish and good spoken and written English.
- Good organizational skills, attention to detail and a responsible approach to work.
- ⁠Experience with medical equipment, medical brands or healthcare distribution is an advantage, but is not required.
Training:
Product and internal process training will be provided.
