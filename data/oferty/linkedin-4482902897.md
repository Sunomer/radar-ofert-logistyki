About the role
Join Xiaomi Poland Home Appliance team, own end-to-end supply chain & delivery operation for white goods (refrigerator, washing machine, air conditioner). You will drive sales-supply-production alignment, inventory governance, warehouse management, logistics vendor performance and SOP standardization locally in Poland. Chinese language capability is a strong plus for cross-region alignment with Xiaomi HQ.
Key Responsibilities
Sales & Supply Alignment (PSI Collaboration)
Align production, sales and delivery plan with GTM, supply planning and line-haul logistics teams; run M+3 rolling forecast and weekly sync rhythm to match white goods sales plan with delivery schedule and hit business targets.
Implement 3-level PSI alignment process, build national inventory alert & stock transfer decision mechanism, mitigate overstock risk caused by push-based allocation.
Coordinate cross-functional resources including procurement, customs, logistics and finance to close the end-to-end delivery loop.
Warehouse & Inventory Management
Build dedicated white goods inventory rules: ageing alert, DOS monitoring and inventory block mechanism; target inventory turnover ≤45 days.
Define stocktaking standard for white goods (monthly full stocktake + ad-hoc spot check), set up automatic discrepancy alert for book-to-physical consistency.
Establish standard SOP for return, exchange and rework; reduce damage rate and residual loss in reverse logistics.
Delivery Capability & Standardization
Develop full-cycle delivery SOP covering warehousing, line haul, customs clearance, last-mile delivery, acceptance and reverse flow for white goods.
Manage logistics vendors, track KPI/SLA and drive continuous service improvement; conduct logistics cost analysis, identify cost-saving opportunities and lead optimization projects.
Requirements
Bachelor’s degree or above, major in Logistics, Supply Chain or related field preferred.
3–5 years of experience in logistics, supply chain or delivery operation; experience in large home appliances / bulky goods or consumer electronics is highly preferred.
Familiar with Poland local logistics ecosystem including customs clearance, warehousing and last-mile distribution; proven white goods logistics operation experience is a big plus.
Strong capability in data analysis, logistics vendor management, cost control and cross-functional project driving.
Excellent cross-border & cross-department communication and problem-solving skills.
Business-level English is mandatory; Chinese language capability is a strong plus.
