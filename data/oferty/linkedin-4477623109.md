About The Role
Dive into the dynamic world of supply chain management as a Supply Planner at Reckitt. This is your chance to apply your analytical skills and business accumen to make impactful decisions that drive our brand forward. Be at the core of ensuring our products not only meet but exceed customer expectations. Your role is essential to the smooth delivery of our leading consumer products, and you'll have the opportunity to tackle meaningful projects that improve our processes and efficiency.
Your responsibilities
Manages product availability and highlights service risks.
Manages inventory levels (NWC) and reviews and sets up TD's leading to appropriate safety stock.
Supports Supply Review and contributes to the IBP process by providing supply planning inputs, risk assessment and inventory recommendations.
Drives SLOB (slow-moving and obsolete) reviews.
Leads root causing and OOS/Distress process and communication to Commercial.
Updates OOS distress portion of customer reporting (e.g. Rossmann).
Leads monthly provision and SLOB process.
Leads and executes allocation process based on directions from DP/commercial.
Manages factory write-offs and FG scrap portion of Supply Chain Losses.
Manages product changeovers and discontinuation, including communication towards factories and DP.
Attends projects meetings with NPI and Marketing teams.
Reviews short-term requests from KAMs.
Supports decomplexity reviews.
The experience we're looking for
2-3 years of previous experience in Supply Planning or a similar Supply Chain role.
Understanding of supply chain flow between manufacturing sites and market.
Structured approach to work and good follow-up discipline to manage a portfolio of 150+ SKUs.
Strong ability to navigate a dynamic environment and manage portfolio transitions as well as short-term business requests.
Ability to effectively prioritize and manage multiple requests simultaneously.
Strong relationship-building skills and ability to collaborate across functions.
Working knowledge of MS Excel and planning systems such as Kinaxis or SAP.
Very good command of English, enabling effective communication in an international environment.
FMCG experience is a plus but not mandatory.
The skills for success
Supply Chain Management, Business Partnership, Collaboration and partnership building, Relationship Management, Business accumen, Productivity management, Improve business processes, Advanced Analytics, Data Analytics, Supply Chain Planning, Logistics Management.
