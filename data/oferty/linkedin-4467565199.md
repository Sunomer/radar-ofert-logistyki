Role Overview
Join a large-scale SAP S/4HANA rollout project for a leading manufacturing client as a Senior SAP SD & TM Consultant. We are looking for two experienced professionals who want to play a key role in shaping and delivering a global transformation program.
Willingness to travel (Min 50%. 10-12 weeks of travel over 6-9 months of rollout duration)
Requirements
At least 5 years of SAP project experience with in-depth knowledge of SAP Sales and Distribution and SAP Transportation Management modules, including order-to-cash, transportation planning, freight execution, freight settlement, carrier management, and integration processes.
Experience in at least 1 SAP E2E implementation over the entire project life cycle, including SAP SD and/or SAP TM scope.
Experience in at least 1 SAP S/4HANA rollout with involvement in Fit-to-Template / Explore phases and transportation-related business processes.
Providing sound SAP SD & TM solutions in the S/4HANA environment based on the customer’s specific business requirements.
Strong understanding of SAP SD and SAP TM integration, ensuring efficient execution of sales, logistics, and transportation processes.
