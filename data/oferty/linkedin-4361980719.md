The role of Continuous Improvement Team Leader is to act as a business partner who works closely with Top and Middle Management, understand business priorities, proposes and implements improvement projects/initiatives which support successful achievement of DSV GBS strategic goals and objectives.
Tasks and Responsibilities include:
Cooperation with GBS units and teams and other process stakeholders,
Track and report continuous improvement project
Assist process transition to operations and participate in SLA review,
Support the establishment and delivery of an operational excellence/ standard work program for GBS
Designing, preparing and conducting continuous improvement methodologies training sessions
Support in defining new KPIs for services provided by GBS and support in their implementation,
Develop, implement and maintain reporting framework for business KPI metrics,
Development, implementation and maintenance of the process maturity model of the organization (Maturity Matrix Model),
Lead and Support the Lean QMS / Continuous Improvement team and others
Compliance with internal regulations, procedures, law
