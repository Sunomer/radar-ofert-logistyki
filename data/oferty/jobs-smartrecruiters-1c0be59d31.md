Job Description
What does it feel like to be an
IT Operations Platforms Specialist
in Netcompany?
As an
IT Operations Platforms Specialist
, you will:
Support the architecture and design of platform solutions, including databases, middleware, virtualisation and container platforms.
Implement and manage platform maintenance routines, schedules and operational procedures.
Implement and operate orchestration and automation tools to maintain, optimise and extend platform solutions.
Implement and operate platform management, monitoring and reporting tools to ensure performance, availability and reliability.
Troubleshoot, diagnose and resolve platform-related incidents and technical issues, including performance analysis and tuning.
Implement and operate shared platform services such as OpenShift, Splunk and Oracle Enterprise Manager, including backup and restore solutions.
Monitor platform health and contribute to continuous improvement, optimisation and operational efficiency.
Produce and maintain technical documentation, operational procedures and support documentation, collaborating with architecture, development, infrastructure and operations teams.
Qualifications
What would make you a fit for the role:
University degree in Computer Science, IT, Engineering or a related field.
At least 10 years of relevant professional experience in IT platform operations, infrastructure or systems administration.
Hands-on experience with databases, middleware, virtualization and container platforms.
Experience with OpenShift/Kubernetes, Splunk, Oracle Enterprise Manager or similar enterprise platforms.
Strong knowledge of automation, orchestration, monitoring and platform management tools.
Experience with backup, restore, performance tuning and troubleshooting of enterprise platforms.
Strong analytical, problem-solving and documentation skills, with a focus on reliability and continuous improvement.
Strong communication skills in English; knowledge of French is an advantage.
Ability to collaborate effectively with architecture, development, infrastructure and operations teams.
