Job Description
Algoteque is preparing to support major industrialization and production launch initiatives. We are seeking experienced Manufacturing & Process Engineers to help transform innovative designs into scalable, efficient production realities.
Responsibilities
Leading industrialization phases, production launches, and NPI (New Product Introduction).
Defining, implementing, and continuously improving manufacturing processes.
Managing industrial transfers and resolving complex production issues.

Proven experience in manufacturing environments such as assembly, injection molding, machining, or automated lines.
Strong practical knowledge of process methodologies: PFMEA, Lean Manufacturing, and root cause analysis.
Ability to act as a bridge between R&D, quality, and production teams.
Communicative English.
