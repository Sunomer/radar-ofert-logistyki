Job Description
Expect more. Connect more. Be more at Diebold Nixdorf. Our teams automate, digitize, and transform the way more than 75 million people around the globe bank and shop in this hyper-connected, consumer-centric world. Join us in connecting people to commerce in this vital, rewarding role.
Responsibilities
As a
Junior Materials Management Specialist at our Repair Center in Nadarzyn
, you will support the timely availability and delivery of materials, equipment, and supplies required to maintain smooth business and repair operations. You will work closely with supply chain, warehouse, repair, and other operational teams to monitor material availability, inventory levels, and deliveries, helping to identify and resolve potential issues.
This is a great opportunity for someone at the beginning of their career who is interested in developing their knowledge of
materials management, inventory planning, and supply chain operations
in an international environment.
Responsibilities
Supporting the monitoring of material availability and inventory levels to meet operational needs.
Assisting with maintaining appropriate stock levels and identifying potential shortages or excess inventory.
Supporting the preparation and handling of excess and obsolete materials.
Assisting with scheduling and coordinating material and manufacturing-related activities.
Supporting the implementation and monitoring of approved material and production schedules.
Monitoring material receipts and disbursements and ensuring that inventory movements are accurately recorded.
Working with internal teams to identify and resolve issues related to material availability, shortages, deliveries, and inventory.
Preparing basic reports and analyses related to materials and inventory.
Supporting the preparation of materials-related data for budgeting and planning activities.
Developing knowledge of materials management processes, systems, and supply chain best practices.
Qualifications
Education in Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Business, Economics, or a related field is an advantage, but relevant work experience will also be considered.
Previous experience in logistics, supply chain, warehouse, materials management, or a similar area is an advantage, but we are also open to candidates at the beginning of their career.
Good organizational and analytical skills, with attention to detail.
Ability to work with different internal teams and communicate effectively.
Proactive approach and willingness to learn and develop within the Materials Management / Supply Chain area.
Basic knowledge of inventory or supply chain processes is an advantage.
Good business English skills, both written and spoken.
Ability to work in hybrid mode, with a 4 days per week from the Repair Center in Nadarzyn.
