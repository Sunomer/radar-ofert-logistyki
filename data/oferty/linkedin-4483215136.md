About the Opportunity
Global Life Science Hub is partnering with an established pharmaceutical manufacturer in Poland to recruit an experienced
Lead Process Engineer
to oversee process engineering activities within its Oral Solid Dosage (OSD) manufacturing operations.
This is an opportunity to take ownership of a small engineering team, lead complex technology transfer projects and drive technical improvements across pharmaceutical manufacturing operations.
The organisation is undergoing significant expansion, with continued investment in manufacturing infrastructure, production capacity and technical capabilities.
Key Responsibilities
Lead and manage a team of five Process Engineers (three on-site and two remote).
Oversee technology transfer projects, managing technical activities from planning through to manufacturing implementation.
Provide technical leadership across oral solid dosage manufacturing processes.
Drive process optimisation, troubleshooting and continuous improvement initiatives.
Support manufacturing operations with technical investigations and process-related challenges.
Coordinate cross-functional activities involving Production, Quality, Engineering and other technical departments.
Manage project timelines, technical documentation and deliverables associated with technology transfers.
Support process scale-up, manufacturing improvements and GMP compliance.
Requirements
Approximately 5+ years of experience in pharmaceutical process engineering, MSAT or a related technical manufacturing function.
Strong technical background in oral solid dosage manufacturing, including tablets, capsules or similar products.
Proven experience managing pharmaceutical technology transfer projects.
Previous team leadership or supervisory experience, with the ability to manage and develop Process Engineers.
Good understanding of GMP manufacturing environments.
Strong technical problem-solving, project management and communication skills.
Fluent Polish language skills.
Willingness to work fully on-site
