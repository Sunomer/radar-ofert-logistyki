The SAP Complex Release Coordinator is responsible for coordinating complex, cross-project SAP releases within Finance IT, where multiple projects, SAP applications, integrations, transports, and go-live activities have dependencies or overlapping timelines. The role is ideal for an experienced SAP professional who wants to leverage their SAP expertise in a broader coordination capacity, focusing on release management, change coordination, and cross-project planning rather than day-to-day defect resolution or enhancement delivery. Working across solutions including SAP S/4HANA, MDG, BW/4HANA, SAC, GRDC, and related integration platforms. Acting as a central coordination point, the SAP Complex Release Coordinator works closely with project teams, architects, technical experts, and business stakeholders to ensure successful and well-controlled releases.
What you'll do:
Identify and coordinate complex or cross-project SAP release windows
Maintain a consolidated view of projects, release scope, transports, integrations, dependencies and key milestones
Identify, document and actively follow up on technical and cross-project dependencies
Coordinate dependency discussions between SAP S/4HANA, MDG, BW/4HANA, SAC, GRDC and related systems
Identify release conflicts, sequencing constraints and potential risks and coordinate their resolution
Coordinate integrated cut-over plans, including sequencing, timing, dependencies, downtime and support requirements
Track release readiness across projects, including testing, approvals, CHG status, transport readiness and rollback plans
Ensure unresolved dependencies and risks are escalated to the appropriate technical or project owner
Support go/no-go alignment and coordination during critical or weekend releases
Capture actions, decisions, risks and lessons learned
Support Release Management with complete and timely information for complex releases
Maintain release calendars, dependency trackers, readiness overviews and coordination templates
Ensure alignment with SAP Release Management and Finance IT processes
Identify recurring issues and contribute to continuous improvement of release coordination
What we're looking for:
Several years of proven experience working with complex SAP landscapes and SAP project deliveries
Experience in SAP Release Management, Release Coordination, Delivery Coordination or a similar role is a plus
Strong understanding of SAP release and transport processes
Practical experience with SAP environments such as: SAP S/4HANA, MDG, BW/4HANA, SAC, GRDC and related integrations
Experience identifying and managing technical dependencies across SAP projects and systems
Good understanding of the SAP delivery lifecycle from build and testing through release, go-live and hypercare
Ability to understand technical discussions, challenge assumptions and involve the right technical experts when required
Strong stakeholder management and communication skills
Experience with ServiceNow CHG and/or SAP release management tools such as ChaRM is an advantage
Structured, detail-oriented, proactive and pragmatic approach to managing complex and time-critical situations
Analytical, structured and technically curious
Able to challenge and escalate when required while maintaining good relationships
Able to balance governance and process with pragmatic business delivery
Persistent in following up on actions and unresolved dependencies
