The Position
Senior Partner Systems Delivery Expert - EPIE
The Opportunity
As a Senior Partner Systems Delivery Expert, you'll lead the technical delivery of mission-critical enterprise integrations. You'll own complex system implementations end-to-end, manage technical dependencies across partners, and drive flawless execution at scale.
You'll define integration roadmaps, coordinate technical workstreams, and make critical decisions on system architecture and delivery approaches. This is hands-on technical leadership where execution excellence matters.
What You'll Do
Technical Management: Own end-to-end delivery of complex system integrations
Define technical roadmaps and coordinate dependencies across teams and vendors
Manage timelines, milestones, and resource allocation
Drive flawless execution of builds, data mappings, and transaction flows
Integration Architecture & Design
Lead technical design of interface testing frameworks and data synchronization solutions
Evaluate and select technical approaches (APIs, middleware, integration platforms)
Manage technical scope aligned with immediate and long-term needs
Ensure architectural decisions support system resilience
Dependency & Risk Management
Orchestrate complex deliveries with multiple dependencies and data flows
Identify technical risks early and surface mitigation strategies
Manage integration points across disparate systems
Resolve technical conflicts between platforms and partners
Technical Leadership
Lead and coordinate technical workstreams across internal teams, vendors, and partners
Mentor technical staff on complex integration challenges
Ensure consistent execution of technical standards
Guide teams through sophisticated problem-solving
System Readiness & Quality
Establish rigorous interface testing and data validation frameworks
Own system readiness activities and end-to-end validation
Troubleshoot transaction synchronization issues
Ensure production-ready integrations with strict data integrity
Technical Stakeholder Alignment
Lead technical discussions with architects and system owners
Translate requirements into clear delivery plans
Communicate risks and mitigation strategies
Maintain alignment across technical organizations
Who You Are
Essential Background
Proven expertise leading the technical delivery of large-scale, complex system integrations and implementations
Extensive hands-on experience managing integration technical workstreams, coordinating across multiple technical teams, and owning delivery outcomes
Deep technical knowledge of enterprise system landscapes, data architectures, integration technologies, and implementation best practices
Demonstrated success orchestrating end-to-end technical implementations with multiple dependencies and partners
SAP & ERP Experience Required
Hands-on SAP and ERP platform experience is essential for this role. If you have a strong background as a Functional Business Analyst, SAP Developer, or Project Manager with proven success delivering enterprise system implementations and managing technical integration workstreams, this position could be an exceptional fit for you
Core Competencies
Advanced technical depth: In-depth understanding of enterprise architectures, data flows, integration platforms, API design, middleware solutions, and implementation methodologies
Technical execution excellence: Lead the design, build, and testing of complex integrations; manage technical scope and dependencies; drive quality and on-time delivery
Complex problem-solving: Analyze multi-system technical issues, troubleshoot data synchronization problems, and evaluate strategic technical solutions
Technical team leadership: Coordinate and mentor technical teams across organizations; drive consistent execution of technical standards
Clear technical communication: Translate technical details into actionable delivery plans; communicate risks, dependencies, and solutions to both technical and business stakeholders
