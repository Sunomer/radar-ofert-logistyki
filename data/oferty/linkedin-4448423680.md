TMS APPLICATION SPECIALIST
Location: Poland / Yusen Logistics
The Company
Yusen Logistics is working to become the world’s preferred supply chain logistics company. Our complete offer is designed to forge better connections between businesses, customers and communities – through innovative supply chain management, freight forwarding, warehousing and distribution services. As a company, we’re dedicated to a culture of continuous improvement, ensuring everyone who works with us is committed, connected and creative in making us the world’s preferred choice.
To strengthen our IT organisation within Cluster East, we are looking for a TMS Application Specialist based in Poland. If you understand transport operations, enjoy working with systems and like turning business requirements into practical solutions, this could be a great next step.
Your Role
As TMS Application Specialist, you will be the link between transport operations and IT, supporting our local operations while also contributing to regional and European TMS initiatives.
You will configure and support our Transport Management Systems, translate operational requirements into system solutions and help users get the most out of our applications. Your work can range from troubleshooting and configuration to new implementations, system improvements, testing and migrations.
You will work independently on local requirements while being part of a wider international IT team across Cluster East and Europe.
Key Responsibilities
Configure and support Transport Management Systems based on operational and business requirements
Translate business needs into functional and technical TMS solutions
Troubleshoot incidents and requests and provide effective support to users
Test new functionalities, configuration changes and system upgrades, including support during UAT
Work with TMS vendors and development teams on enhancements and improvements
Support regional and European TMS implementations, projects and migrations
Create reports and analyse TMS data using tools such as SQL, Crystal Reports or Power BI
Support integrations between TMS platforms, customers and partners through EDI or APIs
Maintain technical documentation, business requirements and knowledge base materials
Identify opportunities to continuously improve and digitalise our transport processes
Who You Are
Around 2–5 years of relevant experience, ideally within TMS, transport/logistics applications or IT application support
Experience working with a Transport Management System such as Soloplan CarLo, Blue Yonder TMS, OTM, Transporeon, Manhattan TMS, Cargowise, e2Open or similar
Understanding of transport, logistics or freight forwarding processes
Hands-on technical mindset with experience in application configuration and troubleshooting
Basic knowledge of databases and SQL is a strong advantage
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Service-minded and comfortable supporting business users
Able to work independently while collaborating with international IT and operational teams
Good command of English
University degree is not required; practical experience and technical capability are what matter most
What We Offer
Joining Yusen Logistics gives you the opportunity to engage, drive and grow in an international environment.
A role where transport, technology and digitalisation come together
Opportunity to work on both local and European TMS initiatives
Collaboration with international IT and operational teams
Opportunity to further develop your technical and TMS expertise
Professional development and career growth opportunities
Occasional travel within Europe
Competitive salary and benefits package
