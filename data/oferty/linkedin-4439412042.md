About The Role
The Manager, Procurement – Audits and Risk mgmt. for Indirects and Enterprise risk reporting is responsible for strengthening governance, transparency, and risk resilience across the end-to-end sourcing lifecycle for indirects services and materials. This role ensures procurement activities not only comply with internal policies and external regulations but also contribute to enterprise-wide risk visibility and reporting.
Key Responsibilities
Procurement Audit & Compliance
Design and execute audit programs across indirect materials & services sourcing (e.g., media, non-working media, professional services, office supplies, HR services)
Assess adherence to procurement policies, contracts, and regulatory requirements
Execute second-line assurance activities to assess operational compliance with the framework, identifying gaps and driving corrective actions
Collaborate with Internal Audit and Compliance teams on risk-based audit planning
Risk Management
Develop and maintain a procurement risk framework covering supply, financial, geopolitical, ESG, and operational risks
Monitor supplier risk assessments and ongoing monitoring (financial health, capacity, single-source exposure), in close alignment with the category strategies and TPRM process
Build end-to-end sourcing and contracting process in line with risk framework, tool roadmap and future state goals
Monitor and keep oversight of mitigation strategies including dual sourcing, contract safeguards, and contingency planning
Lead risk reviews for critical suppliers and high-value sourcing decisions
Support the reporting into relevant Supply Risk forms including material preparation and generation of reporting metrics
Enterprise Risk Reporting (Internal & External)
Translate procurement risks into enterprise-level risk insights for senior leadership and Board reporting
Prepare regular risk reports, dashboards, and heat maps for inclusion in enterprise risk reviews
Contribute to quarterly and annual enterprise risk disclosures (e.g., annual reports, regulatory filings)
Ensure procurement risks are accurately reflected in financial and non-financial reporting frameworks
Partner with Finance, Risk, and Compliance teams to align procurement risk metrics with corporate reporting standards
Support external audit and regulatory reviews by providing documentation, evidence, and risk narratives
Drive consistency and transparency in how procurement risks are communicated across regions and business units
Contract & Commercial Governance
Ensure contracts include appropriate risk protections (e.g., liability clauses, price mechanisms, service levels)
Review and audit contracts to identify gaps
Support dispute resolution and claims management with suppliers
Data Analytics & Insights
Use data analytics to identify anomalies, cost leakage, fraud indicators, and compliance gaps
Develop dashboards and KPIs for procurement risk and audit performance
Provide actionable insights to senior stakeholders to improve sourcing strategies
Supplier Governance & ESG
Assess supplier compliance with ESG, ethical sourcing, and sustainability standards
Support responsible sourcing initiatives and regulatory requirements (e.g., modern slavery, conflict minerals)
Monitor supplier improvement plans and monitor corrective actions
Stakeholder Engagement
Partner with Procurement buyers, Finance, Legal, Risk and Operations teams globally
Act as a trusted advisor on procurement risk and governance matters
Deliver training and awareness programs on procurement compliance and risk management
