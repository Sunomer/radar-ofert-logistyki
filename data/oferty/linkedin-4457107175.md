About The Role
We are looking for an analytical and solutions-driven Operations Specialist to use the data provided to form business insights, spot potential for development and recommend changes in the business in order to improve the quality, compliance and efficiency of driver, vehicle and fleet partner registration processes for our ride-hailing operations in Poland.
You will be working closely with the Operations Manager(s) and the Driver Growth team, where your work will have a direct impact on business growth by achieving quality, compliance and vendor performance milestones and key metrics.
Main Tasks And Responsibilities
Collaborating with an external vendor responsible for registering vehicles, drivers and fleet partners, as well as managing audit processes, to ensure compliance, data accuracy and operational efficiency.
Tracking and developing KPIs to evaluate vendor performance, including audit results, quality and error rates, task completion, turnaround times, backlog and SLA performance, as well as preparing dashboards and reports with key insights for stakeholders.
Evaluating audit quality and SOP effectiveness, including audit criteria, error categorisation and sampling methodology, updating procedures for vendor agents in line with market requirements and regulatory changes, and continuously streamlining vehicle, driver and fleet partner registration processes.
Documenting vendor-related processes, with a strong focus on improving the registration experience and satisfaction of drivers and fleet partners.
Developing and recommending data-backed improvement plans, impact estimates and operational strategies. Identifying root causes behind operational and quality issues and recommending corrective actions, process improvements or automation opportunities.
Leading projects and collaborating with cross-functional teams, particularly the Driver Growth team responsible for vendor management, to provide support on regulatory requirements and jointly adapt audit processes and SOPs based on performance data, statistical insights and evolving market needs.
