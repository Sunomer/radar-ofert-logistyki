Production Supervisor – Data Centre Manufacturing
Location:
West Warsaw, Poland
Type:
Full-time, onsite
The Role
We are looking for a
Production Supervisor
to manage the day-to-day operation of a manufacturing facility producing strut and 2-D steel frames for major data centre projects across Europe.
You will be responsible for keeping the
strut machine line running efficiently
, supervising production staff and overseeing the fabrication and preparation of frames ready for shipment.
Key Responsibilities
Manage the day-to-day strut production line
Supervise and allocate production staff
Oversee 2-D frame fabrication and assembly
Monitor production output, quality and deadlines
Read and work from technical drawings
Coordinate materials and production requirements
Ensure H&S and quality standards are maintained
Troubleshoot production issues and improve efficiency
Work closely with warehouse and logistics teams
About You
Previous experience in
production or manufacturing supervision
Experience managing a shop-floor team
Background in metal/steel fabrication or similar
Comfortable working with machinery and technical drawings
Practical, hands-on approach
Strong focus on quality, safety and productivity
No ego – willing to get involved on the shop floor
