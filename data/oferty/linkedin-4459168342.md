We are seeking a talented individual to join our Motor team at Marsh. This role will be based in the Warsaw office. This is a hybrid role that has a requirement of working at least three days a week in the office.
Our department has taken over operations from Marsh Europe in order to allow our colleagues to focus directly on clients. In our daily work, we strive for efficiency, high quality, and outstanding results delivered in a timely manner. Our scope of service includes policy management, claims, procurement, data management, billing, sales support activities, and more. We are at the heart of Marsh’s value proposition for multinational clients, which allows us to stay closely connected to the business and colleagues across Europe.
As a Team Coordinator in the Motor team, you will be responsible for supporting the Team Leader or Ops Manager in a variety of people management and operational activities within the team.
We will count on you to:
Manage a team of administration specialists, including recruiting, motivating, coaching, and developing team members by setting clear goals, managing and prioritizing workloads, and monitoring performance
Plan and develop new procedures within the team’s area of responsibility
Resolve internal client escalations in a timely manner
Implement business-focused solutions within time and/or cost constraints
Prepare management reports
Drive process improvements
Coordinate various projects and team initiatives
Support the Motor team in delivering high-quality operational services
What you need to have:
Coordination skills and experience in people management
Experience in a shared service centre environment, preferably in insurance (Claims, Policy Servicing, Placement, Client Onboarding)
Excellent command of spoken and written English and very good knowledge of French, at minimum B2 level
Strong customer service experience, ideally at least 3 years
Ability to plan resources and track team utilization
Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
Ability to work effectively in a dynamic, international environment
What makes you stand out:
Ability to build strong relationships with colleagues
Open-minded approach and fast learning capability
Strong oral and written communication skills
Why join our team:
We help you be your best through professional development opportunities, interesting work and supportive leaders.
We foster a vibrant and inclusive culture where you can work with talented colleagues to create new solutions and have impact for colleagues, clients and communities.
Our scale enables us to provide a range of career opportunities, as well as benefits and rewards to enhance your well-being.
