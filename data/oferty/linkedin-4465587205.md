We are seeking an
SAP Business Analyst Logistics
to join our SAP team, helping us translate business needs into effective SAP functionalities. In this role you will act as the key liaison between Business Process Engineers, Logistics business stakeholders, and IT. Your primary responsibility will be to ensure the optimal setup, configuration, and support of SAP Logistics processes, aligning system capabilities with business requirements and strategic objectives.
We are open to hiring this position in our Polish locations in
Czechowice
and
Warsaw
. Therefore, applicants must be based in one of these locations or demonstrate a willingness to relocate.
Your Responsibilities
Analyze and understand business processes in Logistics and gather detailed business requirements.
Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams (Fi/Co, Procurement, SD, PP, etc.), Process Engineers, and Subject Matter Experts to understand application requirements and translate them into functional specifications.
Support the preparation of Business Cases, feasibility studies, Proofs of Concept (PoCs), and effort estimates for business requests, system changes, and projects.
Configure and customize SAP MM and EWM module in line with business process needs, requirements specifications and according to SAP standards, and best practices.
Perform functional testing of SAP configurations to ensure requirements are met and solutions operate effectively.
Support colleagues and end-users during testing phases for systems and applications.
Collaborate with stakeholders to approve releases into quality and production environments.
Define acceptance criteria in partnership with Business and other SAP Analysts.
Document system functionalities, create user manuals, and develop training materials.
Organize and conduct end-user training sessions in collaboration with Key Users and Process Engineers.
Lead and contribute to SAP implementation, rollout, and enhancement projects.
Support change management activities and assisting business stakeholders during system deployments.
What You Bring
Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Business Administration, Logistics or Information Technology
Proven experience as an SAP Business Analyst/Consultant with a focus on SAP EWM and MM
In-depth knowledge of SAP Logistics functionalities including Warehouse Structure set-up and configuration, advanced warehouse management processes in EWM, shipping processes and integration with Production Planning
Experience in Material Flow Systems (MFS) to bridge the gap between IT, Logistics, and our automated warehouse is an advantage
Strong understanding of the integration points between SAP Logistics modules and other SAP modules such as SD, PP, and FI/CO
Fluent in English and Polish
Deep understanding of industrial and commercial business processes, specifically in a Business Analyst role in Logistics
Willingness to travel as needed
