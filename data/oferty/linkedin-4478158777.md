Our Opening and Your Responsibilities
Join Mettler-Toledo as a Business Transformation Expert and prepare our units for Blue Ocean implementation.
🌟 Manage Business Transformation:
* Conduct as-is analysis, identifying business and functional gaps for projects.
* Serve as a trusted advisor on templates and processes, facilitating cross-functional integration.
* Drive template roll-ins for global process alignment, ensuring adherence.
* Communicate Blue Ocean mission and vision to business units.
* Find optimal template solutions, avoiding local adaptations, and document user requirements.
* Act as a liaison between stakeholders and IT teams; maintain process documentation.
* Apply Blue Ocean methodology and manage Data Governance during roll-ins.
* Monitor project KPIs and risks to ensure day 1 readiness and smooth go-live.
🌟 Key User and Stakeholder Guidance:
* Manage local key user teams and guide them through implementation.
* Build ownership among key users by sharing best practices and facilitating exchange.
* Ensure transparency and mitigate post-go-live risks to operations.
🌟 Change Agent Responsibilities:
* Understand critical business needs for go-live and provide change coaching.
* Conduct change impact assessments and define actions with key users.
* Lead organization design sessions and ensure readiness through training and workshops.
* Facilitate smooth transitions with minimal disruptions.
🌟 Starter Pack Activities:
* Prepare units for Blue Ocean implementation by ensuring data and business readiness.
* Drive data cleansing and ensure high-quality master data before roll-in.
* Analyze as-is processes, focusing on significant template and business gaps.
