About the Role
As our
Retail Area Manager
, you’ll play a
key role in scaling Tylko’s physical retail across European markets.
You’ll lead our Store Managers and their teams across stores, kiosks and partner showrooms - where customers walk in, touch the product and decide.
This is face-to-face, B2C retail sales.
You’ll turn a growing network of 10+ locations in 7 countries (DACH, PL, NL, FR, DK) into one high-performing sales organisation - setting clear standards, building strong teams, and making sure every customer gets an exceptional Tylko experience.
Our sale is consultative: high-ticket, configured to the customer, often decided over more than one visit. You’ll own the day-to-day performance of our stores, from hitting sales targets to developing the people behind them. You’ll build repeatable ways of working, create clear growth paths for retail talent, and turn commercial strategy into action on the shop floor.
This is a hands-on role with real ownership and impact. You’ll work closely with teams across Tylko and report directly to our CBO to shape how we sell, how we grow our retail presence, and ultimately how customers experience our brand in the real world.
What You’ll Do
Own sales performance across our stores, kiosks, and partner showrooms - targets, conversion, average order value - and act on the numbers every week
Lead, coach and grow Store Managers, and through them, the teams on the shop floor - including performance management and hiring
Spend real time in our stores, coaching selling, not just reviewing reports
Build scalable sales standards and customer journeys that deliver a consistent Tylko experience across locations
Develop onboarding and career paths that help retail talent succeed and grow with us
Shape incentive and bonus schemes with the CBO, ensuring they support performance and are delivered accurately and on time
Plan and monitor retail budgets, including people and local promotional spend
Partner cross-functionally with e-commerce, marketing, and customer care to improve commercial performance and customer experience
Build staffing and hiring plans to ensure our teams are ready to support new market openings
