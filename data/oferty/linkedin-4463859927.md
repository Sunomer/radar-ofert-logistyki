What You Can Expect
The Global Planning Platforms Associate Director serves as the strategic owner of Zimmer Biomet's planning technology ecosystem and is accountable for the vision, roadmap, governance, adoption, and continuous improvement of enterprise planning platforms. With SAP Integrated Business Planning (IBP) as the core planning platform, this role ensures planning technology effectively enables Demand Planning, Supply Planning, Inventory Planning, Executive IBP, Scenario Planning, Product Rationalization, and future AI-enabled planning capabilities. This position bridges business strategy and technology, driving planning transformation and ensuring technology investments deliver measurable business value.
