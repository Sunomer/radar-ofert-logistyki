Job Description
We're looking for a Microsoft Dynamics 365 Consultant to join a long-term international project focused on implementing and optimizing Microsoft Dynamics 365 solutions. You'll work with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality ERP solutions, support business transformation initiatives, and contribute to the successful rollout of Dynamics 365 across multiple business areas.
Responsibilities
Analyze business requirements related to Supply Chain Management.
Configure Microsoft Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management modules.
Facilitate workshops with business stakeholders.
Support procurement, inventory, warehousing, manufacturing, and logistics processes.
Prepare functional documentation.
Participate in system testing, deployments, and user training.
Work closely with Solution Architects, Developers, and Project Teams.
Profile / Requirements
Proven experience as a Microsoft Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management (SCM) / Dynamics AX Functional Consultant.
Experience with one or more Supply Chain modules, such as Procurement, Inventory, Warehousing, Manufacturing, or Logistics.
Experience delivering ERP implementation projects.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
Professional proficiency in English.
Nice To Have
Microsoft Dynamics 365 certifications.
Background in manufacturing, engineering, logistics, or industrial environments.
