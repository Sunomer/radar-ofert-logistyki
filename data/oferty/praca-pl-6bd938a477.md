WHO ARE WE?
JTI GBS Poland is a global business services center and the largest of its kind within Japan Tobacco International worldwide. We are a team of over 1,000 specialists from various fields, united by a common goal: advancing JTI Group's international business operations. Our expertise spans finance, marketing, sales, HR, research & development, legal & regulatory affairs, and global supply chain. We are driven by passion and commitment, which fuels our unwavering enthusiasm for the future.
JTI GBS Poland is a workplace that embraces diversity. We demonstrated this by joining the exclusive group of companies that received the GES (Global Equality Standard) certification, awarded by EY. This is one of the world’s first certificates focused on diversity and inclusion, assessing equality in opportunities and pay within organizations.
COMPANY ADDRESS
The Global Business Service Center, JTI GBS, is located in the office complex of Fabryka Norblina and occupies two floors of the building. It boasts a modern office space of 6 thousand square meters! Our GBS office in Warsaw has received a world-class recognition for creating an office space that meets sustainable development requirements and has obtained the BREEAM certification.
In addition to achieving the highest BREEAM Outstanding certification rating, our GBS office has also been recognized as the most environmentally friendly space among all assessed buildings in Poland, thus receiving the second highest rating in the world. This result is a testament to the joint efforts and dedication put into creating a new office space that not only meets but exceeds environmental standards, and supports JTI's long-term commitment to sustainable development.
A VIBRANT WORKPLACE
JTI GBS received the prestigious ABSL Diamonds Award in 2022 in the category of Vibrant Workplace. This esteemed award is bestowed upon employers who excel in creating a friendly and inspiring work environment. It serves as evidence of our commitment to fostering a positive atmosphere where our talented employees can thrive and feel motivated. We firmly believe that by providing such a vibrant workplace, we enhance creativity, collaboration, and ultimately deliver exceptional results.
At JTI GBS Poland, we have full confidence in our ability to be a global leader in business services. Our dedicated team, state-of-the-art facilities, and recognition from prestigious organizations like the ABSL are clear indicators of our unwavering commitment to excellence and the creation of a positive work environment. We take great pride in being a part of JTI's Group operations and making valuable contributions to the company's overall success.
Creating fulfilling moments. Creating a better future.
