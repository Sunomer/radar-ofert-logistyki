Welcome to Category Area Wood, the home of four wood related categories. With about 250 co-workers and 200 suppliers across the world, we create a strong wood supply chain for more sustainable and affordable IKEA products for the many.
Wood is an essential material and important part of the IKEA heritage. We can proudly say that around 60% of our sales in IKEA are connected to wood-based products. In Category Area Wood, we procure and source wood-based articles that our many customers enjoy amongst which are iconic IKEA families such as BILLY, PAX, IVAR, KALLAX, POÄNG and our kitchen ranges.
Our culture is entrepreneurial and based on trust. We have the space to lead, explore and learn from mistakes.
As Category Pigment & Veneer we are passionate about creating and supplying desirable, high-quality, and sustainable furniture that are affordable to the many and will complement our customer's home with timeless styles (materials, shapes and colors).
Job Description
You will work in a small, 3-member micro-team with a Business Developer who negotiates prices and take care of the overall commercial relation with the supplier and a Supply Planner who handles logistics. As part of the annual action plan, you will be responsible for the constant improvement of the quality of IKEA products as well as the production process and workflow through tools such as 5S, Lean, and machine capacity utilization. You will also perform:
Developing relationships with suppliers – the micro-team regularly travels to the field in order to understand and solve issues firsthand, we see production from the ground up, help with the implementation of action plans, and cooperate in audit processes.
Operation – resolving claims from A to Z, regular communication with suppliers (emails, calls), meetings with management and the team, securing implementation of specifications, product requirements, compliance to product requirements at Suppliers. In addition to this, you will participate on setting of the audit plan, preparation, gap analysis and gap closing. Assessing production risks and striving towards stable processes.
Managing Quality Compliance by requesting specific articles from Suppliers for spot checks, conducting spot checks in product documentation, in case of deviations - agree and confirm causing party, root cause analysis including containment, corrective and preventive actions.
Identifying Product Improvement opportunities, together with its implementation once approved by stakeholders.
Being part of the Product Development - support Product Engineer in the development phase with production knowledge and Suppliers’ input, implement product on the factory floor.
Data analytics – suppliers quality results.
