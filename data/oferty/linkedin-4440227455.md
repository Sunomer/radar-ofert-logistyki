About The Role
We are looking for a data-driven Senior Operations Specialist to own driver engagement and loyalty across our ride-hailing operations in Poland. You will build the programs, partnerships, and feedback loops that make our platform the best place to drive, and turn retention into a lasting competitive advantage.
In this role, you will work across strategy, partnerships, and execution, owning the driver loyalty program end-to-end, building local partner relationships, and using data to drive real outcomes for thousands of drivers.
Main Tasks And Responsibilities
Take full ownership of the driver loyalty program, scale it, localise it for the Polish market, and drive adoption through smart iteration and continuous driver feedback loops.
Scout, negotiate, and onboard partners across fuel, car maintenance, and lifestyle services, ensuring their benefits are seamlessly integrated and visible in the app.
Lead end-to-end rollout of new driver benefits, owning visibility, accessibility, and uptake from day one, and iterating quickly based on what the data and drivers tell you.
Work closely with central Driver Engagement and Product teams to connect local execution with global strategy, acting as the bridge between Polish market realities and broader platform direction.
Analyse program performance, satisfaction signals, and benefit usage, identifying what's working, what isn't, and acting decisively on your findings.
Build dashboards and reporting that keep stakeholders informed on loyalty KPIs and broader driver engagement metrics, enabling data-driven decisions across the team.
About You
You have 3+ years of experience in an operational/consulting role, ideally with exposure to driver engagement or commercial partnerships.
You are comfortable with data and dashboards, experienced with tools like Looker or Excel, and can translate numbers into clear recommendations and action.
You are a born problem-solver who can structure complicated issues into smaller components
You are experienced in cross-functional project management and can align diverse stakeholders, manage competing priorities, and deliver end-to-end with ownership and independence.
You are strategically minded; you read the market, understand what motivates drivers, and tie your analysis and decisions to tangible business goals.
You are fluent in English and Polish and a strong communicator in both, able to engage drivers, present to leadership, and negotiate with external partners with equal confidence.
