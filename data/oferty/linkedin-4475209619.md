About the role
We're searching for a forward-thinking IT & D Manager, Platform / Solution Architect to join Reckitt's IT & Digital team — a team acknowledged for keeping us interconnected in an ever-evolving digital landscape. Your role will encompass creating cutting-edge end-to-end solutions and strategising closely with business and IT stakeholders. This is an invitation to bring your expertise to a place where you can nurture growth, influence progress, and be a driving force in helping us achieve our purpose.
Your responsibilities
Own the Supply Data & Analytics domain end-to-end, from source systems to business insights.
Define and drive the data and analytics roadmap across Manufacturing, Supply Chain, Planning and Logistics.
Ensure reliable data flows from ERP and planning systems, including Kinaxis, into the enterprise Data Platform.
Build and govern trusted data products, data models and reporting solutions supporting global Supply operations.
Define and standardize Supply Chain KPIs, metrics and reporting frameworks across countries and functions.
Deliver actionable dashboards and insights covering planning, inventory, manufacturing and logistics performance.
Drive data quality, governance and continuous improvement across the Supply data landscape.
Manage the analytics backlog, priorities and delivery plans to maximize business value.
Partner with business stakeholders, technical teams and external vendors to deliver scalable data solutions.
Identify opportunities to leverage advanced analytics and AI to improve Supply Chain performance.
The experience we're looking for

5+ years of experience in Supply Chain Analytics, Supply Data, Business Intelligence, Data Products or a related field
Strong understanding of Supply Chain processes, including Demand Planning, Supply Planning, Manufacturing, Inventory or Logistics
Experience working with ERP and planning systems in a complex business environment
Experience managing data flows into Data Platforms, Data Warehouses or Lakehouse environments
Strong SQL skills and experience working with large datasets and cloud-based data environments
Experience designing data models and delivering reports, dashboards and analytics solutions in Power BI or a similar BI platform
Experience working with cross-functional and international stakeholders, translating business needs into scalable solutions
Experience managing priorities, backlogs or analytics roadmaps and driving delivery outcomes
Strong stakeholder management, communication and problem-solving skills
Fluent English
