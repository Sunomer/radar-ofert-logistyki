Job Description
Are You Ready to Make It Happen at Mondelēz International?
Join our Mission to Lead the Future of Snacking. Make It Possible
This is a secondment/fixed term opportunity until 31/03/2028.
Your scope will be all MEU markets (excl. Russia) and will cover all aspects of the end-to-end DP process and systems applicative map (Master data and transactional data in use in DP, forecast generation incl. integration with Statistical modelling, downstream integration with Supply Planning for forecast release and Opps & Risks management and with IBP / financial forecast monetization).
How You Will Contribute
You will:
Drive improvement of common DP business processes to meet business requirements
Be the key liaison point for the SAP APO DP, BI and SAC technical team
Harmonize and enhance MEU Demand Planning end-to-end system usage for planning processes (SAP APO, SAS, Camden IBP, BI, SAC) and in preparation for o9
Harmonise and enhance BI DP reporting to support the business and highlight adherence to IBP best practices in preparation for o9
Identify system enhancements required for optimization of business processes, supporting COE roadmap to transitioning to o9 best practice processes
Prioritize the business requirements to ensure most important system changes are developed / delivered first
Simplify interface and batch job schedules to support an efficient use of system resources
Monitor system performance and escalate whenever not meeting business requirements
Conduct improvement projects to increase APO DP system and process usage.
Knowledge share, coach and train APO DP, BI and SAC DP key users across MEU to increase stability, animate monthly forums with the key-user community (DP Reference Group, SAC Key-user call, preparing transition to o9)
Coordinate and support monthly IBP volume adjustment process in SAC
Provide technical support to ensure correct execution of Opps & Risks supply feasibility assessment
What You Will Bring
A desire to drive your future and accelerate your career and the following experience and knowledge:
University Degree Required

Knowledge of Demand Planning and/or IBP processes in MEU
Business process re-engineering and strong analytical skills
Strong communication, presentation and influencing skills
Excellent project management skills and ability to lead change management and process redesign projects
