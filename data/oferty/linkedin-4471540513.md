British American Tobacco POLAND is looking for NEW PRODUCT INTRODUCTION (NPI) EXECUTIVE in WARSAW
If you enjoy coordinating high-impact projects, launching innovative products, and making a real difference in a fast-paced environment, this is your chance to join us in Warsaw as a New Product Introduction Executive in our NPI HUB.
We are looking for professionals for the NPI HUB in Warsaw who will coordinate the deployment of regionally and locally driven New Product Introduction (NPI) initiatives in a fast paced and dynamic environment.
Your Key Responsibilities Will Include
Lead end-to-end deployment of New Product Introduction (NPI), product maintenance and regulatory change projects within assigned markets and business areas.
Act as the single point of accountability for project delivery, ensuring alignment on scope, timelines, quality and business objectives.
Coordinate cross-functional teams including Marketing, Operations, Procurement, Regulatory, Product Development, Manufacturing and Supply Chain partners.
Proactively identify project risks, manage mitigation plans and drive timely resolution of issues and dependencies.
Monitor project performance through key NPI KPIs and ensure delivery against agreed targets.
Provide project governance through regular stakeholder updates, stage reviews and executive summaries.
Drive continuous improvement by capturing lessons learned and implementing best practices across the NPI portfolio.
Support strategic category initiatives, innovation deployment and business programmes.
What are we looking for?
University degree in Business, Engineering, Supply Chain, Marketing or a related field.
Minimum 3 years of experience in Project Management, Marketing, Supply Chain, Product Development or a related FMCG environment.
Proven track record in coordinating complex cross-functional projects with multiple stakeholders.
Strong project management, planning and prioritisation skills.
Ability to manage competing priorities and work effectively in a dynamic international environment.
Proficiency in spoken and written English.
