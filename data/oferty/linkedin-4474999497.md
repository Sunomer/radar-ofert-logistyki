C.H. Robinson, one of the world's largest logistics platforms, is looking for our next Ocean Export Agent to join our Global Forwarding team in Warsaw.
As a successful Ocean Export Agent, you will manage end-to-end ocean export shipments, ensuring seamless transportation, documentation compliance, and exceptional customer service. You will work closely with customers, carriers, overseas offices, and internal stakeholders to deliver efficient and reliable import solutions.
At C.H. Robinson, we’re firm believers in the power of in-person collaboration to fuel innovation and propel success. In this role, you will engage with peers on-site three days a week, igniting creativity and driving impactful results. With the flexibility for remote work, this role strikes the perfect balance between teamwork and autonomy.
Apply today and join us as we shape a diverse, inclusive, and more sustainable future for the supply chain industry.
Responsibilities
Manage ocean import shipments from origin through final delivery while ensuring compliance with customer SOPs and regulatory requirements.
Coordinate bookings, shipment tracking, customs documentation, arrival notices, delivery arrangements, and invoicing activities.
Monitor shipment milestones and proactively resolve transportation, documentation, and service issues.
Collaborate with carriers, overseas offices, agents, customs brokers, and internal teams to ensure operational excellence.
Build strong customer relationships through timely communication, issue resolution, and service support.
Provide shipment status updates, operational reporting, and documentation support to customers and stakeholders.
Identify process improvement opportunities and contribute to service, quality, and efficiency initiatives.
Participate in customer reviews and support business growth through exceptional service delivery.
Qualifications
Minimum 3 years of ocean export experience with a forwarder
Fluency in English and Polish
Strong knowledge of ocean export documentation, international shipping processes, and supply chain operations.
Excellent customer service, communication, prioritization, and multitasking skills.
Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment.
Strong problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and adaptability.
