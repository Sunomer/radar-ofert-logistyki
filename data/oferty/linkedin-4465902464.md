What's Exciting About The Role
To strategically lead all aspects of Order Processing within EMEA as a primary regional focus, as part of Verifone's global Order Management alignment - driving global standards while accounting for regional differences, and partnering with peers in other regions and with global teams supporting shared functions such as tooling, reporting, and billing. This role drives the function's evolution through automation and AI to reduce overall order cycle time and improve quality for a better customer experience - all while ensuring fulfilment of business and customer requirements. It's perfect for someone who loves both the big picture and the details, and who can shift a team from reactive to proactive, metrics-driven operations that scale with Verifone's growth.
Key Responsibilities
Lead the Order Management; Order entry and onboarding team to:
Conduct Technical Review of and engineer customer orders for faster delivery through Verifone's EMEA fulfillment operations. Activities include but not limited to planning EMEA terminals and parts stock, and strategizing additional processing capability and/or local soft parts suppliers.
Review and process customer orders with speed and in compliance with internal & external policies/regulations including but not limited to booking policy, sales recognition, ITC & Checkpoint requirements etc.
Develop order execution summary for customer care team as necessary
Handle change order request from customers
Lead Customer Care Team To
Manage contractual requirements of customer orders which include but not limited to documents submission, bank guarantees, production schedule, coordinate 3rd party witness inspections, LC review, advance payment collection, expedite customer orders as necessary and provide weekly order status update to customers, work with freight forwarder for shipment arrangement and billing to customer upon delivery etc.
Measure customer order RDSL/PDSL and continuously drive for performance improvement
Conduct monthly sales forecast and actualization
Continuously drive for productivity improvement
Ensure Operational Readiness and compliance with client onboarding, ordering and product introduction
Proactively work with Legal, Product, Contract Administration and Engineering to ensure review and evaluation of operationalizing new product billing and operational readiness. Identify risks of terms for products and proposals as it impacts compliance, system gaps or scalability of support.
Ensure ongoing monitoring to track commercial/operational risks of backlog orders, monthly review with business leaders for necessary actions to mitigate the risks.
Ensure all related employees (sales, AE, project team, order management team etc) with sound knowledge of VeriFone’s requirements in T&C of sales, internal procedures for T&C review and approval by conducting internal training or other available training source in EPM AP.
Engage with Product and Engineering teams to provide review and guidance for automated onboarding tools
Ensure all team functions support Verfifone policy & applicable governing bodies
Support site international trade compliance and checkpoint function.
Ensure all sale transactions, import & export activities are in compliance with ITC & checkpoint requirements via training, operating procedure, work instructions etc.
Conduct annual self-assessment and follow up to close any outstanding issues.
Manage & supervise order related record keeping and ensure compliance with VeriFone policy
Work with Finance, Billing and FP&A teams on enacting accurate billing and governance policy and procedures
Continuous Improvement, Automation & Systems Strategy
Strategically review what and how the team works, leveraging automation and AI to reduce overall order cycle time and improve quality, enhancing the customer experience
Collaborate with Order Management peers in other regions and with global teams supporting shared functions - including tooling, reporting, and billing - to align on global standards while accounting for regional differences
Shift the function from a reactive to a proactive operating model, aligning cross-functionally on operational readiness ahead of new product delivery
Define and use metrics - volume, time, quality, and cost - to shape strategy, prioritize work, and evaluate team performance and output
Partner with Product, Engineering, and other cross-functional teams to identify the root cause of process gaps and issues, and design scalable solutions that support Verifone's growth
Influence enhancements to Verifone's order management systems, tools, and operations structure to drive better efficiency and outcomes
Engage in solutions conversations with Product and Engineering to identify system and process changes that allow new products to scale without requiring manual workarounds
Monitor compliance requirements and competitor/industry practices to define and incorporate best-in-class standards into team processes
Continuously seek for waste elimination, process automation opportunities to achieve response/process speed and accuracy
Develop, implement, and sustain standard operation procedures for the department and continuously improve the process by analyzing/monitoring errors
