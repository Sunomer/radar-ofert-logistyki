Your role
As a IBP/ITP Analyst, you will support the Integrated Business Planning (IBP) process by delivering accurate data analysis, planning insights, and process improvements. Your work will help ensure planning decisions are supported by reliable data, standardized reporting, and efficient analytical tools, contributing to effective business performance and operational excellence.
Your tasks
Prepare and maintain data packs for Demand Review, Supply Review, Integrated Reconciliation, and Management Business Review (MBR).
Analyze demand trends, supply risks, inventory imbalances, and business performance gaps, providing actionable insights and root-cause analysis.
Ensure consistency and accuracy across planning data, reports, and forecasting views.
Develop and enhance reporting templates, analytical models, and dashboards.
Automate recurring reports and planning activities to improve efficiency and reduce manual effort.
Support data governance and continuous improvement initiatives across planning processes.
Your profile
2-4 years of experience in supply chain, planning, analytics, or a related field.
University degree or technical background in logistics, manufacturing, systems, or business is preferred.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with experience in data interpretation, reporting, and scenario analysis.
Proficiency in Excel and data visualization/reporting tools (e.g., Power BI, Tableau, or similar).
Strong cross-functional collaboration and communication skills, with the ability to influence stakeholders and drive alignment.
Strong ownership, responsibility, and continuous improvement mindset; experience with planning systems and IBP/S&OP processes is an advantage.
Fluent in English.
