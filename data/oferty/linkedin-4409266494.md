Own and coordinate the end-to-end commissioning process of EV chargers into production
Act as the Control Tower gatekeeper for go-live readiness and quality assurance
Validate charger configuration across systems (CPMS, payment, connectivity)
Perform remote commissioning checks, testing:
Charger communication (OCPP)
Transaction flows (start/stop/authorization)
Payment terminal functionality
Connectivity and backend integration
Ensure correct asset configuration
Identify and resolve commissioning defects before site go-live
Take ownership of commissioning-related incidents and drive resolution with vendors
Collaborate with both internal stakeholders and external vendors
Ensure commissioning is completed in line with SLA and rollout timelines
Maintain structured documentation and status tracking of all commissioning activities
Support root cause analysis (RCA) for failed or delayed commissioning cases
Contribute to standardization of commissioning processes, checklists, and playbooks
Identify recurring onboarding issues and drive continuous improvement across markets
