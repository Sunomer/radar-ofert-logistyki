About This Role
We are looking for a
Customer Care Trainer
to educate our support, departments, and customer care teams by conducting seminars and interactive courses for new and current team members.
Customer Care Trainer responsibilities include facilitating on-the-job coaching, developing educational material, and organizing training sessions for new hires. If you’re familiar with teaching soft skills like negotiation and problem-solving, teaching techniques, communication, and leadership, also knowledgeable on KPIs, reporting, and delivery data, these are things we are looking for.
Ultimately, you will make sure our customer care experts develop their skills and successfully address clients’ needs.
Roles & Responsibilities
Develop digital and print educational material (e.g. videos and manuals)
Organize classroom-style seminars about product features and care techniques
Conduct role-playing activities to develop interpersonal skills (e.g. negotiation, teamwork, and conflict management)
Identify individual and team skills gaps
Schedule regular training sessions (e.g. monthly or quarterly)
Ensure new hires take on basic care training courses, including communication and troubleshooting skills as well as teach internal systems.
Liaise with managers and encourage on-the-job coaching (e.g. how to handle difficult client cases)
Coordinate mentorship programs for new customer care experts.
Leadership is a must; the trainer would be the first point of contact and interaction with the team while developing abilities.
Assess the impact of each educational course on staff performance and client satisfaction
Maintain updated records of training curricula and material
Position Requirements
Fluency in English and Spanish (C1 level or higher) with excellent verbal and written communication skills - mandatory requirement.
Proven experience in Customer Care, Customer Support, Contact Center, or Customer Experience environments.
Strong presentation, facilitation, and interpersonal skills, with the ability to engage and influence diverse audiences.
Passion for knowledge sharing, coaching, and helping others develop their skills and confidence.
Familiarity with Learning Management Systems (LMS), training tools, or other learning platforms is an advantage.
Bachelor's degree or equivalent professional experience; previous training, onboarding, mentoring, or coaching experience is a plus, but not required.
