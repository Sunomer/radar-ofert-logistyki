We are looking for a
Sales Training Specialist, Fragrance & Beauty
, to join our Eastern Europe team. If you are a beauty and training professional with a passion for fragrance, makeup, and skincare, we would love to hear from you. In this role, you will deliver training initiatives across the Eastern Europe region for both internal and external audiences, helping our teams create exceptional client experiences and bring the CHANEL vision to life.
In YOUR ROLE you will:
Organize trainings and implement education approach for different training participants internally and externally
Present and communicate the CHANEL philosophy, brand culture and product knowledge
Actively participates in developing expertise groups and building strong learners’ community
Collaborate in the development of training measures as well as e-learning programs according to CHANEL internal guidelines
Working closely with Managers / Business stakeholders to identify areas of improvement and training required
Inspire, coach, and support our teams in building exceptional product knowledge and expertise
Ensure an excellent training support for our customers based on the values of our company
Improving training effectiveness by developing new approaches and techniques
Cooperate with Training teams from Europe
Being an ambassador for continuous learning and development
We are looking for YOUR PROFILE:
Ideally several years of experience in a training or comparable position
Professional experience and training completed within the beauty industry, including makeup expertise
Facilitation and presentation impact with the ability to capture attention and influence audience
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
High learning agility and openness to the new ideas
Positive and energetic outlook, able to motivate others
Clear passion for training and people development
High level of flexibility, stress resilience and willingness to undertake very frequent business travel.
Affinity to the luxury industry and our cosmetic products
Confident handling of MS-Office, especially PowerPoint and Excel
Fluent communication skills in Polish and English; other languages would be a plus
OUR OFFER:
A strong brand in the luxury industry and an attractive working environment
A culture where we value passion, respect, humanity, honesty and responsibility
A diverse, collaborative and inclusive team
Modern workplace and great location, right in the city center of Warsaw
