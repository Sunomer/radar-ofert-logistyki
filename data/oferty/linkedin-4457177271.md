About Bolt

About The Role
We are looking for a data-driven Senior Operations Specialist to own the driver and car registration funnel across our ride-hailing operations in Poland. You will dig into funnel data then design and drive the changes across the board, from a landing page to retention programmes.
In this role, you will work across analysis, execution, and partnerships: owning funnel performance end-to-end, turning data into concrete action plans, and building the external relationships to keep the registration process running smoothly.
Main Tasks And Responsibilities
Take full ownership of the driver and car registration funnel, tracking conversion at every stage from application to first ride, and know precisely where applicants get stuck and why.
Turn analysis into action: design, prioritise, and roll out changes that address the issues you uncover, then measure their impact rigorously and iterate.
Cooperate closely with outside stakeholders - to keep the registration process compliant, efficient, and driver-friendly.
Work closely with central teams to connect local execution with global strategy, acting as the bridge between Polish market realities and broader platform direction.
Build dashboards and reporting that keep stakeholders informed on funnel health, conversion trends, and the measured impact of implemented actions, enabling data-driven decision making across the team.
About You
You have 3+ years of experience in an operational/consulting role, ideally with exposure to driver engagement or commercial partnerships.
You are comfortable with data and dashboards, experienced with tools like Looker or Excel, and can translate numbers into clear recommendations and action.
You are a born problem-solver who can structure complicated issues into smaller components
You are experienced in cross-functional project management and can align diverse stakeholders, manage competing priorities, and deliver end-to-end with ownership and independence.
You are strategically minded, you read the market, understand what motivates drivers, and tie your analysis and decisions to tangible business goals.
You are fluent in English and Polish and a strong communicator in both, able to engage drivers, present to leadership, and negotiate with external partners with equal confidence.
