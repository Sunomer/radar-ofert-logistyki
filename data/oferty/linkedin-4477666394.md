Job Description
Shape the future of global logistics with SAP S/4HANA
Are you passionate about transforming complex supply chain processes into innovative digital solutions? Join our international
Supply Chain Team
and play a key role in one of the most exciting SAP S/4HANA transformation programs. As a
Senior Consultant SAP S/4HANA Supply Chain Management (EWM)
, you will act as a trusted partner for the business, bridging the gap between logistics processes and technical implementation while helping shape the future of warehouse management solutions across a global organization.
This role is ideal for an experienced professional who combines strong process expertise in warehousing and Extended Warehouse Management (EWM) with analytical thinking, stakeholder management skills, and a passion for driving change in an agile environment.
Your tasks
Act as a Senior Consultant within the Supply Chain team for Bosch Rexroth, supporting warehousing and material flow processes.
Serve as a process expert for SAP Extended Warehouse Management (EWM) as part of the SAP S/4HANA transformation project.
Translate business requirements into technical solutions in collaboration with cross-functional teams.
Clarify requirements with process experts and business stakeholders.
Conduct functional analyses and prepare functional requirement specifications.
Ensure alignment between business requirements and technical implementation in close cooperation with business teams and Lead Developers.
Prioritize backlog items together with the Process Delegate, taking into account business needs as well as technical and economic considerations.
Support decision-making regarding custom developments by providing technical assessments and recommendations in cooperation with Lead Developers.
Qualifications
University degree in Computer Science, Business Administration, Supply Chain Management, Logistics, or a related fields.
Experience working with SAP Supply Chain Management modules, particularly Extended Warehouse Management (EWM).
Good understanding of warehousing and material flow processes.
Experience in SAP S/4HANA transformation projects.
Experience in gathering, analyzing, and translating business requirements into technical solutions.
Ability to prepare functional specifications and perform functional analysis.
Strong stakeholder management and communication skills.
Experience working closely with business teams and development teams.
Analytical mindset and ability to provide technical recommendations.
Familiarity with Agile methodologies.
Very good English and Polish communication skills.
Nice to have
German language skills
Academic background or additional education in Supply Chain Management or Logistics.
Experience working in international environments and cross-functional teams.
