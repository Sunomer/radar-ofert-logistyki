Siemens Global Business Services team is looking for an experienced and highly motivated candidate for Team Leader/Logistics Supervisor role. The successful Team Leader/Logistics Supervisor (f/m/d) is a problem-solver, mentor and communicator with outstanding skills and experience in order handling area. You will engage with multiple internal teams to support daily operations across multiple countries. You will utilize a wide range of skills and support several functional areas such as operations, IT and program management, to support performance and productivity improvement initiatives. You will support root cause analysis of issues and propose possible improvements. With outstanding ownership and customer obsession you will play an essential role driving operational delivery on daily basis, being a role model in building healthy team spirit and efficient organization based on trust and partnership.
You'll create impact by:
Delivery of order handing services for Nordic countries (Sweden, Norway)
Managing escalations
Plan and supervise overall delivery of order handling services (in alignment with key stakeholders, corporate strategic initiatives and operational forecasts), to achieve top performance results and excellent customer satisfaction results
Manage a highly performing, motivated team of Logistics Specialists and Seniors (ca. 15 employees)
Manage individual performance, career development and employee satisfaction
Checking and improving team’s performance in the area of quality of service
Performing detailed root cause analysis of problems
Preparation and presentation team performance (KPI dashboards) to country stakeholders
Developing further synergy effect in the country clusters through cross trainings and efficient backup model (increased productivity)
Supporting team members in their daily work
Your defining qualities:
Experience in order handling area, ideally in more than 1 country/business lines
Proven analytical skills, excellent written and oral communication and presentation skills
Ability to work with data and make right decisions based on data analysis
Operate in a fast-moving and sometimes ambiguous environment with full control and responsibility of achieving business objectives
Attention to detail and capability to work on multiple projects/tasks in parallel
Ability to work under limited supervision
Fluency in English, knowledge of any other language will be a plus
Efficient knowledge of Microsoft Office suite (Excel, Power Point)
Expertise knowledge of SAP
Previous experience as a Team Leader/Logistics Supervisor, expert or senior who coordinates project team will be an additional asset
