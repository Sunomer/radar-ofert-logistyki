Job Description
Responsible for logistics, shipping and receiving of goods. Follows established procedures and guidelines to receive, handle, store, record, and dispatch goods in a store or warehouse.
Responsibilities
Order picking
Preparing goods for transportation (loading and unloading activities)
Delivering ordered goods to customers
Receiving deliveries and checking goods against delivery documentation
Maintaining order and cleanliness in the warehouse
Qualifications
FORMAL EDUCATION: Secondary school education
KNOWLEDGE & EXPERIENCE:
Required: Minimum 18 months experience in a similar Warehouse / Shipping role
Preferred:
Safe handling with chemical goods/products
Basic experience operating an ERP system
