Responsibilities:
Manage transportation tenders from receipt of tender documentation through to the preparation of the final customer proposal.
Analyze tender documentation, customer requirements, service scope, and operational conditions.
Coordinate the collection of rates, data, and information from DSV country units.
Ensure the completeness, consistency, and timely submission of tender responses.
Cooperate with DSV domestic and international branches as well as business partners.
Work closely with Sales, Operations, Pricing, and Quality departments.
Participate in tender meetings, proposal reviews, and stakeholder consultations.
Analyze data and calculate transportation costs and rates.
Prepare tender budgets based on transportation rates and the available shipment profile.
Assess the profitability, competitiveness, and feasibility of proposed solutions.
Verify data for completeness, accuracy, and logical consistency.
Provide analytical support for commercial activities.
Prepare reports and analyses.
Requirements
Strong analytical skills and the ability to work with large volumes of data.
Advanced MS Excel skills, including:
Pivot Tables
Lookup functions (e.g., VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP)
Logical functions
Data validation for completeness and accuracy
Conditional formatting
Ability to draw accurate conclusions from data and analyses.
Practical English language proficiency at a minimum B2 level, enabling the analysis of documentation, business correspondence, and participation in meetings.
High level of accuracy, diligence, and accountability for the quality of prepared materials.
Ability to work efficiently under time pressure.
Strong communication skills, independence, a proactive problem-solving approach, and the ability to work effectively in a team.
Bachelor's degree or higher is preferred, particularly in Logistics, Transportation, Economics, Finance, or Management.
Knowledge of the TSL (Transport, Shipping, and Logistics) industry and previous experience in a similar role will be considered an advantage.
