About The Job
Position
As a
Stock Support / Client Advisor
, you represent the Brand and ensure every client receives the Louis Vuitton experience. You identify clients’ needs, support sales goals, and maintain efficient stock operations. The role includes managing deliveries, preparing and replenishing stock quickly and accurately, and ensuring products are available on the shop floor to enhance store performance. You work closely with the store team and supply chain.
Job responsibilities
Job Responsibilities
Maintain well-organized stock rooms (products and packaging) and ensure accurate inventory
Manage deliveries and replenish stock quickly to keep products available on the sales floor
Communicate new stock arrivals and collaborate with Supply Chain to meet client needs
Investigate and resolve stock discrepancies efficiently
Contribute to individual and team sales objectives while delivering an excellent client experience
Build long-term client relationships using clienteling tools
Work as a team player and support store operations
Develop strong brand and product knowledge
Respect Louis Vuitton standards, policies, and procedures
Profile
Profile
Passion for the Brand and client experiences
Proactive and commercial mindset and excellent interpersonal skills
Ability to multi-task in a fast paced environment
Organized & Attention to Detail
Professional Attitude
Team player
Responsibility & Ownership
Strong knowledge in various stock management software and Excel
Able to work alone and to build a strong relation with the team
Strong command of English and local languages
