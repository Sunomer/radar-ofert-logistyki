Job Description
At IKEA, we make healthy and sustainable choices affordable and accessible for many people. We are looking for curious, innovative, and purpose-driven individuals who share our vision.
In this role, you will lead your assignments, collaborate with colleagues and external partners, and contribute to business development. We support your growth through development conversations, learning opportunities, and a culture that encourages learning from experience. Together, we build strong relationships and help each other reach our full potential.
As a Logistics Deviation Specialist, you will join a team of specialists and report to the Logistics Deviation Manager. We know our strength is naming our products, not our jobs, but this role offers plenty of opportunities to grow, make an impact, and help shape the future of our Supply Chain.
What will you do?
Manage logistics extra-cost claims, ensuring accurate cost verification, documentation, and use of cost codes.
Lead fact-based discussions, analyze data, and prepare reports that support decision-making.
Authorize payments to Transport Service Providers and ensure each case is properly documented.
Partner with internal stakeholders to monitor and follow up on extra-cost levels across carriers, units, and distribution centers.
Identify improvement opportunities, track cost trends, and develop action plans together with Internal stakeholders.
Drive a cost-conscious mindset by sharing insights and recommendations with key stakeholders.
Build strong relationships with Transport Service Providers and contribute to developing smarter ways of working for the future.
Qualifications
What are we looking for?
At least 2 years of experience in Supply Chain, Logistics, Finance, or a related field.
Strong analytical skills with the ability to turn data into actionable insights.
Excellent communication skills and confidence working with stakeholders across different countries and cultures.
Fluent spoken and written English.
A collaborative mindset and a passion for building trust-based relationships.
Curiosity, initiative, and a drive to continuously learn and improve.
Values that align with IKEA's vision and culture.
