The
Process Automation & Business Intelligence Department
is responsible for supporting DSV GBS by identifying, designing, and delivering automation, analytics, and digital solutions across various business areas.
Our goal is to
eliminate repetitive and time‑consuming tasks
, improve process quality, reduce Average Handling Time (AHT), and support more efficient and standardized ways of working.
The department consists of three teams:
Business Process Analytics
Development
Maintenance
In addition, the department acts as the
Power BI Center of Excellence (CoE)
for GBS, supporting reporting standards, best practices, and analytical solutions.
As a member of the
Business Process Analytics Team
, you will work closely with
Business Process Owners, stakeholders, and development teams
. Your role is to analyze business processes, understand real business needs, and translate them into clear and structured requirements for automation, digital tools, and reporting solutions.
You will act as a
key link between business and technology
, helping to shape solutions such as web‑based applications, automation tools, and BI reports. Through your work, you will contribute to delivering practical, scalable, and value‑driven improvements that support day‑to‑day operations and business decision‑making.
Your responsibilities:
Analyze end-to-end business processes to identify optimization, standardization, automation, or elimination opportunities, supported by data and measurable impact
Own automation and digital solution recommendations, including expected benefits, risks, and alternative approaches
Elicit, structure, and validate business requirements in close cooperation with Business Process Owners, ensuring development-ready documentation (BRD, user stories, acceptance criteria)
Act as a trusted advisor to business units by proactively identifying improvement opportunities and guiding stakeholders toward suitable automation, analytics, or AI solutions
Evaluate and apply emerging automation, data, and AI technologies to real business problems in alignment with architectural and security standards
Serve as the primary bridge between business and development teams, supporting delivery from analysis through UAT and go-live
Support post‑implementation value tracking and continuous improvement initiatives
Our requirements:
Minimum 2 years of experience in a similar role (Business Analyst, Process Analyst, Automation Analyst or equivalent)
Experience in analysing and documenting business processes, including creating clear visual process maps (e.g. BPMN, As‑Is / To‑Be)
Strong communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with both business stakeholders and technical teams
Proven ability to gather, structure, and validate business requirements and translate them into clear, development‑ready documentation
Ability to understand business needs and challenge them constructively to propose feasible and valuable solutions
Bachelor’s or Master’s degree, preferably in IT, logistics, business, or a related field
Fluent English, both spoken and written (advanced level)
Optional:
Background or hands‑on experience in software development or low‑code solutions
Basic understanding of data concepts, such as SQL or working with structured datasets
Experience with RPA or workflow automation tools
