Purpose of the job / Overall responsibility
:
Requirements engineering and solution design for products within our global digital portfolio, definition of solution concepts and system specifications aligned with business, IT and compliance requirements, end-to-end contribution to the development and rollout of digital products, and collaboration in an agile environment to deliver iterative MVPs and maximize product value.
Responibility:
Gathering requirements and providing engineering and solution design for the product
Identify, analyze, prioritize, and specify (internal/external) customer demands towards our digital portfolio and the respective project scope to determine best solutions
Determine feasibility and respective solution specifications according to IT and Business guidelines and strategies. Closely align with involved business stakeholders, but also with internal IT development team
Transfer solutions into production in close collaboration with involved internal teams (development, test and operations)
Provide vital input into the assessment, design, development, and implementation of digital solutions
Consult and transfer know-how to internal customers and stakeholders in regards of digital solution processes and portfolio
