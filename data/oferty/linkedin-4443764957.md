We are looking for a
Senior Solution Consultant (m/f/*)
CPG / Manufacturing
to join our team. We're looking for an experienced professional with a passion for using the latest technologies – including machine learning – to optimize our customers' supply chains and mentor the next generation of consultants.
You'll be joining a highly skilled team of consultants, taking a leading role in shaping solutions, driving best practices, and contributing to the growth of the team. You'll work closely with both our customers and in-house teams to deliver innovative, creative solutions at scale. The role requires deep expertise in supply chain business processes and software configuration.
What you'll be doing:
Lead customer engagements to define business objectives and translate them into strategic planning solutions.
Drive cross-functional collaboration to architect and deliver tailored supply chain solutions that measurably improve customer profitability.
Own the end-to-end integration of our product with clients' existing systems and processes, ensuring scalability and long-term fit.
Oversee rigorous testing and quality assurance to ensure solutions meet the highest standards.
Act as a trusted advisor, guiding customers in maximizing the value of our product and championing continuous improvement.
Provide senior-level support for critical incidents and lead value-driven development initiatives.
Mentor and coach junior consultants, sharing expertise and raising the overall capability of the team.
What you will bring to the table:
A relevant academic background (e.g., Business, Engineering, Supply Chain Management, Computer Science, Data Analytics, or related fields).
Extensive professional experience in solution design, consulting, supply chain operations, or software implementation, ideally within SaaS or ERP/Planning systems.
Deep expertise in manufacturing operations with a comprehensive understanding of planning and scheduling challenges at scale.
Advanced data analytics, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills; strong proficiency with SQL.
A strong track record of leading complex implementations, including testing, quality assurance, training, and user adoption.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with demonstrated ability to influence at all levels of an organization.
A proactive, adaptable mindset with proven ability to manage multiple priorities and lead in a fast-changing environment.
Fluency in English is required; German is an advantage.
