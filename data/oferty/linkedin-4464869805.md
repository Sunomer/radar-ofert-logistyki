Job Purpose：
The Logistics Coordinator is responsible for managing the whole logistics activities for the region, while developing processes to improve company performance.
Key Responsibilities：
Drive and manage logistics flow from factory to end customers for Europe area
Control logistics and transport annual budget fixed by Top management and suppliers monthly invoices
Monitor spot and regular RFQ with forwarders
Monitor and control Forwarders logistics service level
Review contractual agreement (S&OP) with suppliers
Review logistics specification and sales contact
Manage Buffer stock deliveries for customers from Europe
Manage rework and logistics operations from Poland warehouse
Reverse Logistics and rework management (Quality- Sales & After Sales)
Weekly and monthly reporting KPI (Customer Logistic satisfaction--- L/T Fulfillment---- POD management---- Monthly cut out )
Claim Management (Forwarders—Customers—Insurances)
Identify root cause for claims issues and set up corrective actions plan with forwarders
Work in close collaboration with supply chain Team for Tools implementation and process improvements.
Any other duties as required from time to time by the line manager.
Profile and Experience：
Previous experience in a similar role within the telecom industry or a fast-paced goods international company, ideally with both a B2B and B2C background.
Master or Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain / Transport planning & logistics / Business administration .
Native French and Fluency in English – both written and oral communication skills
Key Competencies：
Planning and organization
Good problem-solving
Clients oriented
Capability to handle priorities and pressure
Fluent in English
ERP
Microsoft Project
