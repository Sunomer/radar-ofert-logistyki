DESCRIPTION OF THE TASKS
The external service provider will perform the following typical tasks and responsibilities:
Requirements & Process Modeling:
Models and documents value chain/streams, business processes, roles, capabilities, and data.
Elicitation & Analysis:
Elicits and analyses business needs, requirements, and user stories.
Stakeholder Support:
Supports business experts and product owners during definition of requirements and software development.
Lifecycle Tracking:
Maintains and tracks requirements/user stories throughout the systems lifecycle.
Specification Transfer:
Transfers business, user, and technical needs into business requirements specifications.
Strategic Mapping:
Maps business goals, project benefits, priorities, components, and system features.
System & Solution Analysis:
Analyses available systems, supports identification of options for potential solutions, and supports preparation of business cases and feasibility studies.
Quality & Change Management:
Contributes to systems design, testing, quality assurance, and change management.
Process Excellence:
Responsible for designing, validating, and testing business processes for achieving business process excellence, taking a holistic approach in planning and managing programmes for continuous process improvements.
Coaching & Collaboration:
Responsible for coaching Frontex staff on upcoming changes (ensuring a plan is in place to teach new skills/process changes) and collaborates with process owners to continually seek out process improvements.
Optimization & Efficiency:
Identifies opportunities for business process improvement leading to improved customer service, lower costs, or higher efficiency/revenue.
Communication:
Communicates project objectives, business cases, requirements, and scope to all stakeholders to provide rapid responses to business needs.
Quantitative Analytics:
Quantitatively analyses business processes, performing cost-benefit and what-if analysis.
Strategic Planning & Transition:
Plans organizational change processes, implements performance monitoring systems for business processes, supports business users in developing strategic/tactical plans, and supports definition of transition roadmaps for business architectures.
