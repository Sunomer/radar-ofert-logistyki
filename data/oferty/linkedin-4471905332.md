Global Logistics Associate, office based hybrid, Warsaw, Poland
ICON is a global healthcare intelligence and clinical research organisation united by a mission to bring new medicines and treatments to patients faster.
As a values-driven organisation, integrity, collaboration, agility, and inclusion are at the heart of how we work and interact with each other, customers, patients and suppliers.
As a Global Logistics Associate at ICON, you will coordinate and manage the efficient movement of goods and materials across the global supply chain. You will support the delivery of high-quality services to our clients by ensuring timely and cost-effective logistics operations.
What You Will Do
Your role will involve delivering supply chain and procurement work to a high standard, working closely with your team and stakeholders.
Key Responsibilities Include
Coordinating the global shipping and distribution of materials, including tracking and managing logistics schedules to meet project timelines.
Communicating with internal teams and external partners to ensure smooth logistics operations and resolve any issues related to shipments and deliveries.
Supporting the development and implementation of logistics strategies and procedures to optimize supply chain efficiency and cost management.
Maintaining accurate records of shipments, inventory, and logistics transactions to support compliance and reporting requirements.
Assisting with the evaluation of logistics performance metrics and identifying opportunities for process improvements and cost savings.
Your Profile
You will bring relevant supply chain and procurement experience, along with the following qualifications and skills.
Required Qualifications And Experience
Bachelor's degree in supply chain management, logistics, business administration, or a related field.
1-2 years of experience in logistics, supply chain management, or a related area, preferably in a global or multi-site environment.
Strong organizational and time-management skills, with the ability to handle multiple priorities and work effectively in a fast-paced environment.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with diverse teams and stakeholders.
Proficiency in logistics and inventory management software, with strong analytical skills and attention to detail.
