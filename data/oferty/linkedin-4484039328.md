About The Role
The role involves managing multiple projects on specific brands, mostly concerning new products development from initiation (product definition) through to first delivery. The individual will be responsible for ensuring accurate and timely completion of all steps leading to successful product launch. It is necessary to stay in close cooperation with cross-functional teams including factory representatives. The individual will be responsible for presenting projects’ updates to the management team and maintain project tracker with all the details. Additionally, the individual will be responsible for portfolio check-ups to make sure all products are properly marked in the system and stop being sold after they stop benefiting the company.
Your responsibilities
Participate in marketing planning sessions
Support preparation for new launches presentations from supply perspective
Run feasibility checks for new initiatives
Ensure keeping the process steps & using established templates
Build CPS for GATE 1 meeting
Track Fast SKU & Quality One records for all new SKUs & act if any delays vs CPS
Keep track on the projects’ timelines to ensure implementation on time
Lead commercial project meetings on brand level
Lead communication with the factories in the execution phase
Update local leadership teams on the projects’ status during Decision Meetings
Lead & track delisting progress on SKU level
Cooperate with regional team & share best practices
Lead process workshops & updates for commercial team members, prepare intro for newcomers
Lead portfolio review process & decomplexity meetings
