About The Role
Plans and executes go-live cutover for the SCM Deployment. Manages the detailed SCM cutover plan across EWM, Planning, Manufacturing, Finance, and technical workstreams for Central and EMEA go-live.
Essential Job Functions
Develop and manage the SCM Deployment cutover plan covering EWM, IBP, PPDS, aATP, Manufacturing, Finance, and technical workstreams
Coordinate stock transfer, inventory freeze, and warehouse cutover activities with EWM and logistics business teams
Manage SCM cutover mock runs and document remediation actions for final cutover
Track cutover task dependencies and critical path for SCM go-live
Facilitate go/no-go decision framework for SCM go-live with the Program Cutover Manager
Produce cutover status reports and coordinate hypercare support planning for SCM streams
Qualifications & Experience
Bachelor's degree in IT, Supply Chain, or Business
Extensive SAP cutover management experience with SCM or logistics implementation delivery
Expertise in SCM cutover including inventory migration, warehouse cutover, and planning data migration sequencing
Experience coordinating cross-functional cutover activities across EWM, PP, Finance, and technical workstreams
Strong project coordination and stakeholder communication skills under cutover pressure
Knowledge of SAP Activate cutover methodology and best practices
Nice To Have
SCM or EWM cutover project experience
SAP Activate methodology certification or training
