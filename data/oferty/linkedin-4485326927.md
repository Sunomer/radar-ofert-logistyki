About the job SAP SD/LE Project Lead
SAP SD/LE Project Lead
About the role
Vortech is looking for an experienced
SAP SD/LE Project Lead
to take ownership of the Sales and Logistics area within the SAP service of a global company in the consumer goods sector, with a strong international retail and e-commerce presence.
This is a stable, long-term service covering international initiatives across sales, integrations, supply chain and logistics. The successful candidate will act as the main SAP SD/LE functional reference for business stakeholders and will coordinate the ABAP development team.
The role combines
SAP SD/LE functional expertise, business analysis and AMS service coordination
, requiring regular interaction with international business teams in English.
Responsibilities
Lead the SAP SD/LE Application Management Service (AMS), including demand prioritisation, SLA monitoring, critical incident management and client reporting.
Act as an IT Business Analyst for international business teams, gathering requirements, proposing solutions based on SAP standard functionality and validating the proposed approach.
Design and configure end-to-end sales and logistics processes, including:
Sales orders
Deliveries
Shipping
Transportation
Billing
Intercompany scenarios
Pricing procedures
Define functional specifications for system enhancements and coordinate their implementation with the ABAP development team.
Define acceptance criteria and coordinate functional testing and production releases.
Manage integrations and interfaces, including IDocs and file-based mass uploads, with web channels, Finance and other systems.
Coordinate dependencies with SAP MM, FICO, integration and business teams.
Requirements
At least 7 years of experience as an SAP SD Consultant.
Hands-on experience with SAP LE, particularly deliveries, shipping and transportation processes.
Experience participating in at least one end-to-end SAP implementation or rollout, rather than exclusively working in support environments.
Experience leading or coordinating an AMS service or technical/functional team, including demand prioritisation, SLA management and coordination with ABAP developers.
Strong knowledge of:
Intercompany processes
Pricing
IDocs
SAP SD-FI integration
Ability to work autonomously with business stakeholders, from requirements gathering through solution definition, configuration and testing.
C1-level English, with the ability to work daily with international business stakeholders.
Availability to attend the Madrid office an average of 6 days per month, according to the team's established calendar.
Permission to work in Spain.
Nice to have
Experience with SAP S/4HANA, including implementations or migrations.
Experience in retail, consumer goods, cosmetics or e-commerce environments.
Knowledge of WM/EWM or SAP SD-MM integration.
SAP SD or S/4HANA Sales certification.
French language skills.
Working conditions
Location: Warsaw, Poland.
Working model: Hybrid, with an average of 6 days per month in the Warsaw office.
Schedule: Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00, with flexibility.
Start date: Immediate / as soon as possible.
