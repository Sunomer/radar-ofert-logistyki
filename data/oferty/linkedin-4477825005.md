Job Description
WHAT YOU’LL DO
As a Demand & Supply Lead, you will be responsible for translating sales market bottom-up demand plans into supply plans and leading reduction forecasting across your Planning Market.
You will:
Buying Quantification
Ensure sum of customer group plans are in line with total aggregated plan per Continent/ Planning Market (PM)
Be responsible to translates Sales Markets’ bottom-up demand plans into supply plans and buying volumes per planning dimension / warehouse (WH), particularly through:
Input provision on local specifics with relevance for supply
Revision of optimal channel split per planning dimension/ Sales Market to be considered in supply planning (based on hindsight analysis)
Operationally accountable on updating, executing, and adjusting splits in PLUTO (by channel and planning dimension)
Identify deviations in Global supply plan vs. bottom-up supply needs from Sales Markets, and provide recommendations on how to adjust (only if needed; responsibility for final buying quantification remains with Global Supply Planning)
Responsible for in-season forecasting of supply in relation to demand towards Global product
Pre-season Allocation to Planning dimensions/ warehouses
Oversee pre-season allocation of stock to PM/ warehouses based on approved buying volumes and SM plans from both a quantitative and qualitative perspective (i.e., ensure allocation follows defined QCPs per Sales Market and reflects Global and Continent priorities):
Quantitative perspective: Monitor warehouse allocation in tools and review data accuracy and correct parameter setup
Qualitative perspective: Review flows based on verbal feedback from Product Merchandising Leads
Follow up with Global Supply Planning and Continent Logistics in case of deviations or disruptions in Anaplan/ Pluto
Gather learnings and share them with Global Supply Planning and Global Supply Planning Development to support continuous improvement of PM / Warehouses allocation logic and tools
WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH
You will collaborate with Product Merchandising Leads and the Supply Planning Manager in the Planning Market, delivering reduction goals and stock efficiency targets to Product Merchandising Leads on a quarterly basis.
WHO YOU ARE
We are looking for people with…
A bachelor’s or master’s degree in supply chain management, logistics, business administration, economics or a related field.
1–2 years of experience in demand or supply planning, forecasting or data analysis within retail, fashion or FMCG.
Solid understanding of end-to-end buying and supply planning processes.
Experience working with planning tools and data-driven decision making (especially Updating, executing, and adjusting splits in PLUTO).
And people who are…
Analytical and structured, with the ability to work with complex data sets.
Clear communicators who can collaborate across markets and functions.
Proactive and accountable, with a solution-oriented mindset.
Comfortable working in a dynamic, international environment.
