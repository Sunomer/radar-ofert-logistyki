Job Description Summary
As an Engineer on the HGP DLE Team, you will be an active contributor to the following objectives:
The Lean Product Technology team is the face of Turbine Module engineering to supply chain. This team leads second source supplier qualifications, dispositioning deviant hardware, supporting lean activities, cost reduction, scrap reduction and pre-emptive scrap and non-conformance reductions as well. The team is critical to enabling fulfillment for our customers.
This job involves working as a hands-on engineer supporting hardware quality and fulfillment. The Hot Gas Path engineer will interface closely with the component design engineers, manufacturing engineers, and the Global Supply Chain initiatives team to ensure GE delivers quality hardware that meets the needs of our service centers and customers.
