Job Description
Do you envision a future where logistics processes are seamlessly integrated and driven by cutting-edge SAP S/4HANA solutions? - Join us as a
Lead Developer SAP S/4HANA Supply Chain Logistics
and play a key role in Bosch Rexroth's global digital transformation. Bring your expertise to the forefront and lead the charge in designing and implementing innovative logistics solutions within SAP S/4HANA, helping optimize warehousing, transportation, and logistics processes worldwide.
In this role you will be working closely with Business Experts, Architects, and Development Teams. You will also turn complex requirements into scalable solutions while shaping the technical direction of our Supply Chain landscape.
Your responsibilities:
Analyze customer requirements in the areas of inbound and outbound logistics and advise stakeholders on the best technical implementation approaches.
Design, develop, test, and deploy solutions for SAP logistics modules, including Extended Warehouse Management (EWM), Logistics Execution (LE), and Transportation Management (TM).
Create and review comprehensive solution concepts, validate them through Proof of Concepts, and present technical alternatives and recommendations to project committees.
Evaluate technical implementation options with regard to feasibility, risks, costs, development effort, and long-term maintainability.
Estimate custom development scope, required capacities, costs, and potential technical debt.
Coordinate and guide development activities throughout the implementation lifecycle.
Promote knowledge sharing within the team and actively contribute to code reviews and design reviews.
Mentor developers and support the continuous improvement of development practices and solution quality.
Support the global rollout of SAP applications and assist key users during deployment and introduction phases across international locations.
