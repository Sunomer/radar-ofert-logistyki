We are looking for a person with experience in business/system analysis to join a project in the retail banking sector. The person in this position will be responsible for analyzing and reconciling business and system requirements in projects related to products such as personal and savings accounts, deposits, and cards. We are looking for someone who can combine business and technical perspectives and collaborate freely with various teams.
Your tasks
Analyzing and reconciling business and system requirements and processes
Planning, conducting, and documenting meetings and analytical workshops
Preparing and approving business and system requirements specifications
Conducting feasibility studies for submitted requirements and changes
Defining quality and acceptance criteria for changes, including developing test scenarios
Estimating the complexity of requirements, including financial outlays and deadlines
Developing and updating user instructions
Requirements
At least 5 years of experience in a business/system analysis role
Experience in the banking or financial sector, preferably in retail banking
Ability to analyze the effectiveness of completed tasks and formulate conclusions and recommendations for further actions
Familiarity with tools and notations: Jira, Confluence, UML, BPMN, and SQL
Knowledge of Scrum and Agile methodologies
Fluent in Polish
Openness to hybrid work, 1–2 days a week from the office in Warsaw
Nice to have
Familiarity with processes in the retail customer area (personal/savings accounts, deposits, cards)
