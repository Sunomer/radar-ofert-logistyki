Job Description
Are You Ready to Make It Happen at Mondelēz International?
Join our Mission to Lead the Future of Snacking. Make It Possible.
Ready to take on the CEE Finished Goods inventory agenda — in the biggest Supply Planning BU in Europe?
We're looking for someone who's eager to learn, bold enough to challenge the status quo, and driven to deliver business and operational excellence at scale. If you thrive on complexity, love turning data into decisions, and want to make a real impact across one of Europe's most dynamic supply chains — this is your stage.
How you will contribute,
You will:
Experience analyzing existing tools and designing/building new automated planning solutions
Analytical Tooling: design, build, and maintain automated reporting and planning tools using advanced Excel (VBA/macros) and Power BI to support data-driven decision-making.
Deep knowledge of supply planning processes: constraints, network flows, and planning models
Inventory & Process Optimization: lead initiatives to optimize inventory, service levels, and cash across the supply network — including boundaries revision, minimum run validation, NPI and waste reduction activities
Strong command of master data management: ensuring accuracy, completeness, and consistency to enable successful deployment
Ability to establish and maintain data check and control frameworks
Collaborates across functions to ensure E2E planning alignment
Comfortable acting as a change agent: championing new planning processes, tools, and ways of working across hubs

Lead the processes within the Supply Planning Hub: provide supply inputs and coordinate cross-functional alignment with other departments (like: yearly OP process, quarterly run rates revision, etc.)
Budgetary Cycle Coordination: manage and coordinate all Supply Planning activities within budgetary update cycles, ensuring accuracy and timeliness.
What You will bring:
A desire to drive your future and accelerate your career and the following experience and knowledge:
5+ years in Supply Planning, preferably within FMCG, with a deep understanding of end-to-end supply chain processes.
Advanced analytical skills: you are the go-to person for making sense of complex data and turning it into clear, actionable insights.
Expert-level excel, strive to develop Power BI proficiency: including VBA, macros, automated dashboards, and the ability to design or re-wire automated tools from scratch.
Hands-on SAP experience (APO, ECC or equivalent planning systems).
Reporting & Data Visibility: strong knowledge of the BI and reporting landscape, with a genuine drive to improve data visibility and leverage it to deliver business excellence.
Cross-Functional Collaboration: excellent interpersonal and influencing skills — you build alignment across functions and work closely with the Central SP Team to ensure MEU-wide standardization and process consistency.
Inventory Governance: a thorough understanding of OpCo/EOC & GSCP model inventory requirements, with the ability to drive full adherence across the network.
Growth Mindset: a natural curiosity and drive to learn: you ask the right questions, challenge the status quo, and continuously push for best-in-class performance and benchmarking.
Comfortable in fast-paced, agile transformation environments where priorities shift and adaptability is key.
