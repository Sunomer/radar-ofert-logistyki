Transportation Management System (TMS) – Manager
Let us introduce you the job offer by EY GDS Poland – a member of the global integrated service delivery center network by EY.
The opportunity
EY GDS is expanding its Digital Supply Chain practice and is looking for an experienced Manager to lead Transportation Management transformation programs, drive business growth and support development of our consulting capabilities.
Your Key Responsibilities
Lead complex Transportation Management transformation programs from strategy through implementation and business adoption.
Act as a trusted advisor to client executives and senior stakeholders.
Manage project delivery teams across functional consultants, architects and technical resources.
Drive project governance, financial management, resource planning, risk management and executive reporting.
Own client relationships and ensure successful delivery against project objectives, budget and timelines.
Support creation of proposals, Statements of Work (SOWs), RFP responses and client presentations.
Identify new business opportunities and contribute to practice growth objectives.
Contribute to development of EY offerings, assets, accelerators and methodologies.
Coach, mentor and develop consultants and senior consultants.
Support recruitment, candidate evaluation and onboarding activities.
Build thought leadership materials and internal knowledge-sharing initiatives.
Skills And Attributes For Success
Strong leadership and people management capabilities.
Ability to manage multiple client engagements simultaneously.
Executive stakeholder management and presentation skills.
Experience managing project financials and engagement economics.
Business development and pre-sales capabilities.
Strong consulting, workshop facilitation and communication skills.
To qualify for the role, you must have
8+ years of experience delivering Transportation Management, Logistics or Supply Chain transformation programs.
Minimum 3 years of consulting leadership or management experience.
Experience managing senior client stakeholders and global delivery teams.
Experience supporting business development, proposal creation and pursuits.
Experience managing project budgets, risks and resource allocation.
Experience with leading TMS platforms such as SAP TM, Blue Yonder, Oracle OTM, Manhattan, MercuryGate, Descartes or e2open.
Ideally, you’ll also have
Experience leading global transformation programs.
Experience developing consulting offerings and accelerators.
Experience managing multi-vendor delivery environments.
Professional certifications in Supply Chain or Project Management.
Experience leveraging AI, analytics and automation in logistics processes.
What We Look For
Strong people leadership capabilities and passion for developing teams.
Entrepreneurial mindset and business-growth orientation.
Strategic thinking combined with hands-on delivery experience.
Ability to build trusted client relationships.
Commitment to developing EY assets and market offerings.
Excellent oral and written communication skills. English mandatory; French, German, Spanish or other European languages highly valued.
