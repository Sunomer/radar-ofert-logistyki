Our Opening and Your Responsibilities
Business Transformation Expert in Suppy Chain Management will define the transition plan, manage the local key users and stakeholders, manage roll-in activities, and act as quality gate and senior change agent towards the business unit. The Business Transformation Expert can align an organization in a challenging new direction and knows how to manage change using structured and transparent methods. The BTE will be a trusted expert, understanding the template and business processes of the units. #TrainingTeam
You will:
* Drive template roll-ins to facilitate global process alignment/template adherence.
* Manage project KPIs and stage gates to monitor roll-in progress and risks.
* Guide key users and management through the implementation and transformation process.
* Act as an advisor toward the local unit.
* Enable unit key users and build ownership by setting expectations, sharing best practices, facilitating cross-unit exchange and job rotation, and creating a positive change environment and strategy.
* Enable stakeholders for change leadership, run business change impact assessments, and define change actions with key users and local stakeholders.
