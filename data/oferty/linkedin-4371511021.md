We are seeking a highly skilled SAP Retail Consultant to join our team. As a consultant, you will be responsible for providing expertise in SAP Retail module, implementing, and supporting solutions, and driving successful project delivery.
About The Job
Collaborate with various internal and external partners to understand their business requirements and translate them into effective solutions.
Configure and customize SAP Retail and SAP MM modules to meet the business requirements, ensuring the best practices and standards are followed.
Design, develop, and implement end-to-end solutions in the areas of Inter and Intra Company Stock Transport orders, inventory management, pricing, and other relevant processes.
Expertise in SAP MM Module specially on STO process.
Experience integrating with 3rd party logistics providers.
Experience in integrating with various POS (preferably XSTORE) and ECOM Order Management (Manhattan OMS) applications.
Experience integrating with 3rd party logistics providers.
Strong knowledge in MM-FI and MM-SD integrations.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including functional consultants, developers, and business stakeholders, to ensure successful project delivery.
Provide expertise and guidance to the testing team during the testing, and post-go-live support phases of the project.
Stay updated with the latest SAP Retail functionalities and industry trends and provide recommendations for system enhancements and optimizations.
About You
Bachelor’s degree in information systems, Computer Science, or a related field.
Strong experience as an SAP Retail and SAP MM consultant, with at least 8 years of hands-on implementation experience.
In-depth knowledge of SAP Retail and SAP MM modules, including configuration, customization, and integration aspects.
In-depth knowledge of SAP CAR and its modules, including configuration and integration with SAP and Non-SAP systems.
Proficient in SAP Retail functionalities such as procurement, inventory management
Sound understanding of retail industry processes and best practices.
Familiarity with SAP S/4HANA and Fiori applications.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to troubleshoot issues in production and provide timely resolutions.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills to effectively collaborate with cross-functional teams and external partners.
Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
SAP certifications in SAP Retail and SAP MM modules are highly desirable.
