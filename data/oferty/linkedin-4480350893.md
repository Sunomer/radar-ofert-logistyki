Hudson Talent Solutions, on behalf of our global chemical manufacturing client, is seeking a
Regional Fleet Coordinator
to join their Procurement Business Operations Team. This is an exciting opportunity to support the day-to-day management of the EMEA vehicle fleet, ensuring smooth fleet operations, regulatory compliance, data accuracy, and excellent support for employees and key stakeholders across the region.
We are looking for a proactive and detail-oriented professional with strong organizational and problem-solving skills. The ideal candidate thrives in a fast-paced international environment, enjoys coordinating multiple activities simultaneously, and is committed to delivering high-quality service while building effective relationships with employees, vendors, and business partners.
Location
:
Poland: Warsaw
Work Schedule
: Hybrid working model, with 3 days per week based in the office.
What You’ll do:
Process driver data changes, vehicle terminations, fuel card requests and respond to maintenance program questions
Monitor and ensure fleet operation in compliance with local rules and regulations
Provide vehicle information to HR for BIK and payroll deductions for employee paid options
Code invoices with correct GL code and cost center
Maintains outside vendor and service provider relationships.
Answer routine questions and assist employees
Implement standard operational standards to maintain vehicles by advocating best practices in company
Maintain and monitor data management system to organize the EMEA fleet
Maintain knowledge and utilize all fleet information to forecast new requirement
Streamline and monitor fuel / electric purchase system with assistance of fuel cards / charge cards for all vehicles and drivers
Facilitate and implement corrective actions and capacity building to manage entire fleet to achieve company objectives
What You bring:
Preferred: University degree or proven track of relevant experience
Experience in Procurement, Supply Chain or Logistics in an international environment
Very good written and verbal communication in English (min. B2)
Good organizational skills, detailed oriented with a "can-do" attitude
Computer and data entry skills (Microsoft Office)
Good view at handling complexity, problem solving and working close to deadlines
Excellent communication and analytical skills
Customer Service Focus and Process Orientation
Flexible to take on new challenges
