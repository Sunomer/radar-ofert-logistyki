Job Title
Process and Knowledge Manager
About The Role
As a CBRE BSO Process and Knowledge Manager you will act as a key partner, supporting transformation programs across Transaction Accounting and Finance and Accounting. Drive operational excellence, enhance transparency, and connect cross functional teams with the goal to improve process performance through initiatives participation and coordination.
What You’ll Do
Actively contribute to the execution of improvement and transformation initiatives, collaborating with cross-functional teams to optimize business processes and outcomes.
Develop and maintain knowledge management framework and with partnership of global knowledge management teams.
Work closely with process owner and delivery team to redesign the business process leveraging CBRE available tools.
Build and sustain strong relationships with internal clients and platform teams to ensure alignment and transparency across initiatives.
Successfully complete improvement projects within Transaction Accounting and OTC.
Set clear expectations and drive high delivery standards, reporting quality and stakeholder engagement.
Partner closely with the wider service delivery leadership to identify improvement opportunities via process mapping and reviews.
Line manage and develop a team of Experts, providing regular coaching, guidance and performance feedback.
Develop and strengthen reporting capabilities to deliver insightful, client-focused solutions.
What You’ll Need
Strong knowledge of Finance and Accounting processes, including Transaction Accounting.
Ability to define, develop, and maintain knowledge architecture, taxonomies, and documentation standards.
Proven experience in process mapping, optimization, and design.
Strong continuous improvement mindset, with the ability to identify opportunities and drive measurable outcomes.
Experience in project management and delivery.
Readiness to support change management and contribute to transformation initiatives, including the adoption of AI-enabled solutions.
Strong analytical and quantitative skills, with the ability to translate data into actionable insights.
Ability to manage cross-functional stakeholders, coordinate data collection, and translate complex information into structured frameworks.
Positive, collaborative, and open-minded approach, with a strong team spirit.
Bachelor’s degree required, with a minimum of 3 years of relevant experience in process improvement, project support, finance, or accounting.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite; experience with AI tools and Power BI is highly desirable.
Knowledge of and practical experience with data visualization tools is considered a strong advantage.
