Are you a master of organization who can turn complex demand forecasts into seamless production schedules? Do you thrive in a fast-paced manufacturing environment and love optimizing resources? If yes, we want you on our team!
We are looking for a skilled Supply Planner to join us in Węgrów and help drive our operational excellence.
What you’ll do:
Build and manage efficient production schedules that balance resource utilization and delivery deadlines.
Work closely with Demand Planning and Purchasing to ensure raw materials and components are always ready.
Align production capacity with constraints and adjust schedules dynamically.
Monitor performance against plan, identify variances, and proactively implement corrective actions.
Interface with operations, purchasing, and marketing to align timelines and expectations.
What we are looking for:
At least 3 years of hands-on experience in supply planning, production scheduling, or materials planning in a manufacturing environment.
Background in the FMCG sector (experience in supplements, cosmetics, or wellness is a huge plus!).
Strong proficiency in MPS (Master Production Scheduling) and MRP (Material Requirements Planning).
Confidence in working with ERP systems like Odoo, SAP, or Oracle, plus great Excel and Tableau skills.
Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain Management, Business Administration, or a related field.
What we offer:
Employment contract and competitive salary depending on experience
Life Insurance
Multisport card,
Vacation allowance,
Possibilities for development in dynamically growing company and production facilities.
