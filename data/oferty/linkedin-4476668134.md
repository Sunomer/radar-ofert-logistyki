Role Description
The BY WMS Technical Consultant role is a full-time, on-site position based in Warsaw. This role is responsible for implementing, configuring, and supporting Blue Yonder Warehouse Management System solutions for customers and internal operations. Day-to-day tasks include analyzing warehouse processes, gathering requirements, designing system configurations, and managing system changes and upgrades. The consultant collaborates closely with operations, IT, and business stakeholders to troubleshoot issues, optimize system performance, and ensure seamless integration with other logistics systems. The role may also involve preparing documentation, conducting user training, and contributing to solution proposals for new business opportunities.
Qualifications
Strong analytical skills to understand warehouse processes, interpret data, and translate business requirements into BY WMS configurations.
Consulting skills to collaborate with stakeholders, conduct workshops, and recommend process and system improvements.
Technical support and troubleshooting skills to diagnose system issues, perform root cause analysis, and coordinate resolutions in a warehouse management environment.
Sales and solution-oriented skills to support pre-sales activities, contribute to proposals, and present technical solutions to customers and internal teams.
Experience working with Blue Yonder (JDA) WMS or similar warehouse management systems, including configuration and integration.
Knowledge of logistics and warehouse operations, preferably in a 3PL or large-scale distribution environment.
Ability to work on-site in Warsaw, communicate clearly in English (and Polish is an advantage), and collaborate in cross-functional, international teams.
Relevant degree in logistics, information technology, engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
