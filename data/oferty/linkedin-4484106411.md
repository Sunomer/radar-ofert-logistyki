About The Role
We are looking for a
Robotic Process Automation Business Analyst
to drive automation initiatives from idea to implementation within the Robotics Centre of Excellence (RoCoE).
This role is responsible for analyzing business processes, identifying automation opportunities, gathering and documenting requirements, and ensuring successful delivery of RPA and AI-driven solutions supporting global DSV operations.
Key Responsibilities
Gathering business requirements for development of Robotics solutions in close cooperation with Business Process Owners and Business Process Experts
Analyze business processes, identify and implement process optimization for UiPath automation
Coordinating of Robotics development full lifecycle
Collaborate with global stakeholders across Business, IT, and RoCoE Development
Validating and evaluating business process candidates for automation
Taking accountability for creating Process Definition Document (PDD) and ensure high-quality delivery standards, documentation, and governance
Taking responsibility for the Robotics solution delivery according to plan and RoCoE Standards
Explore new technologies and AI solutions for RPA
Requirements
Minimum 2 years of experience in a similar role (Business Analyst, Process Analyst, Automation Analyst or equivalent)
Experience with business process automation, including creating clear visual process maps (e.g. BPMN, As Is / To Be)
Experience with process automation and calculation of total benefits due to Robotic solutions
Ability to gather and understand business requirements and deliver them to developers in an appropriate form
Strong communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with both business stakeholders and technical teams
Proven ability to gather, structure, and validate business requirements and translate them into clear, development ready documentation
Ability to understand business needs and challenge them constructively to propose feasible and valuable solutions
Experience working in a global, cross-functional environment
Process-oriented mindset with a focus on continuous improvement
Fluent English, both spoken and written (advanced level)
Nice to have
Experience with RPA, AI, or workflow automation tools
Familiarity with enterprise environments (e.g., SAP, APIs, data platforms)
Experience working in a Center of Excellence / shared services model
