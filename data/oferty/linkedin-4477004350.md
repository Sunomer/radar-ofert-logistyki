We are looking for a Business Efficiency and Benefits Expert for a strategic transformation program in the insurance sector. You will be responsible for managing business benefits and project efficiency aimed at creating a modern ecosystem for handling claims and benefits. By collaborating with experts from various fields, you will have a real impact on the future of customer service and the development of modern technologies. Hybrid work (3 days a week) based in Warsaw. If you are passionate about business analysis and project management, this offer may be ideal for you.
Your tasks
Managing the area of business benefits and project efficiency
Defining and monitoring project success indicators, KPIs, and measures of business benefits realization
Collaborating with process owners and business stakeholders to identify, validate, and monitor benefits
Monitoring the implementation of the Business Case assumptions and identifying deviations from the expected business outcomes
Conducting analyses of process efficiency, cost-effectiveness of solutions, and recommending actions to increase the project's business value
Identifying risks that may affect the achievement of expected benefits and coordinating corrective actions
Preparing analyses, reports, and management materials regarding the realization of business benefits
Supporting decision-making processes by providing efficiency analyses and optimization recommendations
Requirements
Minimum 10 years of experience in business analysis, business value management, or project benefits realization
Previous work in building and monitoring Business Cases for large transformational or technological initiatives
Ability to define KPIs, performance measures, and models for measuring business benefits
Knowledge of conducting financial, cost, and business process efficiency analyses
Experience in preparing materials and recommendations for management
Highly developed analytical skills and the ability to interpret data and present conclusions
Experience in collaborating with business, IT, and management
Fluent knowledge of Polish
Advanced knowledge of English
Nice to have
Knowledge of the insurance industry
