About The Role
As an expert, providing high quality trainings and development of technical skills for internal and external participants in Poland.
Responsibilities
Preparation, organization and managing training sessions for external and internal participant for the organization’s products and/or services.
RDesigning training sessions and education materials in scope of organization needs.
Skills development and helping participants acquire new theoretical and practical competences.
Analyzing training needs and identification of gaps in knowledge, including progress assessment.
Monitoring and analyzing training results to continuous improvement and sharing to organization, motivation for CI.
Maintaining relationships with other training specialists and external suppliers.
Ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements and adherence to the organization's training policy.
Healthy and safety training participants in accordance with organization standards.
Key Requirements
Higher education preferably in Engineering.
3 years plus of experience in conducting training sessions and workshops.
Knowledge of building materials products and familiarity with painting application or very good training skills in similar field.
Knowledge of local legislation and regular practices in the area of complaint regulation.
Research capabilities for gathering information on competition and industry news.
Analytical skills relevant to the job function.
Advanced user of MS Windows and MS Office applications.
Very good knowledge of English and fluent in Polish.
Very good communication skills.
Readiness for travelling.
Driving license B cat.
