Build a focused distribution business within an international B2B marketplace.
sun.store is an international B2B marketplace with more than 30,000 registered professionals browsing products and over €80 million in annual GMV. We offer a broad product selection, strong availability and competitive prices, alongside pan-European delivery through Delivery by sun.store and buyer credit limits through sun.finance. More than 90% of deals on our platform are cross-border: facilitating international trade is at the heart of what we do.
Alongside the marketplace, we run a Direct business: we buy products on our own account and sell them to customers across markets. We are exploring where a distribution model in solar, storage, electrical, electronics and adjacent products could create a stronger customer proposition through local availability, the right product range and closer relationships with
suppliers.
We are looking for a Head of Distribution who can assess that opportunity and, where the economics support it, build the business.
What You’ll Do
Develop the distribution team and operating processes as the business grows.
Develop tools that would allow us to leverage data to improve purchasing, pricing, spot demand and match it with available supply.
Identify the markets, customer segments and product categories where local distribution could win.
Build a repeatable sales operation serving installers and other professional buyers.
Define the right approach to inventory, fulfilment and local logistics, starting at a scale justified by customer demand.
Own commercial results, inventory turns, working capital and profitability.
What We’re Looking For
A proven track record of building or scaling a distribution business in solar, storage, electronics, electrical products or a closely related sector.
Strong relationships with suppliers and an understanding of how professional customers buy.
Experience managing product range, pricing, inventory and working capital through changing market conditions.
The judgment to test a market carefully and scale investment when the economics are proven.
An entrepreneurial, hands-on approach and the ability to work with the data and reach of a B2B marketplace.
Comfort with data and a practical interest in using software and automation to scale commercial work.
This is an opportunity to build a distribution business with substantial ownership of its strategy and execution.
What We Offer
Ownership of growth at a European marketplace, with direct impact on company results.
A seat at the table with the leadership team and a say in strategy and budget.
A disruptive startup in the fast-growing photovoltaics industry, backed by an industry parent company.
The chance to build your own growth team.
10 paid workation days per year, under the applicable contractual arrangements.
Hybrid work: two days per week in our Warsaw city-centre office, with the rest of the week remote.
Private medical care through Lux Med.
