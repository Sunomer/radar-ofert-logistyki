The Impact You'll Make
Danfoss Business System (DBS) was founded in 2003 and is a world class improvement organization with one overall purpose: To ensure that Danfoss has a best-in-class performance. The organization consists of highly qualified specialists split into more than 20 nationalities, who can all challenge and support the businesses as well as develop the practices and solutions needed to take Danfoss to the next level.
DBS specializes in supporting Danfoss factories with a standardized approach to continuously strengthen Safety, Quality, Delivery, Cost, and Inventory.
As a
Delivery Performance Excellence Expert
, you will collaborate with cross-functional teams to design, develop, and implement effective supply chain processes that improve delivery performance to customers and enhance customer satisfaction, with a strong end-to-end focus. The position offers a unique opportunity to contribute to transformation initiatives and make a tangible impact on business results.
In this role, you will be operating in a multicultural environment with highly competent colleagues from all over the world.
We are a global organization with more than 100 factories across the world and therefore it is important that you have the flexibility and willingness to travel, approx. 70% of the time supporting our various locations
. When not travelling, you will have the flexibility of working from home or from one of our offices.
What You’ll Be Doing
Lead supply chain transformation projects across manufacturing sites, identifying and implementing end-to-end improvements in planning, procurement, production, logistics, and customer service.
Drive delivery performance excellence using DBS methodologies, helping factories improve customer delivery results and achieve ambitious performance targets.
Implement and strengthen performance management systems, using data and insights to monitor results, identify gaps, and accelerate improvements.
Drive continuous improvement across Delivery and Inventory through facilitation of Kaizen workshops, process mapping activities, and capability-building initiatives, enabling teams to adopt sustainable ways of working.
Partner with stakeholders across operations, supply chain, and support functions to build a culture of continuous improvement, accountability, and high performance.
What We're Looking For
Proven experience in supply chain management within a manufacturing environment, with expertise in areas such as SIOP, Planning, Procurement, CRM, WMS, or TMS
Strong understanding of end-to-end supply chain processes and hands-on experience leading supply chain transformation and performance improvement initiatives in manufacturing Industry or consultancy supporting manufacturing industry.
Experience working with SAP, Planning & Forecasting systems, SAP BI, Power BI, and advanced Excel for analysis and decision-making.
Strong project leadership, stakeholder management, and facilitation skills, with the ability to influence teams across a matrix organization. A structured, analytical, and data-driven approach to problem-solving, combined with excellent communication and presentation skills.
Interest and experience in digital technologies, data analytics, process transformation, and operational excellence.
Willingness and flexibility to travel globally up to 70% of the time.
