YOUR ROLE
The purpose of this role is to ensure the stable and effective governance, evolution, and global adoption of Contract Logistics core operational system WMS Matrix (Warehouse Management System Matrix – DLx Dispatcher) during the transition to NEXTGEN.
The role safeguards business continuity and growth by managing system governance, risk, and cross‑program alignment, ensuring the platform is consistently deployed, enhanced and connected to wider Contract Logistics initiatives across regions.
The role integrates system governance, business value tracking and data alignment by connecting systems initiatives with Contract Logistics programs and CDP (Cloud Data Platform), supporting scalable growth and informed decision‑making across regions.
WHAT ARE YOU GOING TO DO ?
1 System Governance & Stability
- Provide end‑to‑end governance for WMS Matrix Contract Logistics operational system (Matrix DLx Dispatcher), ensuring controlled evolution, operational stability, mitigation of business risks, quantifying the business benefit.
2 WMS Global Super‑User Community Engagement
- Lead structured engagement with global and regional super‑user communities to ensure alignment on system usage and capabilities (especially WMS Core+ and Healthcare), adoption of enhancements, governance rules and feedback loops.
3 Release Governance & Portfolio Prioritisation
- Own the governance of Matrix release cycles by consolidating Product Enhancement Requests (PERs), assessing business value, risk, operational impact and strategic alignment. Facilitate the prioritisation process with Super-User communities, Operations, Regions, IT and other stakeholders, and prepare release proposals for management review, funding approval and roadmap endorsement. Ensure transparency of release content, enhancement portfolios, delivery roadmaps and expected business outcomes across geographies.
4 Product Enhancement & Demand Management - Govern the end-to-end Product Enhancement Request (PER) lifecycle for Matrix Core+ and Healthcare, ensuring business requirements are captured, refined, validated, documented and translated into actionable development scope. Coordinate stakeholders throughout analysis, estimation, testing, acceptance and deployment activities, ensuring traceability from business need through delivered functionality.
5 Project Management and implementation support – Control appropriate PMO routines in different geographies to secure sponsorship and organizational aspects, PM discipline for implementations, implementation support though demos and knowledge share.
6 Change management and adoption - Prepare organization to onboard the changes, support training materials, webinars and communication.
7 Business Lifecycle Support & Continuous Value Realisation - Support the complete business lifecycle of WMS Matrix across Contract Logistics, from solution evaluation and business case definition through implementation, adoption, optimisation and continuous improvement. Promote the identification of new use cases, operational efficiencies and innovation opportunities, ensuring Matrix remains aligned with evolving business requirements, customer expectations and Contract Logistics transformation objectives. Facilitate integration and alignment with related programs, data initiatives and operational excellence frameworks where required.
