We're growing our logistics team and looking for someone to handle shipments for the Baltic and Nordic markets.
Do you like it when everything is well organised, Excel holds no secrets for you, and you value a good atmosphere at work? And you know the specifics of these markets? Then you might be exactly who we're looking for.
What you'll be doing:
Handling logistics for the Baltic and Nordic markets - accounting for their specifics, regulations and customs rules.
Processing and verifying documents - goods-issue notes (WZ), delivery notifications (ASNs), etc.
Working with the warehouse and courier companies.
Coordinating shipments and follow-up deliveries.
Liaising with the Client Service and Production teams.
Creating reports and summaries, and supporting the logistics team in day-to-day work.
Handling complaints and delivery shortages.
Verifying the delivery plan and keeping in ongoing contact with the client and suppliers regarding changes, delays and discrepancies.
Managing the weekly replenishment process – forecasting, planning, master data, and monitoring supplier stock levels against agreed min/max thresholds.
Who we're looking for:
Experience in logistics for the Baltic / Nordic markets – you know their specifics, regulations and customs rules.
A university degree (logistics is a plus).
Very good command of Excel and MS Office.
English at min. B2 level.
You handle several fronts at once – accurately and with an eye for detail.
A willingness to learn and grow, and an inner drive.
A category B driving licence.
