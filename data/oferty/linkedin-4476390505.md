Responsibilities
Mission:
Do you want to use your analytical skills and project management experience in the growing and highly visible field of data quality management?
Then join us in Société Générale Global Solution Center Poland. Currently we're seeking data quality coordinator for our Business Transformation Office (BTO) team, part of Global Banking and Advisory Business Unit (GLBA).
GLBA/BTO aims to support the strategy, development and transformation of the activity, while maintaining a solid and secure working framework. Multiple and growing challenges surrounding data have led to the creation of a data office which is reinforced by a project management capacity within Global Solution Center in Poland (GSC).
Your future scope of responsibility will cover:
Under the functional supervision of the Chief Data Officer (CDO), ensuring a high level of data quality on part of GLBA’s data perimeter
Coordinating stakeholders (project managers, business data owners, application owners) to define, implement and follow action plans to improve data quality on their perimeter
Monitoring and reporting data quality indicators and participating in committees supporting documentation preparation on his/her perimeter
Participating in improvements in data quality management processes and tools, both at Global Banking and Advisory Business Unit (GLBA) and Group level
Required
Profile required
You will be best suited for this role if:
Hold an university degree
Bring project management experience, including requirements management, prioritization, planning, change management, and stakeholder coordination
Communicate confidently in English and can effectively present requirements and policies to diverse stakeholders; proficiency in French would be an asset
Understand data quality and Data Governance frameworks and principles
Possess knowledge of GLBA and/or wholesale banking activities
Demonstrate adaptability and the ability to reprioritize in response to evolving business needs
Maintain high standards of quality while delivering projects within agreed timelines and apply strong analytical skills to solve complex problems
