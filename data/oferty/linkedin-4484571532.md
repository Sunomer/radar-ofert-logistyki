Role description:
Do you understand warehouse operations and enjoy solving complex logistics challenges? Join us in helping some of Europe's leading warehouses improve productivity, inventory accuracy, automation integration, and customer service through Astro WMS®.
Who we are?
Consafe Logistics is a software development company. Our product, Astro WMS®, makes the warehouse operations of logistics and supply chain companies smarter, more effective, and thus more sustainable. We’ve been in the business for more than 40 years, and we still love it! We also know that we would be nothing without our amazing co-workers, so we continuously strive to provide them with an attractive workplace with long-term development and career opportunities.
What does a Business Analyst do at Consafe Logistics?
They work closely with both customers and our internal developer's team,
They identify customer challenges and advise which Astro WMS® functionalities would serve them best, helping them increase their efficiency with our software.
You will help customers optimize:
Inbound and receiving processes
Putaway strategies
Replenishment and slotting
Wave and order planning
Picking optimization
Inventory control
Automation and robotics integration
Labor efficiency and warehouse KPIs
Build technical documentation that will be used as a base to implement changes in the system.
How would your daily work look like?
Your key tasks will involve:
Consultancy on how Astro WMS® can create the most optimal warehouse processes,
Leading and participating in workshops with customers,
Participating in project go-lives on site together with the rest of the project team,
Holding training sessions with Astro WMS® users,
Working with technical preparation such as configurating, setting parameters as well as testing and verifing the system according to the customer requirements,
Process design discussions,
Challenging existing ways of working,
Influencing how future warehouse operations are built,
Attending team meetings, working with other application consultants, developers and project managers in different teams depending on the size of the projects.
Travel is typically 20-30%, mainly for workshops, solution design sessions, testing, and go-live support.
What makes you a great candidate?
If you have a curious and proactive mindset, that´s a good start already! 😊 The thing is, we are really passionate about warehouse management and delivering smart logistics warehouse solutions for the future, so we are looking for colleagues to share this enthusiasm with.
The role also requires a very good general IT knowledge and fluent oral and written Polish and English, but apart from that, our Application Consultants have various backgrounds.
You might be a great fit:
If you have experience in working with supply chain, logistics and/or warehouse management systems, as well as other systems, like ERP/TMS/WCS or similar.
Or you have worked with a warehouse management system on a site, as a business application consultant, or similar.
Or if you have a junior profile with an educational background in logistics and supply chain management, e.g., MSc Mechanical Engineering, MSc Information Systems, MSc Industrial Engineering and Management, or other relevant education.
