At PepsiCo, you’ll discover a place where our mission is to create smiles with every sip and every bite. With a portfolio of more than 500 beloved brands, including Pepsi, Doritos, and Lipton Ice Tea, our work touches millions of people every day.

Whatever your role, you’ll be part of a global community that values your ideas and empowers you to make an impact on your career and the world around you.
The EMEA Packaging Automation Manager plays a key role in ensuring the successful due diligence and detailed design of capital projects across the region, including both greenfield and brownfield developments, low-cost automation initiatives, and master planning and debottlenecking projects.
This role provides Center of Excellence (CoE) subject matter expertise across Packaging and Automation technologies to support the regional capital agenda, with a focus on design, scoping, and costing activities. The position will lead next-generation automation innovation initiatives and unlock affordable, efficient, and sustainable solutions across secondary packaging and end-of-line operations to deliver productivity and functional goals across EMEA.
The role supports approximately 75 Foods plants and acts as the primary gatekeeper of PepsiCo's engineering specifications, standards, and best practices for Packaging and Automation Technologies, in line with PepsiCo Global Engineering strategies. The position covers all primary, secondary, and multi-pack packaging formats and serves as a key liaison with commercialization teams on new go-to-market initiatives, such as canister and e-commerce developments. Technical design support may also be provided to third-party manufacturers, joint ventures, and FOSO partners on an as-needed basis
Responsibilities
Provide engineering design expertise across approximately 75 Foods plants operating within seven OU/MUs, plus India.
Contribute significantly to the design and scoping of major Foods capital projects.
Support the execution team in delivering strategic projects across the region.
Support Co-Manufacturers and Joint Venture partners in designing and implementing projects in accordance with PepsiCo engineering standards.
