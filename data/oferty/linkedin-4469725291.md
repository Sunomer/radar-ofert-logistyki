Position
We are currently looking for a candidate for the position of Senior SMT Process & Manufacturing Engineer for our client from the electronics manufacturing industry.
Offered salary
Salary: from 18 000 to 20 000 PLN gross/month, depending on experience and competences.
You’re ideal for this role if you:
Have 5+ years of hands-on experience with SMT and THT manufacturing processes.
Have participated in at least two electronics manufacturing implementations, including at least one involving SMT technology.Have practical experience in launching, setting up, or relocating SMT production lines.
Have strong knowledge of SMT processes, including solder paste printing, pick & place, reflow profiling, NPI, and process optimization.
Have experience with THT and manual assembly processes.Are familiar with IPC standards (IPC-A-610, J-STD-001) and ESD requirements.
Have experience in production organization, process engineering, quality improvement, and troubleshooting.Are comfortable working hands-on with production teams and providing technical training.
Have experience in high-quality manufacturing environments such as electronics, automotive, aerospace, medical devices, or defence.
Nice to have:
IPC CIS/CIT certification,
Experience selecting and purchasing SMT equipment,
Knowledge of MES and production traceability systems,
Technical English.
Your day-to-day responsibilities include:
Setting up and launching SMT, THT, and manual assembly processes.
Leading NPI and pilot production runs.Defining and optimizing SMT/THT process parameters.
Troubleshooting production and quality issues.Implementing IPC, ESD, and quality standards.
Creating SOPs, work instructions, and production documentation.
Training production and quality teams.
Supporting continuous process improvement and defect reduction.
