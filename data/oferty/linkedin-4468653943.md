It's more than a job
Kuehne+Nagel is one of the world's leading logistics providers. To strengthen our global Contract Logistics IT organization, we are looking for a Senior Solution Engineer (Development) to join our international engineering team.
In this role, you will design, develop, and implement scalable solutions that support warehouse and logistics operations across our global technology landscape. Working with Blue Yonder WMS (MOCA, SaaS) and KN SwiftLOG, you will combine software engineering, solution design, stakeholder management, and technical leadership to deliver high-quality solutions throughout the entire Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).
This position offers the opportunity to work on business-critical logistics platforms within a collaborative international environment focused on innovation, automation, and continuous improvement.
‎
As an IT Deployment Solutions Engineer, you will take ownership of designing, building, testing, and supporting technical solutions for global warehouse and logistics platforms. You will work closely with business stakeholders, architects, developers, and operations teams to translate business requirements into scalable, secure, and maintainable solutions.
Besides development, you will contribute to solution architecture, technical governance, testing, deployments, and continuous improvement initiatives while ensuring solutions meet enterprise standards for quality, performance, and security.
How you create impact
Design, develop, configure, and implement scalable solutions within Blue Yonder WMS (MOCA, SaaS) and KN SwiftLOG platforms
Collaborate with business stakeholders to translate functional and technical requirements into robust solution designs
Support the full Software Development Life Cycle, including design, development, testing, deployment, and hypercare
Develop integrations, APIs, and data flows while ensuring alignment with enterprise architecture and coding standards
Perform root cause analysis, troubleshoot complex technical issues, and provide L2/L3 support during stabilization
Monitor application performance, optimize system reliability, and drive technical improvements
Maintain technical documentation and promote knowledge sharing, reusable assets, and engineering best practices
Contribute to governance, security, compliance, and continuous improvement initiatives across the global engineering organization
What we would like you to bring
Strong experience with Blue Yonder WMS (MOCA framework, SaaS), including custom development, configuration, and integrations
Experience with APIs, SQL, system integrations, CI/CD practices, Agile methodologies, and the Software Development Life Cycle
Strong software engineering skills with experience in debugging, performance optimization, secure coding, and technical problem solving
Excellent communication skills with the ability to work effectively with technical and business stakeholders in an international environment
Experience within logistics, warehouse management, or supply chain systems is highly desirable; familiarity with KN SwiftLOG, cloud/SaaS environments, or DevOps practices is considered an advantage
What's in it for you
This role offers the opportunity to work on global warehouse and logistics platforms used across large-scale international operations. You will combine solution engineering, software development, architecture, and technical leadership while collaborating with colleagues across multiple countries.
Working with modern warehouse technologies, cloud platforms, and enterprise logistics systems, you will help shape the future of digital logistics by delivering innovative, scalable, and business-critical solutions. At the same time, you will continue to develop your technical expertise within an international engineering team focused on innovation, automation, and continuous improvement.
