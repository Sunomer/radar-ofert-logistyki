Do you want to actively drive IT transformation initiatives and work on a wide range of interesting business challenges with key stakeholders across all management levels?
Finance & Group Systems has 100+ employees and owns DSV’s global ERP, HR, and Compliance systems. As a Solution Architect you will play a key role in defining and enforcing global DSV architecture principles and working on the continuous improvement of DSV’s IT landscape.
DSV is on a fantastic growth journey. Our IT architecture is constantly evolving, and we are looking for a trusted advisor who can analyze complex business challenges/requirements and produce designs and specifications to allow them to be solved.
Working with many stakeholders across Finance & Group Systems and across DSV
Joining Finance & Group Systems in Hedehusene, Denmark, you will join an existing community of Solution Architects who collaborate closely with our key stakeholders in the business. You will further be liaising with many of our IT vendors as well as DSV’s Global Enterprise Architecture team.
Speaking at internal expert review forums and advocating for strong architectural principles
As Solution Architect, you will create solution designs and present them to relevant expert forums for review and approval. You will further act as a strong advocate of architectural principles, raising awareness and being a knowledge resource to stakeholders with less business/IT experience. Finally, you will focus on continuously improving our existing architecture and keeping it well-documented.
Your Detailed Responsibilities Will Be To
Challenge, influence and support our stakeholders to solve issues and help realize the company vision
Specify technologies and application architecture as basis for application development/configuration & implementation
Design and communicate architecture designs for new solutions – while ensuring the designs adhere to design guidelines
Identify relevant solution scenarios and translate them into architectural models and roadmaps aligned to DSV’s guiding principles
Work with delivery teams to develop sustainable and achievable solutions, and help verifying the delivered system is consistent with the agreed architecture
Produce and maintain solution documentation in alignment with DSV’s documentation standards and IT strategy and drive solution implementation according to these designs
Obtain approval of solution designs via various architectural forums
