About The Role
In this role, you will own the end-to-end delivery of the Manufacturing workstream, from solution design through configuration, testing, data migration, and cutover. You will work closely with cross-functional teams spanning PPDS, EWM, QM, and Finance (CO-PC) to ensure seamless integration of manufacturing processes within the broader SCM landscape. Your work will directly shape how production planning, capacity management, and shop floor control operate across multiple manufacturing entities.
Beyond technical delivery, you will manage and guide Manufacturing Consultants, conduct quality reviews, and ensure the workstream meets the highest standards of design and execution. This role is ideal for an experienced SAP PP/QM er who thrives in complex, multi-entity transformation programmes and wants to make a tangible impact on manufacturing operations across Central and EMEA.
Essential Job Functions
PP and QM configuration for the Central and EMEA SCM deployment
Design production order management, capacity planning, and shop floor control processes
Configure PP master data including BOMs, routings, work centres, and MRP planning parameters
Integrate Manufacturing processes with PPDS, EWM, QM, and Finance (CO-PC) workstreams
Manage Manufacturing Consultant deliverables and oversee resource allocation
Conduct quality reviews to ensure design and configuration standards are met
Drive the Manufacturing testing strategy across the programme
Oversee production data migration activities
cutover planning and execution for Manufacturing processes
Qualifications & Experience
SAP PP/QM implementation leadership experience with SCM deployment or manufacturing programme delivery
Expert knowledge of S/4HANA PP production order management, BOMs, routings, and MRP
Integration expertise with PPDS, EWM, QM, and CO-PC for manufacturing process flows
Proven ability to Manufacturing workstreams in large SCM transformation programmes
Strong manufacturing operations domain knowledge across process and discrete manufacturing
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering, Manufacturing, or IT
Nice To Have
SAP PP or Manufacturing certification
Experience with SAP MES or digital manufacturing integration
