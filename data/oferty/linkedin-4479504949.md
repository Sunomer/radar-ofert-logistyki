Job Description
As an
SAP Consultant in Design to Manufacture (D2M)
, you will act as a core functional and technical expert, bridging the gap between business processes and modern enterprise solutions. In this role, you will design, optimize, and deliver end-to-end solutions that span the entire product lifecycle - from initial product design and engineering, through production planning and manufacturing execution, to quality management and supply chain integration.
We are looking for an experienced consultant professional with a solid background in classic SAP areas who is eager to shape the future of manufacturing.
No advanced technical cloud experience yet? No problem!
We value your deep functional and process-oriented expertise. If you have the drive to learn, we provide comprehensive internal training to help you master SAP BTP, SAP Business AI, and emerging technologies like Agentic AI. Our goal is to support your professional growth as we work together within the RISE with SAP framework and modern Clean Core extensibility strategies.
Key Responsibilities
Functional & Technical Consulting: Analyze business requirements across the Design to Manufacture (D2M) lifecycle. Translate business needs into standard SAP solutions and identify areas where modern technologies can bring added value.
Process Blueprinting & Customizing: Lead discovery workshops, prepare business blueprints, and perform system configuration (customizing) in your core SAP module(s).
End-to-End Implementation: Design and execute system integration tests, coordinate user acceptance testing (UAT), support data migration activities, and provide hypercare and post-go-live support.
Collaboration & Alignment: Work closely with international business stakeholders, key users, and cloud developers to align process designs with standard-aligned architectures.
Continuous Innovation: Actively participate in proof-of-concept (PoC) initiatives. Gradually learn and support the design of AI/GenAI use cases, side-by-side extensions, and intelligent workflows to automate manufacturing and quality processes.
