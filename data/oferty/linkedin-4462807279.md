YOUR TASKS
Comprehensive management and oversight of the efficient operation of the company's branch in Warsaw (Młochów)
Organization and supervision of the timely execution of pallet shipment transportation.
Effective management of operational teams, drivers, and administrative staff to ensure the highest level of service quality for customers.
Budget oversight, including planning and monitoring the achievement of key KPIs.
Driving transportation cost efficiency in the areas of collection, distribution, and linehaul operations.Initiating operational process improvements within the branch to optimize workforce utilization.
Collaborating with the company's central functions and other business units to support business growth and development.
Building and maintaining strong customer relationships through direct contact and the proactive development of optimal solutions.
Creating a collaborative and positive work environment through relationship building, team motivation, talent identification, and talent development.
Fostering a culture of continuous improvement by implementing and developing LEAN concepts in cooperation with central business units.
Organizing work in compliance with 5S Safety standards, the Labor Code, and Health & Safety regulations.
OUR EXPECTATIONS
Minimum 3 years of experience in managing a logistics branch or 5 years of experience managing a specific operational area, such as collection and distribution.
Practical knowledge of road transport operations; experience in palletized freight transportation is considered an advantage.
Proven experience in managing diverse teams across different functional areas.High level of independence, accountability, and responsibility in decision-making.
Proactive approach to tasks, with the ability to identify opportunities for process improvements and optimization.Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to build relationships and motivate team members.
Results-oriented mindset with a focus on achieving objectives and driving branch development.
Strong analytical skills, including the ability to evaluate performance and optimize costs.
Good command of English, enabling effective and confident communication.
