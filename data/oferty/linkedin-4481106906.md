THE ROLE AND YOUR IMPACT AT DOTT
Internship, full-time, hybrid (4 days at our warehouse in Ożarów Mazowiecki, Warsaw area).
As an OPS Admin at Dott, you will play a key role in ensuring smooth daily operations at our Ożarów Mazowiecki warehouse hub. In this hands-on role, you will act as the primary local point of contact for HR administration, support site operations, and assist with vendor and recycling logistics.
Working directly alongside the Site Ops Manager, you will gain well-rounded experience across HR operations, office management, and logistics administration within a fast-growing international company.
Your Responsibilities
HR Administration & Employee Onboarding
First Point of Contact: Serve as the primary onsite contact for warehouse employees on HR inquiries, managing the issuance and circulation of essential documentation (certificates, forms, statements).
Onboarding Coordination: Structure and lead the induction process for new warehouse hires, set up employee accounts, assign lockers, and manage the locker allocation system.
HR Operations Support: Maintain accurate HR archives, manage employee benefits programs (Enel-Med medical care and MultiSport cards enrolments, updates, and inquiries), and ensure data consistency in HR systems.
Site & Office Operations
Facility & Stock Management: Monitor inventory and place orders for office supplies, cleaning products, and PPE (Personal Protective Equipment), maintaining clear records of issued safety gear.
General Administrative Support: Assist with internal communications, document flow, meeting setup, event coordination, simple reporting, and ad-hoc operational projects.
Logistics, Vendors & Financial Admin
Recycling & Sales Support: Coordinate pick-ups and documentation with recycling partners, assist in organizing sales visits, and maintain the on-site showroom.
Invoice Management: Collect and process purchase invoices, upload documents into the payment system, and liaise with vendors to retrieve missing invoices or duplicates.
What We Offer
Real Ownership & Mentorship: Direct mentorship from the Site Ops Manager with hands-on exposure to core HR and operations processes from day one.
Career Growth: Potential opportunity for long-term employment following the successful completion of the internship.
Perks & Benefits: Access to private medical care (Enel-Med) and MultiSport card benefits.
Convenient Location: Accessible by public transport from Warsaw, with free on-site parking available for employees
You’ll Fit Great With These Skills And Qualifications
Background: Current student or recent graduate in Business Administration, HR, Management, Logistics, or a related field.
Languages: Fluency in Polish and good working knowledge of English (both written and spoken).
Organizational Skills: Highly organized, detail-oriented, and able to prioritize tasks effectively in a dynamic environment.
Confidentiality: High level of discretion and integrity when handling sensitive personal data.
Communication: Strong interpersonal skills, approachable attitude, and a passion for supporting team members with everyday challenges.
Tech Savvy: Proficiency in Google Workspace or MS Office (especially Excel/Google Sheets).
Mindset: Proactive, eager to learn, takes initiative, and comfortable asking questions when needed.
Nice to Have: Prior administrative, office, or HR support experience, or familiarity with tools such as Humanity or basic expense/invoice management systems.
