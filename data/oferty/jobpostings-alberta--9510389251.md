The Alberta Public Service
works to build a stronger province for current and future generations. We make a difference in the lives of Albertans through rewarding and diverse career opportunities.
The
Ministry of Agriculture and Irrigation
is responsible for the policies, legislation, regulations, programs, and services that enable Alberta’s agriculture and food sectors to flourish and contribute to Alberta’s economic recovery and growth. The ministry’s role is diverse, ranging from food safety and industry growth and diversification to increasing investment and supporting growth in rural Alberta. The ministry also supports foundational programs like irrigation and agricultural societies that help create strong rural communities and resilient agriculture sectors.
Role Responsibilities
Passionate about programs that make a difference?
This is your opportunity to drive the success of Agricultural Societies grant programs, from design and delivery to reporting and evaluation, helping support Alberta’s agricultural sector and rural communities.
Key Responsibilities:
Grant Program Administration:
Administer, review, and process grant applications in collaboration with the Agricultural Societies Program Team, ensuring funding is delivered accurately, consistently, and in alignment with program requirements.
Grant Monitoring and Compliance:
Assess recipient reports to ensure compliance with funding agreements and program requirements, following up as needed to address reporting gaps and obtain additional information.
Program Development and Continuous Improvement:
Conduct research, environmental scans, and best-practice reviews to identify emerging trends and opportunities. Support the design of new grant initiatives and the ongoing enhancement of programs, policies, processes, and service delivery.
Reporting and Performance Measurement:
Improve reporting tools and processes to effectively capture program outcomes and performance indicators, enabling meaningful evaluation and informed decision-making.
Data Analysis and Evaluation:
Analyze program, stakeholder, and funding data to identify trends, measure outcomes, assess program effectiveness, support risk management, and drive continuous improvement.
Stakeholder Engagement and Communications:
Develop and deliver clear, effective communications for agricultural societies, stakeholders, and ministry leadership, including reports, briefing materials, presentations, dashboards, and other decision-support resources.
