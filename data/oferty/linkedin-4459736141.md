The Role
Managed Services are provided to our customers after they go live with Veeva Quality applications. We aim to be our customers' trusted partners and ensure they leverage the additional value Veeva applications offer.
Our Quality Services Team comprises 60+ consultants in Europe, of which 15+ are in the Managed Services Team. Due to the rapid growth of our client portfolio, we are opening many career opportunities for our new and existing employees. We hire people from industry, tech, and consulting, which adds diversity to our team.
Are you passionate about technology and life sciences? Would you like to be an advisor to our customers?
As a Quality Consultant, you will support a group of customers assigned to you. Your task will be understanding their business processes, providing guidance, discussing Vault changes, and applying all the required configurations. You will also actively collaborate with other teams at Veeva, shape ideas, enhance our delivery model, and work closely with our product management team to communicate the customer's requirements and meet the evolving needs of the industry.
To thrive in this role, you are likely to have the ability to adapt and learn new technology. You have been configuring, supporting, or implementing software solutions. You enjoy working with customers, speak fluent English, and have strong communication and presentation skills. We have lots of training to help you get up to speed on the knowledge part!
However, if you have yet to gain direct experience in IT, the exposure to Quality Management and knowledge of the regulations applying to the Life Sciences industry (pharma, biotech) is highly valued. In this case, you must be prepared for comprehensive training and much self-learning. Our team will support you and provide the necessary materials and mentoring, so your first few weeks will be intensive in learning the Veeva Quality solution and configuration. (You will not be the first person in the team who, with no experience in implementing systems, is now flourishing and enjoys working at Veeva every day). We give you an excellent opportunity to upskill and enhance your CV!
If you are unsure whether your experience matches the requirements, please feel free to submit your application.
What You'll Do
Implement new features within the Veeva Quality suite at Life Sciences companies ranging from the world’s largest pharmaceutical companies to emerging biotech
Solutioning and configuration of Vault applications to meet customer needs, including but not limited to workflows, document types, security, fields, page layouts, notifications
Work closely with customer's business and IT staff to understand their requirements. Think critically and help them achieve their business vision
Act as customer liaison, managing communication between implementation and maintenance teams, customer stakeholders, and internal Veeva Product teams
Guide customers in adopting Vault and industry best practices as part of post-implementation programs focused on the holistic success of the customer
Drive change management methodology for our customers switching from on-premise to SaaS solutions
Assist with Quality processes, including documents, Quality Events, CAPAs, Deviations, Change Control, Audits, and Training
Requirements
Experience with configuring/supporting/implementing software solutions, ideally content management software or experience in the Life Sciences industry with exposure to Quality Management and an affinity to technology
Experience in working in a Consulting or Customer facing role, managing customer expectations
Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English
Ability to work independently with clients, comprehend and translate business requirements, and create corresponding solution designs
At least a Bachelor’s degree in Management Information Systems, Computer Science, Life Sciences, or others
This is a remote, full-time role with Veeva. Qualified candidates must be legally authorized to be employed in Europe. Veeva Systems does not anticipate providing sponsorship for employment visa status for this employment position
