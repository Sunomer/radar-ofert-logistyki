We are looking for an experienced Project Manager who will drive strategic business and technology initiatives across DSV GBS. The role includes managing process improvement, automation and AI-related projects that support operational excellence and business transformation.
Your Responsibilities
Coordination of selected projects across DSV GBS
Leading and driving cross-functional projects related to process improvement, automation, digitalization and AI-enabled solutions
Supervising the process of creating and implementing applications, databases, reports and systems
Development of project management methodologies, standards and best practices within the department
Supporting the identification, evaluation and implementation of Artificial Intelligence solutions that improve business processes and employee experience
Creating, testing and implementing tools and databases that automate processes within the company
Improving communication and collaboration with project stakeholders across the organization
Managing project scope, schedule, budget and change requests throughout the project lifecycle
Risk management, including proactive identification, mitigation and implementation of corrective actions
Monitoring project performance and reporting project progress to internal stakeholders
Facilitating workshops, requirement-gathering sessions and project meetings
Our Requirements
Minimum 3 years of professional experience as a Project Manager, preferably in an international environment
Excellent understanding of project management methodologies and the full project lifecycle
Experience in managing process improvement, automation, digital transformation or technology-related projects will be an advantage
Interest in Artificial Intelligence and emerging technologies; experience with AI-related projects, solutions or tools will be considered a strong asset
Strong stakeholder management and communication skills
Customer-focused mindset with the ability to understand business needs and translate them into project deliverables
Highly analytical and solution-oriented approach
Excellent organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines
Self-driven attitude with the ability to work independently and under pressure
Fluency in spoken and written English (C1)
