What you'll do
Drive commercial excellence initiatives across Poland and the wider CEE region, supporting sales growth, profitability improvement and customer value creation.
Develop and deliver commercial analytics, performance reporting and actionable business insights to support strategic decision-making.
Lead customer segmentation, account planning and pipeline management processes in line with global best practices.
Optimize pricing strategies, monitor customer and product profitability and identify margin improvement opportunities.
Support go-to-market and channel strategies by identifying growth opportunities and prioritizing high-value customer segments.
Drive CRM excellence, data quality and adoption across the sales organization while partnering with global IT and Commercial Excellence teams on tool enhancements.
Analyze sales productivity and sales force effectiveness, providing recommendations to improve performance and efficiency.
Lead the implementation and deployment of SHINE and SHARP methodologies across the region.
Build commercial capabilities through training, coaching and change management initiatives for local sales teams.
Facilitate cross-functional collaboration between Sales, Marketing, Finance and Commercial Excellence stakeholders.
Lead the regional Commercial Excellence community and actively contribute to the global Commercial Excellence network, ensuring best-practice sharing across markets.
Support Joint Business Planning activities and help sales teams prepare high-quality customer growth plans.
Define and execute the long-term Commercial Excellence strategy across the CEE region in alignment with business objectives and growth ambitions.
Promote AI-driven and technology-enabled commercial practices to enhance pricing, customer insights, sales productivity and decision-making.
Drive measurable commercial impact through improved pricing performance, margin expansion and market share growth.
