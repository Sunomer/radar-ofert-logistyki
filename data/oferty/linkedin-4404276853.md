Role purpose
We are looking for a senior, highly experienced leader to build and lead the Global Customs Design & Implementation function. This role owns the global methodology, governance framework, and execution quality for all customer implementations across Global Customs. The Director acts as the strategic owner of end-to-end solution design and implementation delivery, ensuring that customer requirements from RFI/RFQ through go-live are translated into clear, executable, and scalable solutions. The Director also directly leads implementations for high-visibility, high-complexity global customers, serving as the senior point of accountability. In addition to handling strategic implementations, the Director will lead a team of E2E Implementation Managers, provide senior stakeholder alignment, and drive continuous improvement of the global implementation approach. The ideal candidate combines strong leadership, deep operational/technical understanding, and the ability to influence at senior levels across the organization.
Key Responsibilities
Leadership & Governance
Translate customer requirements from RFI/RFQ documents into clear technical and operational solution designs
Build solution blueprints, including operational flows, reporting needs, visibility tools, control tower structures, etc.
Ensure the proposed solution is deliverable, cost-aligned, and operationally viable
Participate in customer presentations and Q&A sessions as required
Project Leadership & Implementation Management
Act as single end-to-end owner for each assigned implementation project
Develop and manage detailed project plans, timelines, and deliverables
Coordinate all involved parties: region/country operations, IT, legal, compliance, and commercial teams
Lead project governance: status reporting, risk management, issue escalation and cost management
Oversee SOP/IOP creation, process documentation, and operational readiness
Prepare go-live plans and coordinate region/country teams
Go-Live & Hypercare
Lead the cutover to operations, ensuring a controlled and structured go-live
Monitor operational and technical stability during hypercare
Facilitate customer touchpoints during early operational phases
Coordinate quick resolution of early-life issues
Validate that performance, reporting, and service levels are functioning as contracted
Ensure a clean and documented handover to operational ownership
Internal Collaboration & Continuous Improvement
Work closely with global customs commercial, IT, and regional operational teams
Identify recurring issues and contribute to building scalable global best practices
Support development of standard templates for project plans, SOPs, reporting and documentation
Provide feedback loops to improve future solution designs and tender responses
What Success Looks Like
Customer receives a consistent, high-quality experience with one primary point of contact
Tender solutions are implemented exactly as scoped, on time, and within expected service levels
Technical and operational readiness are clearly defined, documented, and achieved
Go-live is smooth, controlled, and free of major issues
Customer satisfaction is high during and after implementation
Internal stakeholders experience fewer handovers, less rework, and clearer accountability
Implementation risks are identified early and managed proactively
A repeatable and scalable implementation framework is strengthened over time
