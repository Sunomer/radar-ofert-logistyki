4flow consulting advises companies worldwide on logistics and supply chain management – from strategy to implementation. We continue our successful growth this year and are looking for talented professionals to join our excellent team at our location in Warsaw.
What your new challenge will look like
You take on a responsible role in challenging logistics planning and implementation projects and develop holistic solutions together with the project team for national and international clients.
You create and evaluate layout alternatives, design material flows, and define efficient logistics and production processes, automation and digitalization potential into account.
You develop detailed functional and system specifications for warehouse automation technology.
You support tendering processes for logistics equipment and systems in close coordination with customers and suppliers. You implement the developed concepts directly on site at our clients’ locations.
You take responsibility for the successful delivery of sub-projects and workstreams
In addition, you actively contribute to internal optimization and development projects, share your knowledge within the team and help further develop 4flow’s knowledge management.
Why you belong at 4flow
You have successfully completed a master’s degree with a focus on logistics, supply chain management, industrial engineering, or a comparable field.
You have several years of relevant professional experience in logistics planning, logistics engineering, process automation, and have independently managed complex projects related to the construction of distribution centers or in production environments.
You are characterized by strong analytical and conceptual skills, a deep understanding of processes, and the ability to structure and clearly communicate complex topics.
You have very good user knowledge of MS Office; experience with CAD software is a plus, as well as a good technical understanding of logistics systems and automation solutions.
You communicate confidently in English, German is a plus and present yourself professionally and persuasively when dealing with clients.
A high willingness to travel and, ideally, international project or overseas experience complete your profile.
