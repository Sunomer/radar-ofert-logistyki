About the role
This is a specialist leadership role within our ERP transformation across the Eastern Europe & Turkey as we migrate off SAP and implement Microsoft Dynamics 365, with a target go-live of September 1, 2027. Reporting to the Finance Lead for ERP Transformation, you will lead the finance design and delivery across the
Source-to-Pay (S2P)
and
Supply Finance
workstreams, ensuring procurement, accounts payable, inventory, product costing, freight, inventory provisions, and cost of goods sold are accurately configured and controlled in the new system.
You will own the design, testing, and implementation of end-to-end processes covering purchasing, supplier invoicing and payments, inventory valuation, standard costing, manufacturing variances, landed costs, and related financial reporting. Working closely with Finance, Procurement, Supply Chain, Operations, Shared Services, CHQ Transformation teams, and external implementation partners, you will help define future-state processes, support data migration and testing, and drive successful adoption of Dynamics 365.
This is a hands-on leadership role for someone with deep expertise in procurement, accounts payable, and supply chain finance, who can translate business requirements into practical ERP solutions while ensuring strong financial controls, accurate accounting, and clear visibility of costs, margins, and working capital.
Your responsibilities
Lead and support the Source-to-Pay and Supply Finance workstreams of the SAP-to-Microsoft Dynamics 365 implementation across the Eastern Europe & Turkey, translating business requirements into system design and ensuring alignment with global templates.
Design, configure, and validate procurement, accounts payable, costing, inventory, and freight processes, including purchase requisitions, purchase orders, approvals, invoice processing, payment workflows, standard costing, inventory valuation, and landed cost management.
Implement and maintain product costing structures, including standard cost setup, cost roll-ups, costing sheets, manufacturing and purchase price variances, ensuring accurate financial reporting and compliance with global standards.
Configure and validate inventory accounting, inventory controls, inventory provisioning (EMO), physical inventory processes, inventory movements, and reconciliation between inventory sub-ledgers and the general ledger.
Establish and maintain a robust control environment covering segregation of duties, approval limits, audit trails, inventory controls, transfer pricing, intercompany transactions, and compliance with corporate policies.
Manage vendor master data, supplier onboarding, banking and tax information, while ensuring accurate migration of vendor, procurement, inventory, and finance-related data from SAP to Microsoft Dynamics 365.
Support tax compliance on procurement and inventory transactions, ensuring accurate treatment of indirect taxes, use tax, landed costs, and other local regulatory requirements across the EE region.
Configure and support month-end close activities related to procurement, payables, inventory, cost of goods sold (COGS), variance settlement, inventory roll-forward, and margin reporting to ensure accurate and timely financial results.
Lead and coordinate testing, user acceptance testing (UAT), data migration, cutover, hypercare, and post go-live stabilization activities, managing issues through resolution while working closely with finance, supply chain, procurement teams, and external implementation partners.
Collaborate with business stakeholders, operations, supply chain, finance leadership, and global transformation teams to adapt global solutions to local business requirements, drive process improvements, support decision-making through reporting and analytics, and ensure successful program delivery.
Experience we're looking for
Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Supply Chain, Business Administration, or a related discipline. Professional accounting qualification (CPA, CA, ACCA, CIMA, or equivalent) is an advantage.
5+ years of experience in finance transformation, source-to-pay, procurement, accounts payable, cost accounting, inventory accounting, or supply chain finance within a corporate, shared services, manufacturing, consumer goods, or FMCG environment.
Strong hands-on knowledge of the purchase-to-pay cycle, including procurement, invoice processing, matching, approvals, payment processing, and vendor management.
Deep understanding of standard costing, manufacturing variances, inventory valuation, inventory provisioning, freight costing, and cost of goods sold (COGS).
Proven experience contributing to or leading ERP implementations, ideally Microsoft Dynamics 365, with exposure to Finance, Procurement, Inventory, and Supply Chain modules.
Experience with data migration, process design, testing, user acceptance testing, cutover activities, and post-go-live support.
Strong understanding of financial controls, segregation of duties, audit requirements, and compliance frameworks.
Experience working with cross-functional teams, external implementation partners, and global stakeholders in a matrix environment.
Advanced Microsoft Excel skills are required; Power BI experience is an advantage.
Project management experience or formal certifications (PMP, PRINCE2, Agile, or equivalent) would be considered beneficial.
