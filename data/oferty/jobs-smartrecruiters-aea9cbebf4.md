Company Description
Join Sigma Software as a Senior Business Analyst and become a key contributor to a large-scale smart solutions platform that combines enterprise integrations, product customization, and modern digital experiences.
We are looking for a Senior specialist who is comfortable driving product discussions, managing requirements, and supporting strategic decisions together with Customers and engineering teams.
We at Sigma Software value ownership, collaboration, and continuous improvement. You will join an experienced international team working in an AI-driven development environment with the opportunity to influence product decisions, delivery processes, and business outcomes.
CUSTOMER
Our Customer is a global provider of premium electrical installation and smart-building solutions for residential, commercial, and professional environments. The company develops configurable products and digital services used by end customers, electrical installers, wholesalers, system integrators, and certified partners worldwide.
PROJECT
The project focuses on building a modern web-based customization platform that allows users to configure and personalize products using fonts, colors, styles, sizes, and graphical elements. The platform supports guided design workflows, reusable configuration templates, bulk customization scenarios, and structured order management processes.
The solution integrates with multiple enterprise systems, including authentication services, ERP platforms, product data management, and content management systems. The project follows an AI-driven software development approach that combines modern engineering practices with AI-assisted workflows and collaborative product delivery.
Key Technologies: Svelte, TypeScript, Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, Drupal CMS, ERP, MDM, CI/CD
