Meaningful Work From Day One
This role is responsible for accurately forecasting customers' demand for a specific set of European markets, ensuring our supply chain operations are aligned with market needs. This includes analyzing sales data, collaborating with sales and marketing teams, and developing sophisticated forecasting models to predict future demand. You will be the key link between market trends and supply chain execution, acting as a crucial liaison between sales, marketing, and the Global Planning team to ensure efficient inventory management and optimal product availability.
This is an office/hybrid role (4 days a week at Warsaw office with the option to work remotely on Fridays)
Inventory Management
What You Can Expect
Track inventory levels and update records regularly.
Plan, coordinate, and post-production orders (repacks, displays, and value-added packs) at the third-party warehouse.
Maintain SAP database for local SKUs (repacks and VAPs).
Optimize inventory levels to balance supply and demand while minimizing holding costs.
Coordinate with the Global Planning team to manage stock levels effectively.
Demand Planning And Forecasting
Develop and maintain accurate demand forecasts by analyzing historical sales data, market trends, and other relevant factors.
Work closely with sales, marketing, and supply chain teams to gather insights and align demand plans with business objectives.
Collect, analyze, and interpret complex data to identify patterns and trends influencing demand.
Collaborate with supply chain and operations teams to optimize inventory levels and minimize stockouts or excess inventory.
Participate in S&OP meetings and contribute to developing integrated sales and operations plans.
Masterdata Management
Maintain SAP database for local SKUs
Maintain logistic data in internal and external data tools and systems
Collaboration and Communication
Collaborate effectively with the Global Planning team to align on strategy and best practices.
Build and maintain strong relationships with local commercial teams.
Communicate effectively with internal and external stakeholders to ensure smooth operations.
What You Bring To The Table
Education: Bachelor’s degree or equivalent in Business, Supply Chain Management, or a related field.

Minimum 2 years of relevant experience in inventory management or demand planning
Experience in successful related work within a changing environment
