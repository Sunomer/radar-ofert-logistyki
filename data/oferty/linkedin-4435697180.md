Roles And Responsibilities

In the role of a Principal Consultant, you will anchor the engagement effort for assignments, all the way from business process consulting and problem definition to solution design, development and deployment. You will be pivotal to problem definition and discovery of the overall solution and guide teams on project processes, deliverables. As a thought leader in your domain, you will be the key advisor to architecture and design reviews. You will anchor business pursuit initiatives, client training, in-house capability building. You will have the opportunity to shape value-adding consulting solutions that enable our clients to meet the changing needs of the global landscape.

Skills /Competencies

 Essential: 

 10+ years of SAP experience with strong expertise in SAP QM (S/4HANA preferred) 
 Deep functional knowledge of inspection processing, batch management, quality notifications, and QM reporting 
 Proven experience in end-to-end QM implementations and global rollouts 
 Strong experience in global template governance and standardization 
 Expertise in defining WRICEF objects and functional specifications 
 Lead end-to-end design, configuration, and rollout of SAP S/4HANA QM processes including inspection planning, results recording, usage decision, and notifications 
 Configure and optimize QM transactions (QA11, QA12, QA32, QA33) including selection screens, ALV layouts, and table controls 
 Govern global QM template objects (quality score procedures, usage decision, follow-up actions, inventory postings, code groups) ensuring adherence to naming standards and rollout guidelines 
 Design and manage batch-related quality processes including Quality Batch Status, expired batches, and alignment with stock types via enhancements and background jobs 
 Define WRICEF requirements and provide functional specifications for QM developments (e.g., batch automation, job notifications, inspection lot controls) 
 Drive data quality initiatives by identifying inconsistencies, defining validation logic, and enabling controlled correction/deletion mechanisms 
 Define and validate QM reporting requirements (e.g., chain-of-custody tracking, event-based reporting with timestamps, user, and movement data) 
 Define Fiori app requirements and ensure usability, performance, and adoption through optimized selection criteria 
 Collaborate with security teams to ensure role-based access and resolve authorization issues 
 Ensure compliance with global template standards (e.g., ASPIRE) and seamless integration with MM, PP, and SD 

Preferred

 Multilingual proficiency but English is MUST 
 Experience in regulated industries (e.g., pharma, life sciences) 
 Exposure to LIMS or external quality systems 
 Familiarity with SAP Fiori apps and UX design principles 
 Knowledge of audit, compliance, and data governance frameworks
