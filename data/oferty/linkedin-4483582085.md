We are looking for an experienced Factory Planning Subject Matter Expert to support one of our manufacturing sites. In this role, you will provide expert guidance on production planning, capacity challenges, and operational risks, helping teams make informed decisions that balance service, cost, inventory, and production performance.
You will work closely with manufacturing, operations, and site leadership teams to evaluate planning scenarios, identify opportunities for improvement, and ensure planning processes are effective, consistent, and resilient. You will also contribute to the development of planning standards, performance metrics, and business continuity plans while helping drive best practices across the organization.
This is an excellent opportunity for a planning professional with strong manufacturing knowledge who enjoys solving complex operational challenges, influencing stakeholders, and delivering meaningful business impact.
The role
Act as the key escalation point
for factory planning risks, scenarios and decision support.
Govern planning performance and compliance
, ensuring standards, stability and action closure.
Assess capacity, supply and BCP scenarios
, quantifying service, cost and inventory impacts.
Drive planning excellence
, identifying root causes and implementing sustainable improvements.
Support planning standards and KPI governance
, leveraging factory learnings and performance insights.
Partner with Operations and Factory leaders
to manage constraints, risks and mitigation plans.
Key accountabilities
Degree in Supply Chain, Operations, Engineering, Business or related field.
Strong English communication skills.
Strong manufacturing planning and factory operations knowledge.
Scenario building and trade-off communication.
Governance discipline (templates, logs, pack quality) and stakeholder management
Lean/Six Sigma and/or project/programme management certification (PMP/PRINCE2) and advanced analytics/reporting skills (Power BI/SQL/Excel modelling) would be nice to have
