Sword Services Greece is an IT Services and Software Solutions provider, delivering innovative solutions to European public sector Organizations and leading private companies. As our portfolio continues to expand, we are growing our international teams with talented professionals who want to contribute to large-scale digital transformation initiatives. We are currently looking for a Technical ICT Project Coordinator to join an international European public-sector project.
The role focuses on
ICT project coordination
, infrastructure engagement, stakeholder and supplier coordination, governance and service transition, working closely with technical teams, business stakeholders and external suppliers.
What you will do...
Coordinate ICT projects and initiatives throughout their lifecycle, from planning and execution to monitoring and closure
Prepare and maintain project plans, schedules, milestones, deliverables and acceptance criteria
Track progress, risks, issues, changes, resources and cross-team dependencies
Coordinate engagement across technical teams covering networks, cloud/data centre, cybersecurity, databases, IAM, workplace, monitoring and operations
Facilitate technical scoping, readiness, testing, integration and operational activities
Organise and facilitate project meetings, technical workshops, status reviews and decision sessions
Support procurement documentation, supplier mobilisation and ICT contract implementation
Maintain project documentation, dashboards, action, decision, dependency and change logs
Coordinate testing, acceptance, handover, operational readiness and knowledge transfer
Technical Skills required...
Minimum 5 years of IT-relevant professional experience, including at least 3 years in a similar position
BSc (or higher) in Information Technology, Computer Science, or any other relevant field
Strong knowledge of ICT project lifecycle, governance and delivery methods, including PM² or equivalent
Good understanding of ICT infrastructure domains, including networks, cloud/data centre, databases, IAM, workplace services, monitoring and operations
Good knowledge of project planning and control tools
Good understanding of ICT service management, change, operational readiness and service transition
Experience with stakeholder, supplier and contract coordination in an ICT environment
Strong analytical, organisational and problem-solving skills
Excellent communication, facilitation, reporting and documentation skills
At least one internationally recognised certification: PM², PRINCE2, PMP or equivalent
Experience in the following will be considered as an asset:
Experience coordinating ICT infrastructure projects involving multiple technical domains
Experience coordinating dependencies across network, cloud/data centre, databases, IAM, workplace, cybersecurity, monitoring or service operations
Experience supporting supplier engagement, procurement documentation, Proofs of Concept or ICT contract implementation
Practical experience with Azure DevOps, ServiceNow, Jira, MS Project, Planner or equivalent
Experience in service transition and operational readiness
Behavioral Attributes...
Strong analytical and problem-solving mindset.
Ability to translate business needs into effective technical solutions.
Strong communication and collaboration skills.
Ability to work both independently and as part of international Agile teams.
Strong sense of ownership, accountability, and attention to detail.
Proactive attitude with a continuous learning mindset.
