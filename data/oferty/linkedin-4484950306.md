As Material Planner Finished Goods Analyst and part of the European Distribution Center Material Planning team, you will play a key role in ensuring the right products are available at the right time. You will manage replenishment, inventory and supply planning activities, using SAP and your analytical expertise to anticipate supply risks, optimize stock levels and maintain strong delivery performance.
More about the role :
Ensure product availability and delivery capability across the markets supplied by the Distribution Center, in line with defined targets.
Own the daily replenishment and back-order management process, proactively identifying supply risks and driving corrective actions while balancing service levels and inventory costs.
Manage demand forecasts, inventory turns and safety stock for new launches, running products and phase-out products.
Perform daily material and supply planning in SAP, including maintaining MRP parameters, safety stock levels, material master data, and creating and tracking replenishment orders.
Partner with plants, suppliers and internal stakeholders to resolve supply, quality and order-related issues and optimize delivery performance.
Coordinate changes to supply plans, including reprogramming and order adjustments, while supporting efficient freight and inventory management.
Collaborate with headquarters on projects and continuous improvements to strengthen supply planning and operational performance.
More about you :
University degree in Logistics, Economics, Supply Chain Management, or a related field.
At least 5 years of experience in Supply Chain and/or Supply Planning, with a solid understanding of planning processes and business priorities.
Strong proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel, combined with practical experience using SAP/R3, especially the MM module.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to work confidently with data and translate insights into effective actions.
A strong sense of urgency and accountability, with the drive and adaptability to deliver against challenging targets in a dynamic environment.
Fluent English, with strong communication skills and a collaborative, team-oriented approach.
A confident and resilient mindset, able to adapt to changing priorities, take ownership and maintain focus on results.
Nice to have :
Experience with o9 Solutions or another demand planning tool
APICS certification or a comparable qualification
Previous industry experience in a relevant environment
Basic knowledge of SAP BW (Business Warehouse)
Additional language skills, particularly German
Project management experience or skills
