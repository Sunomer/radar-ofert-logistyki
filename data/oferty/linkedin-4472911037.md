For our client implementing projects in modern logistics and warehouse automation, we are looking for a Control Room Operator. You will join a team responsible for overseeing logistics processes at the client’s logistics facilities in Germany, in modern, automated distribution centers. Your task will be to monitor material flow, analyze operational events, and collaborate with technical and maintenance teams to ensure warehouse continuity and efficiency. If you have experience in a warehouse environment, know WMS systems, and communicate fluently in German, apply today!
Your tasks
Monitoring the flow of materials and logistics processes in automated warehouse systems
Operation and ongoing control of systems supporting the functioning of the distribution center
Analysis of alarms, operational incidents, and reports affecting warehouse continuity
Coordinating activities between operational teams, maintenance technicians, and WMS specialists
Escalating operational issues and supporting repair and optimization activities
Requirements
At least 3 years of experience in warehouse logistics, intralogistics, or a similar operational role
Knowledge of Warehouse Management Systems (WMS) and warehouse processes
Experience in coordinating operational activities and collaborating with technical teams
English language proficiency at a minimum B2 level
Very good command of German enabling fluent communication in the work environment
Willingness to work on-site in Germany
Nice to have
Experience working in a Control Room or Operations Center
Knowledge of WCS, MFC, or SCADA systems
Experience working in automated high-bay warehouses
Knowledge of warehouse automation and material flow
