Your role
Senior E2E Planner's role makes sure supply, capacity, and production plans come together smoothly so operations run efficiently. It focuses on spotting risks early, balancing priorities like service, cost, and inventory and finding practical solutions. It also works closely with different teams to turn plans into clear, day-to-day production actions.
Your tasks
Develops and executes integrated production, capacity, and supply plans across all horizons to ensure stable, efficient operations.
Identifies and mitigates supply risks through inventory analysis, constraint resolution, and contingency planning.
Balances demand, capacity, materials, and assets to deliver feasible MPS aligned with service, cost, and inventory targets.
Leads long-term capacity planning via RCCP, scenario modelling, and bottleneck management.
Translates weekly MPS into daily production schedules, managing material risks and execution constraints.
Drives cross-functional alignment with DRP, MRP, operations, procurement, commercial, and finance teams.
Optimizes the supply network using modelling and cost-to-serve vs service trade-off analysis.
Ensures strong planning governance through KPI tracking, constraint validation, and alignment across planning horizons.
Your profile
Minimum 4 years’ experience in supply/operations roles within FMCG or international environments.
Strong planning experience (supply, demand, distribution) and fit to competency expectations.
Fluent in English written and spoken.
Skilled in planning systems (SAP APO, Axapta, Vanguard, SAS).
Advanced MS Office; experience with SQL, data warehouses, and system implementations is a plus.
Strong ownership, responsibility, continuous improvement mindset and confident in data‑driven decision‑making.
University degree or technical background (logistics, manufacturing, systems, or business is preferred).
Readiness to work late shifts (8 hours between 8AM - 4PM and 4 PM – 12 AM), 2-3 days per week.
