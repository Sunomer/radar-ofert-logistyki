Your role
As a Senior Transformation Consultant, you will independently lead transformation workstreams and own delivery from planning to go-live, while helping shape the approach and supporting colleagues.
Your tasks
Lead workstreams independently, applying PMO practices: own planning, delivery, risks and stakeholders
Facilitate workshops that drive alignment and decisions across business, technology and procurement stakeholders, often in an international setting
Prepare steering committee materials and present to senior stakeholders
Coach less experienced team members
Lead target operating model design (processes, roles, governance) and its translation into blueprints, policies and KPIs, with a focus on procurement and, in some projects, supply chain management (SCM)
Optimise end-to-end procurement processes (e.g. source-to-contract, procure-to-pay, supplier management) to unlock efficiency, compliance and savings; in SCM projects also planning, logistics and order-to-cash
Steer ERP and procurement technology implementations (e.g. SAP Ariba, S/4HANA, Coupa; SAP IBP in SCM projects), from fit-gap to testing and cutover
Drive change management, communication and business readiness activities that deliver real user adoption
Oversee training and knowledge transfer for procurement (and, where relevant, supply chain) users
Lead cutover planning, go-live readiness and hypercare
Your profile
Have a track record of leading workstreams in transformation, change or implementation projects
Have solid experience in procurement (or alternatively SCM) and know its processes and operating models well
Can structure complex work, manage senior stakeholders and communicate clearly with business and technology teams
Work well in international settings and are fluent in English
Speak German: this is a big advantage, as many of our projects involve German-speaking clients
Nice to have:
Experience with ERP and procurement systems (e.g. SAP, Ariba, Coupa) or supply chain systems (e.g. IBP)
Experience in change management or business readiness, and in coaching colleagues
PMO or project management certification (e.g. PRINCE2, PMP, Prosci)
