Planning Service Center
We are looking for an
MRP Excellence Steward
to join our
Planning Service Center (PSC)
. The position can be based in
Warsaw, Poland, or Barcelona, Spain
.
The MRP Excellence Steward will lead and oversee Z-EUR initiatives aimed at improving and strengthening materials planning processes. The role is responsible for ensuring efficient and effective materials management while contributing to process optimization and cost reduction.
In this role, you will work closely with materials planning teams in the factories and cross-functional stakeholders to introduce best practices, improve processes and tools, and strengthen materials planning capabilities across the organization.
Key Responsibilities:
Identify and implement best practices in materials planning, including the development and refinement of processes and tools.
Establish and monitor standard key performance indicators for materials planning across the PSC, including inventory turnover, supplier performance and other relevant measures.
Drive initiatives aimed at improving the defined materials planning KPIs across the PSC whenever required.
Continuously review and optimize materials planning processes to eliminate inefficiencies, reduce manual intervention and automate tasks wherever possible.
Foster collaboration between materials planners and relevant stakeholders.
Evaluate and lead the implementation of materials planning software and tools to streamline and enhance materials planning processes.
Provide training and mentorship to the materials planning community to strengthen its skills and expertise.
Collaborate with MRP Excellence Stewards in other PSCs to ensure process standardization, share MRP best practices and build a global expert network across zones.
Cooperate closely with IT, Procurement, Regulatory Affairs and DSP functions.
Ensure that assigned projects are delivered on time, within budget and according to defined quality standards.
Define and monitor project-specific KPIs and support the training of the materials planning community in newly introduced processes and systems.
What will make you successful:
Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain, Engineering, Business, Economics or a related field.
Master’s degree in Supply Chain Management, Economics or an MBA would be an advantage.
Six Sigma certification or a comparable qualification would be an asset.
Approximately 5 years of professional experience in supply planning.
At least 3 years of experience in materials planning.
At least 2 years of experience in process optimization and project management.
Profound understanding of supply chain planning.
Strong analytical background.
Experience working with planning and ERP systems, preferably SAP and OMP.
Strong stakeholder-management skills.
Good understanding of Nestlé’s business environment.
Ability to identify common ground and develop solutions that benefit the wider organization.
Ability to act as an ambassador for change and support the implementation of new ways of working.
