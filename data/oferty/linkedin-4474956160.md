Join JJP Biologics and help bring life-changing therapies to patients with rare and ultra-rare diseases.
JJP Biologics
is a European biopharmaceutical company focused on advancing innovative biologic therapies for patients with significant unmet medical needs. As we continue to grow our clinical development portfolio, we are seeking an
Operations & Clinical Supply Chain Manager (M/F/D)
to lead critical supply chain and operational activities supporting our clinical programs.
Your responsibilities
Coordinate supply chain activities for Drug Substance (DS) and Drug Product (DP)
Manage operational collaboration with CDMOs, CROs,
Plan manufacturing slots and material availability for clinical programs
Oversee logistics, shipments, and storage of GMP materials
Work closely with the Clinical team to define and execute the clinical supply strategy, ensure timely availability of IMP and study materials, and support packaging, labeling, and distribution to clinical sites
Monitor timelines, risks, and dependencies across the supply chain
Support clinical supply planning and forecasting
Ensure proper documentation flow (CoA, release documents, shipment documentation)
Coordinate internal stakeholders across CMC, QA, RA, and Clinical teams
Our requirements
Minimum 4 years of experience in Supply Chain, Operations, or Clinical Supply within a regulated GxP environment (GMP/GCP) in pharmaceutical or biotechnology development
Good understanding of GMP requirements related to manufacturing, release, storage, and shipment of clinical materials
Experience in planning manufacturing slots and coordinating material availability for clinical programs
Knowledge of clinical supply processes including packaging, labeling, distribution, and IP management is a strong advantage
Experience in collaboration with CDMOs, CROs and logistics providers
Ability to collaborate across functional lines and work effectively in a matrix environment
Strong organizational and analytical skills, with the ability to manage multiple timelines and priorities
Proactive, hands-on approach with strong problem-solving skills
Proficiency in standard office tools (Excel, PowerPoint, Word, Outlook, Visio, etc.)
Fluent English, both written and spoken, with strong communication and presentation skills

Work on innovative biologics programs
High level of ownership and impact
International environment and collaboration with global partners
Work in a small, close-knit, experienced team
Dynamic and growing biotech organization
Attractive terms of employment: competitive medical package for the whole family, co-financing of Multisport cards and cafeteria.
