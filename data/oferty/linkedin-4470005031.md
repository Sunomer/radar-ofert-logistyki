Konecta | Supporting The Coca-Cola Company across Europe
Konecta is looking for an exceptional Digital Operations Director, Europe to lead our European Digital Operations organisation supporting The Coca-Cola Company (TCCC). Therefore, it’s important for our Digital Operations Director, Europe to be based in a major European city with a Coca-Cola office, enabling them to embed within a local office and participate in as many local routines as possible.
Leading a geographically dispersed team of 30–50+ digital professionals, you will set the direction for Digital Operations across Europe, combining operational excellence with digital transformation, people leadership and outstanding consumer experiences.
Working closely with senior Coca-Cola marketing, digital and technology leaders, you will play a key role in translating European digital strategy into effective execution across local markets.
What you'll do
- Lead the strategy, priorities and operating model for Digital Operations across a geographically dispersed team of 30-50 people in Europe.
- Lead and develop a multi-layered organisation of Area Managers and Digital Managers, a true "leader of leaders"
- Drive digital transformation, capability building and adoption of new platforms and ways of working across Europe
- Oversee the delivery and localisation of websites, apps, CRM programmes and connected consumer experiences.
- Manage performance, capacity, delivery, risks and workforce planning across markets.
- Build trusted relationships with senior Coca-Cola stakeholders and influence decisions across a complex European organisation.
- Simplify and standardise ways of working while empowering markets to respond to local needs.
- Use data and consumer insights to improve performance and digital outcomes.
- Champion collaboration, accountability, innovation and continuous improvement.
- Balancing European standards and governance with local market flexibility
Who we're looking for
You are a senior digital leader with 10+ years' experience across digital marketing, digital operations or technology-enabled consumer experiences.
You have experience leading large, international and geographically dispersed teams, including managing and developing other leaders.
You combine strong knowledge of digital platforms, CRM, websites, apps and consumer data with a proven ability to lead organisational change and digital transformation.
You are comfortable influencing senior executives, navigating complex matrix organisations and translating strategy into execution across multiple markets.
Above all, you are passionate about people, technology, marketing and creating outstanding digital consumer experiences at scale.
Ready to lead Digital Operations across one of Europe's most exciting and complex digital ecosystems? We'd love to hear from you.
Important: Konecta Team is dedicated to exclusively supporting the digital strategy of its client Coca-Cola. You will be employed by Konecta Group or our local 3rd party employment partner who will be responsible for all aspects related to the employment contract and relationship. You will be part of an amazing team tasked to drive the digital strategy and transformation for Coca-Cola, covering aspects such as digital marketing & operations, digital commerce, social listening, publishing, data & programmatic, digital experiences and innovation.
