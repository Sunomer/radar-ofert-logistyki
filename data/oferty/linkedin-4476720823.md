Purpose of the Position
We are looking for a
Trade Operations Specialist – Brokerage Management
to join our Global Trade Advisory & Solutions team and support one of our key international clients with the daily execution of its customs and brokerage management activities.
This is a hands-on operational role in a fast-paced international environment. You will work closely with customs brokers, client stakeholders and our Advisory team to monitor activities, follow up on open topics and ensure that operational tasks are completed accurately and on time.
We are looking for someone who is organized, proactive, comfortable working under pressure and eager to further develop within customs and international trade.
Main Responsibilities
Support the daily execution and monitoring of customs brokerage activities
Follow up with customs brokers and client stakeholders on open actions, operational topics and issues
Monitor broker performance, operational KPIs and customs-related data
Identify missing information, delays, discrepancies or inconsistencies and ensure appropriate follow-up
Escalate operational issues when required and support their timely resolution
Maintain operational trackers, reports and supporting documentation
Coordinate with internal and external stakeholders to ensure agreed actions are completed accurately and on time
Support the team in resolving day-to-day operational issues related to customs and brokerage activities
Ensure operational information and documentation are complete, accurate and kept up to date
Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives and optimization of brokerage management processes
What To Expect
