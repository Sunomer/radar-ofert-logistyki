We are looking for an experienced Installation Manager to work on a nationwide implementation program in the public transport sector. You will be responsible for planning and executing the installation of modern technological solutions in city buses, collaborating with technical teams and subcontractors across multiple locations. Your task will be to ensure smooth coordination of all elements of the installation process, including vehicle availability, equipment, and trained teams. The role requires readiness for frequent travel and shift work. If you are someone with project management experience and want to impact modern solutions in public transport, this offer is for you.
Your tasks
Planning and updating the installation schedule across multiple locations, including managing resources, changes, travel, and accommodation for teams
Coordinating vehicle availability with operators and quickly rescheduling work in case of changes
Managing subcontractors and field technician teams, including work assignment, performance monitoring, and corrective actions
Verifying technician training and organizing additional training in case of quality issues
Supervising the dismantling of old equipment, installation of new equipment, configuration, and commissioning
Maintaining a consistent quality control system, including inspections and preventive actions
Cost control and weekly reporting on progress, resource utilization, and risks
Collaborating with operators, depot managers, and engineering, logistics, finance, and procurement teams
Requirements
Minimum 5 years of experience in executing multi-site installation projects, field service, or equipment rollouts in the area of installation and modernization of devices
Availability for international travel (Ireland) approximately 90% of the working time
Experience in planning the work of technical teams across multiple locations simultaneously
Competence in managing subcontractors and enforcing quality and performance
Good project management skills: scheduling, resources, risks, dependencies, corrective actions
Cost awareness regarding team performance, travel costs, downtime, and corrections
Advanced knowledge of Polish and English
Residence in Poland
Nice to have
Knowledge of onboard systems: vehicle location (AVL/GPS), passenger information, ticketing systems
Knowledge of vehicle electrical installations and experience in configuring, diagnosing, or commissioning systems
Experience working with public transport operators
Ability to use project management or resource planning tools
PRINCE2, PMP, APM certification or equivalent
