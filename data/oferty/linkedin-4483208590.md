About The Job
As a Global Order Operations Analyst, you will manage the end-to-end Order to Cash process for a portfolio of Top and Strategic Accounts, driving revenue, cash flow, and a best-in-class customer experience.
You will act as a key point of contact for your customers, ensuring smooth operations and strong collaboration across internal teams and external partners
Key Responsibilities
Customer Experience & Account Management
Act as the primary point of contact for Top and Strategic Accounts in Germany
Build strong relationships with customers and internal stakeholders (Sales, Finance, Planning, Logistics)
Manage and resolve customer enquiries across orders, deliveries, returns, and disputes
Provide clear visibility on order status, delivery timelines, and issue resolution
Order & Delivery Management
Manage the full order lifecycle from placement to delivery
Ensure order accuracy, fulfillment, and on-time shipping
Coordinate with logistics, sales, and planning to resolve supply issues
Ensure compliance with customer-specific requirements (e.g., VAS, EDI)
Accounts Receivable & Credit Control
Monitor receivables, collections, and overdue balances
Minimize bad debt risk and support dispute and claim resolution
Investigate billing issues, chargebacks, and discrepancies
Support credit management and adherence to credit limits
Reporting & Analysis
Analyze order book performance, risks, and opportunities
Provide insights to support forecasting and decision-making
Track key KPIs (e.g., delivery performance, DSO, aged debt)
Process & Compliance
Ensure adherence to SOPs, SOX controls, and company policies
Collaborate with external partners to meet service levels
Maintain accurate and up-to-date customer and order data
Cross-functional Collaboration
Work closely with Sales, Finance, Distribution, and external partners
Participate in customer meetings to improve performance and processes
Support system enhancements and continuous improvement initiatives
About You
Fluent in German and English - written and spoken.
Experience in Customer Service, Order Management, or Accounts Receivable in a B2B environment.
Strong knowledge of SAP or similar ERP systems and advanced Excel skills (Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP, IF..).
Highly analytical with the ability to interpret data, identify trends, and translate insights into actions.
Strong problem-solving skills, with the ability to proactively identify root causes, manage complex situations, and implement effective, sustainable solutions.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a collaborative and customer-focused mindset.
Highly accountable, with a proactive and ownership mindset.
Knowledge of EDI processes, logistics flows, and credit/AR management is advantageous.
