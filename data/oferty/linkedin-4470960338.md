Job Purpose:
DHL is looking for an Expert – Center of Excellence “COE” Transportation to join our Procurement Center of Excellence team based in ESSA.
This role sits within DPDHL Corporate Procurement and is a key part in supporting our ESSA Transportation procurement team to deliver a best-in-class sourcing approach to all DHL Group Business Units via management of procurement events in our e-sourcing tools as well as contributing to the management of SRM processes and data within our Salesforce e-tool.
Reporting into the Head of Transport Sourcing CoE for EMEA, this is an interface role for managing both e-sourcing events within a 3PL & 4PL environment alongside supporting the ESSA Transportation Procurement Leadership Team with key Supplier Relationship Management activities.
We are looking for a candidate to join the team in one of the following locations:
Warsaw (Poland), Valencia (Spain), Prague/Ostrava (Czech Republic), or Budapest (Hungary)
.
Your tasks:
Working collaboratively with colleagues in Procurement and the Business Units setting up e-sourcing events and analyzing outcomes of these events and optimizing sourcing processes towards digital and automated solutions.
Data analysis, preparation and interpretation of complex data, comparative bid analysis.
Support colleagues within the ESSA team to translate customer sourcing strategies into effective e-sourcing solutions, participating in both internal/external customer meetings and supplier negotiations as required.
Maintaining tool Data quality and Supporting colleagues within the ESSA team to identify and resolve/escalate any technical issues on our supplier relationship management “SRM” e-tool.
Continuous review and monitoring of our supplier relationship management processes to identify improvements and efficiencies for the ESSA team.
Be an interactive and participative team member within the ESSA Transport Procurement community.
Your profile:
Typically more than 2 years’ experience within a Procurement, Logistics Operations or Data analyst role.
Fluent in English (Written/Verbal), other European languages desirable but not essential.
Knowledge of E-sourcing tools (Jaggaer ASO/Bravo/Salesforce) desirable, but not essential. (knowledge of any other eSourcing tools are also an asset)
Very good knowledge of Microsoft Office tools particularly Excel advance functions. good knowledge of data visualization tools such as Power BI is desirable.
Ability to manage complex projects under time pressure.
Process minded, but comfortable working in an agile environment with an ability to adjust to changing requirements and timelines.
High degree of customer orientation, bringing a logical and analytical approach to complex matters.
Ability to work both independently and within a team.
Experience and understanding of the logistics sector (preferably Road, Air and Ocean transportation) within and outside of DHL Group. All modes desirable but not essential.
Procurement background with specialization in Transportation and Logistics services desirable.
Undergraduate degree with logistics, supply chain, business administration, Statistics / Data Analyst , engineering desirable, but not essential.
Data Analytics specific qualification e.g. CAP,IABAC, CCA or Category specific qualification e.g. Supply Chain related desirable, but not essential.
