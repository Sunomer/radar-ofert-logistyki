You support the development and management of a company-wide Quality Management System focused on standardization and assurance, including auditing activities to ensure compliance with requirements. Defines, governs and maintains a limited set of Global Critical Standards. Uses strong background in manufacturing, lab, supply chain and quality to evaluate conformance and adequacy of standards, aligning with customer expectations. Provides recommendations to regional senior management regarding conformance and data-based recommendations for continuous improvement.
You need to provide leadership in the areas of Quality Management, to the Regional Business groups and Manufacturing Quality Teams. The key aspects of this role are Customer Satisfaction and Quality Complaint Prevention. The role monitors, manages and advises on how the quality system and internal stakeholders are performing, utilizing statistics of actual performance against set targeted measures.
This role will be based on hybrid work model and located in one of our entities in UK, Spain or Poland.
A Day in the Life
Provide leadership in the area of Quality Management to the Regional Business Groups and Manufacturing Quality Teams including QMS Audits and Certifications
Process Owner Quality Management System (QMS), Quality Critical Stanbdards and QMS Audits
Responsible for the Quality Policy, Quality Manuals, Risks and Opportunities, and Scope of the Quality Management System
Analyze metrics and trends to have a deep understanding of opportunities
Promote GoalZERO for Quality Culture, continual growth and development with emphasis on prevention, standard work, and risk‑based thinking.
Report to Management on the performance of the QMS and need for improvements or resources, while accounting for risks
Work with process owners to develop, communicate, and maintain RACI analyses of QMS Processes incl. MOC, clearly distinguishing global standard ownership from regional and site execution responsibilities.
