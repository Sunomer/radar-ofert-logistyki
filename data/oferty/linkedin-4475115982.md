Join our Automotive team and shape what’s next for leading OEMs and mobility players across Europe. You’ll be part of a multilingual, European network that collaborates across disciplines to deliver end‑to‑end change for our clients.
ABOUT TEAM AND WORK:
You’ll be at the heart of automotive reinvention—driving new sales models, electrification, connected services and data‑driven operations that redefine how customers discover, buy, use and care for vehicles—and how OEMs operate in a fast‑changing market.
Impact areas we are driving
Enterprise & customer experience transformations across sales, CRM/CX, digital marketing, and after‑sales.
Retailer & network excellence: dealer operations, coaching and training, performance management.
Next‑gen sales models: agency/direct (D2C) transformation and B2B sales (including leasing partnerships and financial services).
Electromobility & mobility services: strategy, product management (SDV, connected solutions, mobility), and fleet operations.
Data‑driven enterprise: turning insights into action across the value chain with state-of-the-art delivery methods and technologies (such as Agentic AI).
We are applying proven program and project methodologies and state‑of‑the‑art technologies to deliver measurable, end‑to‑end outcomes.
THE WORK:
Co‑create strategies and define operating models that modernize sales, after-sales and customer experiences, as well as orchestrate complex change at Automotive OEMs.
Set up, pilot, and scale new programs—from agency sales to e‑mobility offerings—using agile delivery and best‑practice frameworks.
Partner with cross‑functional Accenture teams (e.g., Applied Intelligence, Technology Strategy & Advisory) to bring integrated business + technology solutions to life
