About The Job
As Product Analyst for Total Rewards, you are the technical and operational guarantor of campaign delivery. You secure end-to-end campaign readiness and execution by coordinating configuration activities, performing or validating selected system and data administration interventions, overseeing testing and release preparation, and ensuring that risks, dependencies and issues are actively managed. You also coordinate consultants and Chapter members, animate Agile delivery rituals and maintain the backlog management tool as the reliable source of delivery status.
You work closely with the Product Owner and Senior Product Analyst to turn prioritized and technically ready stories into controlled campaign delivery. Depending on your experience and demonstrated capability, you may take increasing ownership of selected configuration and data administration activities within agreed technical guardrails. You ensure that campaign tasks, owners, timelines, blockers, testing evidence and documentation are actively managed, and you escalate higher-risk changes or arbitration needs when required.
Key Responsibilities
Delivery Coordination and Rewards Campaign Execution
Coordinate the operational delivery of Rewards initiatives, enhancements and campaign-related activities
Manage day-to-day follow-up of configuration work performed by consultants and Chapter members
Monitor workload, timeline, progress, risks, blockers, dependencies and quality of deliverables
Ensure that campaign preparation, configuration, testing, release and post-go-live activities are properly followed up
Escalate risks, delays or arbitration needs to the Senior Product Analyst or Product Owner when required
Agile Rituals and Backlog Tool Management
Own the preparation, animation and follow-up of the team Daily
Maintain the backlog management tool, ensuring that stories, tasks, owners, statuses, priorities, dependencies and blockers are up to date
Ensure that the Kanban board or backlog management tool is used as the team’s single source of truth for delivery follow-up
Facilitate operational Agile rituals such as Daily meetings, delivery follow-ups, retrospectives, sprint reviews and preparation sessions
Produce delivery visibility through progress updates, dashboards, action lists and risk trackers
Configuration Follow-up and Consultant Coordination
Coordinate the pool of external consultants and Chapter members involved in SAP SuccessFactors configuration activities
Track configuration progress and confirm alignment with validated requirements and technical guidance provided by the
Senior Product Analyst
Review and validate selected system configurations against approved requirements and technical guidance, and perform limited hands-on technical interventions in SAP SuccessFactors when required, within the agreed scope and under the guidance of the Senior Product Analyst
Support issue tracking, defect resolution and root-cause analysis by gathering facts, evidence and context
Ensure configuration outputs are documented and ready for testing, release and future maintenance
Testing, Documentation and Release Support
Prepare, coordinate and execute functional test scenarios where relevant
Support User Acceptance Testing by ensuring clear test scope, test evidence, defect follow-up and stakeholder communication
Maintain requirements traceability, test evidence, release notes and operational documentation
Contribute to demos and delivery sessions for new features, enhancements and campaign readiness
Operational Communication
Ensure smooth operational communication between consultants, Chapter members, the Product Owner, the Senior
Product Analyst and HR stakeholders
Prepare and present progress reports, delivery dashboards and operational follow-up materials
Help maintain a structured and transparent delivery cadence across the Rewards product team
About You
We expect you to have
Minimum 2 to 3 years of experience in HRIS, HR Technology, Rewards, compensation processes or a Product Analyst role
Experience coordinating consultants, technical contributors or configuration activities
Hands-on experience reviewing or maintaining SAP SuccessFactors system configurations and performing HRIS data administration activities, including data validation, uploads, corrections and quality controls
Experience supporting HR campaigns, recurring Rewards cycles or SAP SuccessFactors delivery is a strong asset
Skills
Good working knowledge of SAP SuccessFactors Rewards configuration, including the ability to review selected settings, understand configuration logic and perform limited system interventions within an approved scope
Knowledge of HRIS data administration, including data preparation, validation, uploads, corrections, access-sensitive handling and data quality controls
Strong coordination, follow-up, documentation and delivery tracking skills
Experience with Agile rituals, Daily meetings, Kanban boards and backlog management tools
Ability to work with consultants, technical teams and business stakeholders in a structured and reliable way
Attention to detail, operational rigor and problem-solving mindset
