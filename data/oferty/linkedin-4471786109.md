Dover Imaging & Identification (DII) is a segment of Dover Corporation that includes the following brands: Markem-Imaje, Blue Bite, Solaris Laser, Systech, MS Printing Solutions and JK Group. We design, manufacture and deliver industrial equipment, consumables, software and services solutions for the marking & coding, consumer engagement, product traceability, authentication, and digital textile printing markets.
With more than 40 offices and over 3,000 employees worldwide, DII has a presence in +28 countries and offers numerous job opportunities globally. If you're looking for an international, multidisciplinary and collaborative environment that offers a world of opportunities, come join us at DII.
The Role
As a
Process Engineer
, you will take part in the deployment of site manufacturing processes and the industrialization of new products.
What You Will Do
Create and implement Manufacturing instructions, manufacturing BOM, tooling, Poke Yoke, equipment's for existing product
Learn and develop PE marketing technologies knowledge & competencies through strong connection with international PE team
Lead and/or contribute to a variety of manufacturing projects, ensuring that the processes are robust, efficient and properly documented. Thes could be for new product introduction (NPI) or engineering changes of our current products.
Perform summary of the status and achievements in relation to the project and regularly report to the high level of management team
Define production process for new products in relation to R&D requirements and critical parameters to ensure robustness and repeatability of the production
Define and deploy SPC in relation to product and process requirements
Work closely with our contract manufacturers (Globally) to support the operational activity & optimize the current manufacturing infrastructure
Work in a global, cross-functional environment across multiple disciplines for effective execution
Support the new product introduction in production: labor, quality, capacity and schedule on track
Perform PFMEA's and implement Control Plan for product and production line
Perform root cause analysis and implement corrective actions
Support Manufacturing, Field Service and Repair Centers
As part of the Global organization, you will support other sites adhoc.
What You Need To Have
Degree in engineering with a minimum of 5 years of experience in process engineering in technical industry
Proven experience in successful development of manufacturing processes
Project management skills
Proven operational knowledge of mechanics, pneumatics and electronics
Good knowledge of methodologies: Six Sigma, lean, DFM/DFA, PFMEA, SPC, DOE etc
Good knowledge of CAD (Inventor& Creo)
Knowledge of WindChill, JIRA, SAP would be an additional advantage
Fluent in English (at least B2 level) and Polish
Availability to travel internationally: 5%
