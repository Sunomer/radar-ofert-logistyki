Role and Summary
Specialist, Program Management, Customer Engagement
The Consumer Acquisition & Engagement team is looking for a client-focused Product Management Specialist to manage customer-engagement and card-portfolio initiatives across multiple markets.
The role combines client relationship management, product and customer-engagement expertise, and hands-on delivery. The successful candidate will work with clients to understand their card-acquisition, engagement, usage and transaction objectives, translate those needs into relevant campaigns or solutions, and coordinate implementation with internal teams, vendors and external partners.
This is a hands-on role covering the full initiative lifecycle, from understanding the initial client need and developing the approach through implementation, reporting, financial settlement and follow-up. The successful candidate will be comfortable managing client relationships while remaining closely involved in operational and financial detail.
The role offers international exposure, visible ownership of client outcomes and the opportunity to contribute ideas that improve customer journeys, product performance and cardholder engagement. This reflects the multi-market, client-facing and delivery-focused scope confirmed during the intake.
Role Responsibilities
Own day-to-day relationships with assigned clients and develop a strong understanding of their customer-engagement and card-portfolio objectives.
Translate client needs and customer insights into campaigns, product enhancements or practical engagement solutions.
Manage customer-acquisition, engagement, loyalty and promotional initiatives from initial requirements through implementation, monitoring, reporting and closure.
Support clients in improving card acquisition, customer engagement, card usage and transaction activity.
Use customer, campaign and portfolio information to identify opportunities and develop practical recommendations.
Contribute to the development and enhancement of customer-facing products, propositions and experiences.
Coordinate timelines, milestones, dependencies, risks and agreed actions across several parallel initiatives.
Prepare reports, presentations and supporting documentation for clients and internal stakeholders.
Coordinate agreements, campaign funding, budgets, costs and financial settlement with Finance, suppliers and relevant partners.
Maintain accurate and timely campaign, financial and reporting documentation.
Partner with Marketing, Advisors, Product, Managed Services, Finance and other teams to deliver coordinated client solutions.
Manage relationships with external vendors and partners involved in product, programme and campaign delivery.
Identify opportunities to improve customer journeys, campaign performance and existing delivery approaches.
Proactively address obstacles, resolve delivery issues and maintain clear communication between clients and internal teams.
Take personal accountability for assigned clients, initiatives and agreed outcomes.
