The role of Specialist, Lean,QMS (Continuous Improvement) is to act as a business partner who works closely with middle management and the teams, understand business priorities, proposes and implements improvement projects/initiatives which support successful achievement of DSV GBS strategic goals and objectives.
Your responsibilities:
Support to build and strengthen continuous improvement culture within GBS
Cooperation with GBS units and teams and other process stakeholders,
Track and report continuous improvement project and initiatives
Assist process transition to operations and participate in SLA review
Support the establishment and delivery of an operational excellence/ standard work program for GBS
Designing, preparing and conducting continuous improvement methodologies training sessions
Support in defining new KPIs for services provided by GBS and support in their implementation
Develop, implement and maintain reporting framework for business KPI metrics
Implementation and maintenance of the process maturity model of the organization (Maturity Matrix Model)
Support Operations in the creation, development/update of process documentations outside of process migration scope, and conduct documentation audits based on GBS standards
Our requirements:
Experience in working in an international environment supported by a minimum of 2 years of professional practice
Experience in describing business process
High proficiency in communicating in English (at least B2)
Completed training from Lean: Yellow/Grean Belt
Good organization skills
Office presence at least 3 times per week
