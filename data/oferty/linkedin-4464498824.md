ECCO’s vision is to be the leading premium brand for shoes and leather goods. In parallel, the company continues to strengthen its Global Business Services (GBS) organization to support smooth, reliable and well‑organized finance operations.
Since 2022, ECCO has been consolidating the majority of its transactional finance and accounting processes within its GBS structure. Today, Warsaw serves as a key hub delivering finance services in a global setup, bringing together experienced accountants and finance specialists who support ECCO entities around the world.
By joining ECCO GBS in Warsaw, you become part of a mature, international organization focused on continuous improvement, process excellence and the use of digital transformation tools to support the business in a fast‑changing environment.
Role Purpose
Lead the global end-to-end process areas, driving process standardisation, governance and continuous improvement across the Global Business Services (GBS) organisation.
Ensure alignment between GBS and the broader Finance functions – including Business Finance / FP&A, Group Controlling & Reporting, Group Tax, and Group Treasury – to enable an integrated finance operating model, support ERP-enabled transformation (S/4HANA), and deliver operational excellence through harmonised global processes..
Responsibilities
Own the global process and governance by defining the global process framework, standards, policies, process taxonomy, KPIs, and service catalogue, while ensuring alignment with adjacent Finance processes and business requirements.
Take part in finance transformation initiatives in GBS, including S/4HANA implementation, GBS expansion, operating model design, and process harmonisation, working closely with Finance, IT, Business stakeholders, and Global Process Owners across other process domains to deliver scalable and digital-first solutions.
Drive continuous improvement and process excellence through process modelling (BPMN), process governance, automation opportunities, performance management, and data-driven decision making, embedding best practices and Lean continuous improvement principles across the global organisation.
Requirements
8–12+ years' experience in Finance, Shared Services or GBS, with significant expertise in Record to Report and strong understanding of adjacent Finance functions (FP&A, Group Controlling & Reporting, Tax, Treasury) and end-to-end finance processes.
Demonstrated experience leading global transformation programmes, including ERP implementations (preferably SAP S/4HANA), GBS operating model design or roll-outs, process governance, and stakeholder management within complex multinational organisations.
Strong process management capability, including BPMN/process modelling, process taxonomy, KPI design, governance frameworks, and continuous improvement methodologies; Lean Six Sigma certification or equivalent experience is considered an advantage
