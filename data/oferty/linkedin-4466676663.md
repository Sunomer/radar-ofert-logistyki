Description & Requirements
Who we are looking for
We are a technology consulting firm building and operating next-generation AI supercompute infrastructure for the world’s most ambitious organizations. As a Technical Project Coordinator you will support the delivery of complete AI supercomputing solutions. You will work with Deloitte teams and clients to understand their AI workloads.
At least 2–4 years of hands-on experience in IT projects (project management, infrastructure, systems, platform, networking, AI solutions).
Ability to work closely with specialists in adjacent domains, ask the right questions, understand cross-stack dependencies, challenge assumptions constructively, and turn multiple technical inputs into a coherent end-to-end solution.
Experience in project management or business support (projects related to designing, deploying, operating, or supporting data-centre environments, AI transformations).
Understanding of server architecture, agentic AI, go to market strategies.
Experience working with technology vendors, delivery partners, customers, or cross-functional engineering and business teams.
Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to explain technical trade-offs to engineering teams, vendors, customers, and senior decision-makers.
Fluent English and Polish is a must
Your future role
Coordinating work on client proposals and project delivery in accordance with Deloitte policy.
Participating in meetings with technology partners and assisting in the selection of technologies for projects.
You will collaborate directly with leading technology vendors, data-centre operators, platform teams, and customers while working on GPU clusters that demand precision across physical infrastructure, high-performance networking, storage, orchestration, and operations.
Backed by our standing as NVIDIA’s leading EMEA consulting partner, you will gain exposure to advanced AI infrastructure designs, specialist communities.
This is a role for executing predefined projects in the area of agentic AI in Healthcare and widely in AI factories projects.
What we offer
You will work with technology—and at a scale. Work within an international organization serving the Polish market and the broader Central European region. A clear career path enabling personal growth and the achievement of professional goals, access to cutting-edge AI technologies, and the opportunity to scale them for major organizations.
